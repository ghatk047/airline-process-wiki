Slide 3 of 3 — KPIs & Risk Register
StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Screen potential partners against loyalty fit criteria Minimum 20 candidates screened per category (hotel/car/retail) per quarter Retail and hotel categories have inconsistent earn-per-dollar standards across IATA-affiliated programmes; benchmarking requires manual OAG and ATPCO data pulls with no automated feed
1.2 Score partner on member overlap and revenue potential Projected incremental miles-earned revenue ≥ $500K annually per partner Member spend data outside airline transactions is incomplete; reliance on third-party data append (e.g. Acxiom) adds latency of 4–6 weeks and increases data privacy compliance overhead under GDPR and CCPA
1.3 Approve partner for commercial engagement Decision turnaround ≤ 10 business days from submission Approval workflows require sign-off from Legal, Finance, and Brand teams simultaneously; lack of a unified workflow tool causes email-chain approvals that are difficult to audit ⚠
2.1 Define earn/burn mile rates per partner category Earn rate set within ±10% of top-3 competitor benchmark to maintain programme competitiveness Earn rate proposals must account for currency-of-sale variations across hotel booking platforms; multi-currency mile valuation is not natively handled in SAP FI without custom enhancement
2.2 Model financial liability and breakage assumptions Projected breakage rate ≥ 20% to ensure programme profitability; liability exposure within approved board ceiling IFRS 15 requires deferred revenue recognition for miles issued; SAP FI configuration for loyalty liability is complex and frequently requires external audit review, delaying deal closure by 2–4 weeks ⚠
2.3 Negotiate SLA terms, data-sharing clauses, and audit rights Contract execution within 60 days of first commercial engagement; SLA uptime commitment ≥ 99.5% for transaction API Hotel and car rental partners often resist audit rights on transaction data, creating reconciliation disputes; without real-time feeds, monthly batch reconciliations result in miles posting delays of up to 72 hours, breaching member-facing SLA
2.4 Execute legally signed partnership agreement 100% of executed agreements stored in CRM document vault within 24 hours of signature Wet-signature requirements from some international hotel chains (particularly APAC and MEA regions) extend execution timelines; e-signature platforms (DocuSign) not universally accepted by all partners
3.1 Configure partner profile in loyalty management system Partner configuration completed within 5 business days of contract execution Comarch Loyalty rule engine requires structured XML earn-rule definitions; hotel chains with tiered earn rates (e.g. brand-tier multipliers) require multiple rule variants, increasing configuration time and regression test scope
3.2 Build and certify partner transaction API connector API throughput ≥ 500 transactions per second; p99 latency ≤ 2 seconds Car rental partners (e.g. Hertz, Enterprise) still expose legacy SOAP APIs; bridging SOAP-to-REST within AWS API Gateway adds development cycles and creates brittle transformation layers that break on partner schema updates
3.3 Execute UAT — earn/burn transaction end-to-end test Transaction error rate < 0.5%; all earn/burn scenarios in test matrix pass before production release Member account linking between Amadeus Altéa PSS and partner booking engines relies on loyalty number matching; mismatched account formats (numeric vs alphanumeric) cause silent earn failures that are only detected in post-flight reconciliation ⚠
3.4 Enable partner earn/burn in production environment Zero P1 incidents in first 72 hours post-launch; real-time alert threshold set at error rate > 1% Production rollbacks are disruptive if a partner API sends unexpected payloads post-launch; Comarch Loyalty does not support canary releases natively, requiring full cutover with no gradual traffic ramp
4.1 Publish partner to member-facing loyalty portal and app Partner page live within 3 business days of production enablement; page load time ≤ 2 seconds Loyalty portal content management is tightly coupled to Altéa PSS member-facing APIs; content updates require IT release cycle (bi-weekly) rather than CMS self-service, slowing time-to-market for partner promotions
4.2 Launch targeted co-marketing campaign to member segments Email open rate ≥ 22%; click-through rate (CTR) ≥ 2%; campaign ROI ≥ 3:1 on miles-issued cost Member communication frequency caps (set in Salesforce Marketing Cloud) limit partnership launch campaigns to one email per member per week; competing loyalty promotions from the same period suppress campaign visibility ⚠
5.1 Monitor partner transaction volumes and error rates weekly Transaction volume within ±15% of forecast; earn posting success rate ≥ 99.5% Hotel partners send batch transaction files (nightly SFTP) rather than real-time API calls; delayed posting means members do not see miles for 24–72 hours, generating inbound contact centre volume and NPS drag
5.2 Audit points liability exposure and breakage monthly Liability reconciliation completed by business day 5 of each month; breakage rate maintained ≥ 18% IFRS 15 standalone selling price (SSP) for miles must be updated quarterly; SAP CO-PA margin reporting does not natively integrate with Comarch Loyalty ledger, requiring manual extract-transform-load (ETL) via AWS Redshift ⚠
5.3 Trigger partner performance improvement plan if KPIs missed PIP issued within 10 business days of confirmed SLA breach; partner response received within 5 business days SLA penalty clauses are often commercially difficult to enforce with major hotel chains (Marriott, Hilton) due to contractual carve-outs; legal escalation stalls programme improvements and damages long-term relationship
6.1 Conduct annual partner business review (ABR) ABR completed ≥ 90 days before contract expiry; partner NPS score ≥ +30 for renewal consideration Attribution of loyalty revenue uplift to specific partners is difficult; multi-partner journeys (hotel + car + flight) where miles are earned across a trip create ambiguous incrementality data that weakens negotiation positions ⚠
6.2 Execute contract renewal, renegotiation, or off-boarding Contract renewal signed ≥ 30 days before expiry; off-boarding completed within 45 days with zero orphaned earn transactions Partner off-boarding requires coordinated deactivation across Comarch Loyalty, AWS API Gateway, Salesforce Marketing Cloud suppression lists, and Altéa PSS member portal; no single orchestration tool manages the full off-boarding workflow, creating risk of earn transactions processed after contract end ⚠
