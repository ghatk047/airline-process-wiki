Slide 3 of 3 — KPIs & Risk Register
StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Extract booked passenger manifest from PSS Manifest extraction latency ≤15 min from trigger; data completeness ≥99.5% Altéa API rate limits during peak booking windows can delay manifest pull by 30–45 min, pushing communications outside the optimal 72h window
1.2 Enrich profiles with loyalty tier & preferences Profile match rate ≥95% against CRM records; enrichment processing ≤10 min per 10,000 records Non-member or pseudonymous bookings via OTA channels lack Rapid Rewards linkage, resulting in generic (non-personalised) communication for ~18% of bookings
1.3 Validate communication consent & channel eligibility Opt-out suppression accuracy 100%; consent validation completed ≤5 min before send queue Consent records stored in multiple systems (PSS, CRM, mobile app) can be out of sync; GDPR Article 7 requires unambiguous consent — mismatch risk if unified consent hub is absent ⚠
2.1 Build personalised comms cadence schedule Schedule build time ≤20 min for full departure manifest; ≥98% of schedules created before T-72h window Multi-leg itineraries with short connections require independent cadence per leg; logic errors in connection-aware scheduling have caused duplicate or mis-timed notifications
2.2 Generate dynamic itinerary & ancillary offer content Content personalisation token fill rate ≥99%; render error rate ≤0.1% across email clients Gate and terminal data is often unavailable at T-72h for airports operating under dynamic gate assignment (e.g., Midway MDW), forcing placeholder text that reduces perceived communication quality
2.3 Validate dynamic content against live flight data Data staleness rate ≤0.5% at send time; validation cycle ≤8 min Schedule changes filed in Altéa after content generation but before send can produce communications with incorrect departure times — requires a final data refresh check within 30 min of send
2.4 Run GDPR / CAN-SPAM compliance check on content 100% of sends include mandatory unsubscribe link; compliance check completion ≤5 min Transactional vs marketing classification is disputed for upsell content (e.g., ancillary offers embedded in itinerary emails); misclassification exposes airline to CAN-SPAM violation — legal review required per campaign type ⚠
3.1 Send 72h check-in invitation with OLCI link Email open rate ≥45%; SMS delivery rate ≥98%; OLCI click-through rate ≥35% Online check-in opens at T-24h for some fare classes (e.g., Basic Economy restrictions); sending OLCI link at T-72h for ineligible passengers creates customer confusion and misdirected contact centre calls
3.2 Deliver ancillary upsell offers — seat, baggage, lounge Ancillary revenue per communication ≥$4.50; offer acceptance rate ≥8%; unsubscribe rate ≤0.2% ATPCO ancillary pricing updates can lag real-time seat inventory, resulting in offers quoting prices that have since changed — requires live price validation at click-through, not at send time
3.3 Send travel readiness advisory — docs, visa, weather Advisory delivery ≥ T-48h before departure; advisory relevance score ≥4.2/5 in post-trip survey International routes to Mexico and Caribbean require ESTA / visa data that is sourced from IATA Travel Centre; data freshness is ±24h — incorrect requirements issued to passengers have led to denied-boarding incidents and liability claims
3.4 Confirm check-in status and deliver boarding pass Mobile boarding pass adoption rate ≥72%; boarding pass delivery latency ≤30 sec post check-in PKPass wallet integration with Altéa DCS has known compatibility gaps for codeshare itineraries — passengers on partner-operated segments occasionally receive blank boarding passes, requiring airport reprint ⚠
4.1 Monitor real-time flight status for schedule changes Flight status event latency ≤2 min from OPS system to comms trigger; monitoring uptime ≥99.9% SITA AMS and Altéa PSS publish flight status updates independently; reconciliation lag between the two systems (up to 8 min) can result in duplicate or conflicting passenger notifications
4.2 Detect IROPS threshold breach and raise alert IROPS detection-to-trigger latency ≤3 min; false positive alert rate ≤1% Threshold parameters are configured statically — a 25-min delay that extends to 90 min in increments may not trigger the 30-min rule until too late, leaving passengers uninformed during the critical re-booking window ⚠
4.3 Send gate change, delay or cancellation notification First notification delivered ≤5 min of IROPS trigger; SMS delivery rate ≥99%; passenger awareness rate ≥90% (tracked via boarding rate post-alert) Airport-side gate change displays (FIDS) and passenger SMS notifications are not synchronised — passengers often receive gate-change SMS after the FIDS has already shown the new gate, eroding trust in the notification system
4.4 Generate and deliver rebooking options for cancelled flights Rebooking offer delivered ≤10 min of cancellation confirmation; self-service rebooking rate ≥55%; contact centre deflection rate ≥40% Altéa availability query during IROPS events is throttled due to simultaneous mass rebooking — alternative availability returned to passengers can be stale within 90 sec, leading to 'flight no longer available' errors at checkout and escalated call volumes ⚠
