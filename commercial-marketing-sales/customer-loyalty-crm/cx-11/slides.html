1.1 Monitor operational alerts for IRROP triggers IRROP detection-to-alert time ≤15 min from event confirmation SITA AMS integration with Altéa PSS can lag 5–10 min during high-volume events, widening the window before rebooking can begin and increasing downstream queue pressure
1.2 Classify disruption type, cause, and regulatory scope Cause code classification accuracy ≥95%; regulatory flag set within 5 min of classification Weather vs. technical cause code misclassification directly affects DOT compensation liability exposure; Altéa delay code mapping requires manual override when ops and commercial teams disagree
1.3 Activate IRROP protocol and notify stakeholders All operational stakeholders notified within 10 min of IRROP classification; case opened with correct priority tier in 100% of activations Parallel activation across Altéa, Salesforce, and the crew management system has no single handshake confirmation; coordination gaps emerge during simultaneous multi-flight IRROPs ⚠
2.1 Extract affected passenger manifest and PNR records Manifest extraction completed within 5 min of IRROP activation; contact data completeness ≥92% of affected PAX Passengers booked through OTAs and GDS channels frequently have incomplete or agency-held contact fields in the Altéa PNR; direct SMS or email outreach fails for approximately 8% of affected passengers
2.2 Segment passengers by recovery priority tier 100% of premium-tier and connection-at-risk passengers classified as P1 before notification dispatch Altéa segmentation logic does not automatically identify passengers with connection windows under 60 min on the same PNR; analysts must manually review itineraries, adding 10–15 min per large event
2.3 Send proactive disruption notification with self-serve link ≥90% of affected PAX notified within 30 min of IRROP activation; self-serve rebooking adoption ≥35% of notified passengers Push notification delivery drops to approximately 72% for passengers without the airline app installed; passengers booked via travel agencies require manual outreach via GDS telex, which averages 20 min per agency ⚠
3.1 Run automated rebooking engine against open inventory Auto-rebook match rate ≥70% of affected passengers on same-day service; engine execution time ≤3 min per 200-PAX event Inventory constraints on popular routes during a large IRROP cause cascading rebooking failures; Altéa auto-rebook does not dynamically reprice across fare classes without a manual IT change request, locking lower-fare passengers out of available premium inventory
3.2 Validate alternative itinerary against re-protection policy Re-protection policy compliance ≥98% of issued auto-rebooks; policy exception review completed within 5 min Re-protection thresholds are hardcoded configuration parameters in Altéa requiring an IT change request to update; during major weather events affecting 50+ flights simultaneously the static thresholds cannot flex to match available capacity ⚠
3.3 Issue confirmed rebooking and send updated itinerary Itinerary confirmation delivered within 5 min of rebooking commit; boarding pass pre-issuance success rate ≥95% DCS boarding pass pre-issuance fails for departures more than 24 hrs out or where gate assignment is pending at origin; passengers receive confirmation but must re-check in at the airport, creating service desk queue spikes
4.1 Assess passenger welfare entitlement under applicable regulations Entitlement determination completed within 15 min of IRROP activation; regulatory accuracy ≥99% validated by Customer Relations Dual-regulation scenarios on transatlantic routes require manual legal review; Salesforce workflow rules do not auto-resolve cross-jurisdictional entitlement conflicts, exposing the airline to under-compensation claims and EU regulatory fines ⚠
4.2 Issue hotel accommodation vouchers for stranded passengers Hotel voucher issuance within 45 min of overnight determination; room availability confirmed for ≥90% of entitled passengers Hotel partner API connectivity is inconsistent at smaller outstations; manual phone booking is required at approximately 30% of network stations, extending wait times to 2+ hrs and exhausting customer service staff capacity during simultaneous multi-station IRROPs
4.3 Distribute meal and ground transportation vouchers ≥85% of vouchers issued digitally (app or email); airport service desk queue time ≤20 min during events under 300 PAX Digital voucher adoption varies significantly by passenger demographic; elderly and tech-averse passengers queue at service desks, creating bottlenecks during large IRROPs that overwhelm deployed airport staff ratios
5.1 Route unresolved cases to contact centre agent queue P1 passengers reached by agent within 5 min; P2 within 15 min; P3 within 30 min of queue entry Contact centre surge during large IRROP events pushes wait times to 60–90 min for P3 passengers; AWS Connect IVR overflow routing is not dynamically linked to real-time IRROP case volume for on-demand agent staffing scale-up
5.2 Present interline and OAL rebooking options to passenger Interline option presented within 10 min of agent engagement; OAL rebook success rate ≥60% when invoked Interline agreements and bilateral e-ticketing eligibility must be manually verified in Sabre before issuance; Altéa has no automated interline rebook capability for many partner carriers, requiring agents to switch between Sabre and Altéa and manually reissue tickets
5.3 Confirm passenger decision on rebooking or refund Agent average handling time ≤12 min per case; passenger acceptance rate of presented alternatives ≥75% Refund processing for NDC-booked tickets falls outside the standard Altéa refund workflow; agents must manually process credits across 2–3 systems, extending handling time by 8–12 min per NDC case and increasing error rates ⚠
6.1 Calculate entitlement and initiate compensation payment Refund processing completed within 7 business days per DOT requirement; compensation acknowledgement letter issued within 24 hrs of claim receipt Refunds for GDS and OTA-booked tickets must route through the original selling channel; the airline has no direct refund path to the customer, requiring agency debit memos or manual credit card processing that can extend settlement to 15–30 business days
6.2 Log IRROP event data and capture NPS impact Post-IRROP NPS survey response rate ≥25%; recovery satisfaction score ≥3.5 out of 5.0; event record published within 48 hrs of case closure NPS survey responses from post-IRROP emails are not automatically linked to specific IRROP event records in Salesforce; manual tagging is required, delaying analytics availability by 48–72 hrs and reducing the accuracy of root cause attribution
