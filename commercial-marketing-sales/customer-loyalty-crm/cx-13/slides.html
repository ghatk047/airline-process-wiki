StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Evaluate co-brand bank partner candidates Partner evaluation cycle ≤60 days; minimum 3 candidates scored Airline loyalty platforms (e.g., Comarch Loyalty) lack native RFP workflow tooling — scoring is manual in spreadsheets, creating audit gaps for legal review ⚠
1.2 Negotiate revenue-share and miles pricing terms Miles CPM negotiated ≥$0.011 per mile; sign-up bonus cost per card ≤$120 Breakage assumptions differ between airline actuary and bank models — misalignment inflates miles liability on SAP S/4HANA FI balance sheet by up to 15% ⚠
1.3 Execute partnership agreement and integration SLA Contract execution ≤90 days from term sheet; SLA uptime commitment ≥99.5% PCI-DSS and GDPR/CCPA data-sharing clauses require iterative legal review — delays frequently push launch dates 4–8 weeks beyond plan
2.1 Define card tier earn rates and bonus categories Earn rate design approved by actuary within 10 business days; ≥3 spend bonus categories defined Earn rule complexity (dining, travel, grocery multipliers) creates combinatorial edge cases in Comarch rule engine — insufficient QA leads to overpayment incidents
2.2 Configure earn rules in loyalty management platform Rule configuration UAT pass rate ≥98%; zero overpayment defects in regression suite BIN range changes during card reissue cycles (e.g., chip migration) are not proactively communicated by Visa/Mastercard — stale BIN mapping causes missed miles posts
2.3 Validate earn rules against PSS booking data Miles earn accuracy ≥99.9% in UAT; <5 exception cases per 1,000 test transactions Altéa loyalty module integration requires custom API adapter for co-brand transactions — adapter versioning lags Altéa PSS upgrades, causing earn rule regressions post-patch ⚠
2.4 Certify integration with bank card processor Certification completed ≤30 days; API response latency ≤200ms p99 Bank processor certification environments are infrequently refreshed — test data does not reflect production edge cases, leading to post-launch transaction failures
3.1 Receive daily transaction file from bank partner File receipt by 06:00 local time daily; file completeness ≥99.95% of expected records Bank SFTP file delivery SLAs are frequently breached (5–10 min late) — cascading delay into daily miles posting batch impacts member experience for same-day redemptions
3.2 Match cardholder to loyalty programme member Member match rate ≥99.2%; unmatched queue resolved within 48 hours Name discrepancies between bank card embossing (legal name) and loyalty profile (preferred name) cause false negatives — no automated fuzzy-match in current Comarch-Redshift pipeline ⚠
3.3 Calculate miles earned per matched transaction Miles calculation accuracy ≥99.99%; batch processing completed within 2 hours of file receipt MCC mapping inconsistencies from Visa/Mastercard network (e.g., new fintech merchants with catch-all MCCs) result in incorrect bonus tier application — no real-time MCC correction feed
3.4 Post miles to member loyalty account Miles posting within 24 hours of transaction date; member notification sent ≤1 hour post-post Suspended accounts (fraud hold by bank) do not trigger Comarch status sync — miles post to suspended accounts creating redemption liability that cannot be fulfilled
4.1 Execute co-brand card acquisition campaign Campaign conversion rate ≥2.5% of targeted members; cost per new card account ≤$85 Boarding pass insert channel requires coordination with PSS vendor (Altéa) for printing logic — lead time is 6 weeks, making last-minute promotional changes impossible
4.2 Validate welcome bonus eligibility for new cardholders Welcome bonus activation within 5 business days of threshold met; ≤0.1% erroneous bonus grants Bank spend threshold data arrives in arrears (T+3 days) — members who query missing welcome bonus generate high CSAT complaints before data is available ⚠
4.3 Activate welcome bonus miles for eligible cardholders Welcome bonus credited ≤24 hours of eligibility approval; zero duplicate grants in batch Idempotency controls in Comarch bonus posting API are not enforced at the bank feed layer — duplicate activation events from bank cause double-grant incidents requiring costly manual reversal
5.1 Reconcile miles sold to bank vs miles posted Monthly reconciliation variance ≤0.05% of total miles posted; reconciliation completed by day 5 of following month SAP FI miles liability account uses standard cost per mile — market-rate fluctuations in miles CPM are not reflected until year-end revaluation, distorting monthly P&L by up to $2M ⚠
5.2 Invoice bank partner for miles purchased Invoice issued within 3 business days of reconciliation sign-off; DSO ≤35 days Bank disputes over MCC-categorised bonus miles (billed at higher CPM) are frequent — lack of automated MCC audit trail in SAP AR module prolongs dispute resolution to 60+ days
6.1 Generate quarterly partnership performance report Active cardholder rate ≥65% (spent in last 90 days); miles revenue per active card ≥$180/yr No single source of truth for 'active cardholder' definition — Comarch, Altéa, and bank definitions diverge, causing QBR figures to be challenged by bank partner and delaying review sign-off ⚠
6.2 Conduct annual contract review and renegotiation Contract renewal completed ≥90 days before expiry; CPM improvement ≥3% vs prior term Airline bargaining leverage is limited without alliance membership — bank partners benchmark co-brand terms against carriers with larger frequent flyer bases, compressing CPM improvement potential ⚠
