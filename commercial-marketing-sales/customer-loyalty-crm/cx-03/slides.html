StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Member Initiates Redemption via Digital Channel Digital channel redemption rate ≥85% of all award transactions Session timeout during multi-step redemption causes abandoned requests; no mid-session save capability in legacy web portals
1.2 Validate Member Account Status & Tier Account validation response time <2 seconds (99th percentile) Members with disputed fraud flags are incorrectly blocked; manual review queue SLA averages 4 business days, causing member complaints ⚠
1.3 Verify Miles Balance Sufficiency Real-time balance accuracy ≥99.95% (no stale cache reads) Miles balance caching latency of up to 15 minutes causes members to see inflated balances post-accrual, leading to failed redemption attempts at checkout ⚠
2.1 Query Award Seat Inventory in PSS Award seat search response time <3 seconds; award seat availability ≥10% of total cabin capacity on published routes Award inventory buckets are statically defined and rarely recalibrated; high-demand routes show zero award availability 6+ months out despite low commercial load ⚠
2.2 Apply Award Inventory Control Rules Award inventory utilisation rate ≥72% of allocated buckets per quarter Blackout date logic is manually maintained in a spreadsheet outside the RM system; discrepancies between RM rules and loyalty portal display cause member-facing availability errors ⚠
2.3 Check Partner Award Availability Partner API availability ≥99.5% uptime; catalogue refresh latency <24 hours Partner APIs use non-standard schemas; mapping failures cause 3–5% of partner redemption requests to error silently without member notification ⚠
2.4 Hold Award Seat or Partner Slot Hold-to-booking conversion rate ≥78%; hold timeout abandonment rate <22% 15-minute TTL is insufficient for members using screen readers or with accessibility needs; no TTL extension capability exists in the current PSS configuration
3.1 Calculate Miles Required & Applicable Taxes Pricing calculation accuracy 100% (zero mis-priced awards issued) Government-imposed departure taxes and carrier surcharges are inconsistently applied to award tickets; manual overrides required for certain markets increase processing time by 40%
3.2 Collect Cash Co-Pay for Taxes & Surcharges Payment authorisation success rate ≥97.5%; fraud decline rate <0.8% PCI-DSS tokenisation scope forces redirect to external payment page, breaking the redemption flow and increasing cart abandonment by an estimated 12% ⚠
3.3 Member Confirms Award Booking Confirmation completion rate ≥80% of members who reach review screen Members presented with unexpected surcharge totals at confirmation abandon at a 35% rate; no pre-checkout surcharge estimate is shown earlier in the funnel ⚠
3.4 Deduct Miles from Member Account Miles deduction posting time <5 seconds; deduction error rate <0.01% Concurrent redemption requests from the same account (e.g., via app and web simultaneously) can cause double-deduction if idempotency checks are not enforced at the API layer
4.1 Issue Award Ticket or E-Voucher Ticket issuance success rate ≥99.8%; issuance time <30 seconds Award tickets on interline itineraries require manual segment-by-segment ticketing in Altéa; automation gap adds 20–45 minutes per complex itinerary and scales poorly during peak redemption periods ⚠
4.2 Transmit Partner Booking via API Partner booking API success rate ≥98.5%; confirmation delivery time <2 minutes Real-time partner booking APIs cover only 60% of partners; remaining 40% use batch file exchange with 24-hour confirmation lag, leaving members without instant booking confirmation ⚠
4.3 Send E-Ticket & Booking Confirmation to Member Confirmation delivery rate ≥99.2%; delivery time <5 minutes of ticket issuance Members who update contact preferences in the loyalty portal mid-session may receive confirmations to a stale email address due to CRM sync latency of up to 6 hours
4.4 Update Member Redemption History Ledger update latency <10 seconds; history record accuracy 100% Partner redemption events written by the NDC gateway use a different transaction format than PSS award tickets; consolidation into a single view requires nightly batch job, preventing real-time statement accuracy
5.1 Reconcile Miles Deducted vs Tickets Issued Reconciliation match rate ≥99.9%; exception resolution within 2 business days Ticketing system and loyalty ledger use different timestamps (UTC vs local departure time); timezone mismatch causes ~0.3% of daily transactions to appear as exceptions requiring manual investigation ⚠
5.2 Report Liability Reduction to Finance Monthly loyalty liability reporting accuracy ±0.5% variance vs actuals; posted within 3 business days of month-end CPM (cost per mile) used for liability valuation is updated quarterly; interim month fluctuations in redemption mix (premium vs economy) create material variance in reported liability that is not visible until quarter-end
5.3 Analyse Redemption Patterns for Programme Optimisation Award seat utilisation rate ≥75%; partner redemption share ≥20% of total redemptions Redemption data from partner ecosystems arrives with 48-hour delay and inconsistent granularity; analytics dashboards cannot provide same-day programme performance visibility needed for dynamic award pricing decisions
