1.1 Ingest flight activity from PSS transaction feed Transaction ingestion latency ≤4 hours post-departure; error rate <0.1% PSS transaction feeds use EDIFACT format; schema mismatches between Altéa versions cause silent drop of codeshare and interline segments, under-crediting elite members
1.2 Validate fare class earning eligibility vs ATPCO rules Eligibility rule coverage ≥99.5% of active fare families; <0.5% manual review rate ATPCO fare rules update fortnightly; loyalty rule tables often lag by 1–2 cycles, causing incorrect non-qualifying flags on newly filed promotional fares and generating member complaints ⚠
1.3 Post qualifying miles/segments/spend to member ledger Ledger posting accuracy ≥99.9%; retro-credit request rate <2% of eligible transactions Partner airline codeshare miles require a separate interline settlement file; delays of up to 72 hours in partner data delivery create visible ledger gaps that prompt unnecessary member calls to contact centre
2.1 Aggregate qualifying credits against tier thresholds Batch refresh cycle ≤6 hours; member progress accuracy vs ledger ±0 credits Real-time tier progress is surfaced via mobile app; Redshift batch latency causes app to show stale progress bars, triggering trust complaints from high-value members after qualifying flights
2.2 Apply partner airline and credit card earning multipliers Multiplier application accuracy ≥99.8%; promotional credit disputes <0.3% of bonus transactions Co-branded credit card spend multipliers are fed by card issuer APIs on a monthly settlement cycle; mid-month tier assessments cannot include unsettled spend, causing members to miss tier upgrades they would have earned had the spend posted on time
2.3 Flag members within 10% of next tier threshold Nudge email open rate ≥35%; incremental qualifying activity uplift ≥8% among nudged cohort Personalisation tokens for nudge emails pull live balance from Altéa Loyalty API; API rate limits during peak send windows cause token failures, resulting in generic messages that undermine member engagement
2.4 Assess whether tier upgrade threshold has been met Tier threshold assessment latency ≤1 hour of qualifying transaction posting; false-positive rate <0.01% Concurrent qualifying flights on the same calendar day can create race conditions in the threshold check, causing duplicate tier upgrade events that provision double benefit sets until a nightly reconciliation job corrects them ⚠
3.1 Award new tier status in Amadeus Altéa Loyalty Tier award processing time ≤30 minutes from trigger; tier award error rate <0.05% Tier validity date logic varies by programme (calendar-year vs rolling-12-month); incorrect configuration during programme rule changes has granted tier status with wrong expiry dates, requiring costly retroactive corrections
3.2 Provision upgrade coupons and lounge access in PSS Benefit provisioning completion ≤2 hours of tier award; coupon redemption error rate at airport <0.5% Benefit provisioning relies on Altéa OSI/SSR message injection into the PNR; check-in agents at third-party handling stations without direct Altéa access may not see provisioned benefits, causing lounge access denials and escalations
3.3 Dispatch tier upgrade congratulation and benefit summary Email delivery within 2 hours of tier award; delivery success rate ≥98%; unsubscribe rate <0.2% Physical elite card production is outsourced; card delivery SLA of 10 business days conflicts with member expectation of immediate tangible recognition, generating contact centre calls in the interim window
4.1 Receive upgrade request at booking or check-in gate Upgrade request capture rate ≥99% of eligible members; request queue processing time ≤60 seconds Upgrade request channels (online check-in, kiosk, agent) do not share a single priority queue; the same coupon can be presented simultaneously across channels, creating duplicate upgrade requests that consume inventory incorrectly
4.2 Check upgrade class seat inventory availability in PSS Upgrade clearance rate for top-tier members ≥70% on eligible routes; waitlist clear rate ≥50% by T-24h Upgrade inventory buckets are controlled by Revenue Management; RM teams frequently close upgrade classes on high-demand flights up to T-72h, leaving top-tier members waitlisted on routes where premium seats are visible but commercially blocked ⚠
4.3 Process upgrade rebook or manage waitlist clearance Upgrade processing time ≤5 minutes at check-in; coupon deduction accuracy 100% Automated upgrade engines do not always respect manual override priorities set by premium service desks for VVIP members; automated processing can displace a manually held upgrade, requiring agent correction under time pressure at the gate
4.4 Reissue boarding pass with new cabin assignment Boarding pass reissue time ≤2 minutes; boarding document error rate <0.01% Mobile boarding pass updates via Altéa's push mechanism require the member's app to be online at time of upgrade; offline devices display the original boarding pass, creating security lane discrepancies and boarding delays
5.1 Log tier dispute or competitive status match claim in CRM Case creation time ≤5 minutes; first response SLA ≤4 business hours for elite members Status match requests from competing programme members require manual verification of competitor tier documentation; no automated data exchange with competing programmes means agents must visually verify screenshots, which are easily manipulated
5.2 Review documentation and approve or reject dispute Dispute resolution time ≤5 business days; first-contact resolution rate ≥80%; approval accuracy vs policy ≥99% Agents lack a single-screen view joining Salesforce case data with Altéa Loyalty transaction history; switching between systems to verify activity increases handle time and introduces manual transcription errors in the resolution record ⚠
5.3 Apply manual tier or credit adjustment in loyalty system Adjustment posting accuracy 100%; case closure within 1 business day of approval Manual tier adjustments bypass the standard threshold assessment workflow, meaning benefit provisioning in the PSS must also be triggered manually; agents without elevated permissions cannot complete both steps, creating a handoff gap where tier is awarded but benefits are not yet active
6.1 Run end-of-qualifying-period tier review batch job Batch job completion within 4-hour overnight window; zero failed member records; data reconciliation variance <0.01% Annual batch job processes millions of member records in a single overnight window; AWS Redshift cluster scaling has historically underestimated job duration, causing batch overruns that delay morning check-in benefit reads at airports on the first day of the new programme year
6.2 Assess requalification threshold met or missed Requalification assessment accuracy ≥99.99%; zero incorrect downgrades of members who requalified Grace period eligibility rules (e.g., extended status for members within 500 miles of requalification) are configured manually in Altéa; rule misconfiguration during programme refreshes has incorrectly downgraded members who should have received a grace extension, resulting in high-value member attrition ⚠
6.3 Execute tier renewal, downgrade, or extension notification Notification delivery within 24 hours of batch completion; downgrade appeal rate <5% among downgraded members Downgrade communications must be carefully sequenced to avoid triggering opt-outs; generic downgrade emails sent without personalised re-earn incentives have historically caused measurable NPS drops among former top-tier members who subsequently churn to competitor programmes
