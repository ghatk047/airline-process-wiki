Slide 3 of 3 — KPIs & Risk Register
StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Monitor competitive fare environment Monitoring cycle ≤24 hrs for all revenue-managed markets ATPCO Insights carries a 24-hr lag vs real-time competitor GDS filings; analysts must cross-check Sabre FareManager for same-day changes, adding manual effort
1.2 Identify and classify fare change trigger Trigger-to-brief turnaround ≤4 hrs for urgent competitive matches Differentiating reactive (competitor match) vs strategic (sale/promo) triggers requires manual cross-referencing of RM inventory alerts and market briefs — no integrated triage workflow exists ⚠
1.3 Align fare strategy with revenue management RM alignment sign-off within 2 business hours of trigger brief submission Disconnect between NRM O&D inventory state and ATPCO-filed availability can cause short-lived promotional fares to sell out before retraction is processed, creating revenue leakage ⚠
2.1 Define fare amounts, OD pairs, and basis codes Fare basis code uniqueness validation pass rate 100% prior to rule coding ATPCO 8-character fare basis code limit constrains structured naming conventions for complex promotional fares with multiple conditions, forcing non-intuitive abbreviations that increase audit risk
2.2 Code ATPCO rule categories (Cat 3/5/6/7/14/15/25) Rule coding accuracy ≥99.5% (errors per 1,000 rule records filed) Cat 25 (Fare by Rule) inheritance logic silently breaks when a parent tariff record is updated without cascading to child rules — causes undetected pricing errors across hundreds of derived markets
2.3 Build routing data and footnote records Routing validation pass rate ≥99% on first submission to ATPCO IATA Routing Validator rejects non-IATA city pair combinations — LCC point-to-point networks with thin interline coverage frequently trigger routing exceptions requiring manual override justification
2.4 Validate rule completeness and conflict check Pre-submission conflict rate ≤2%; zero records submitted with open blocking flags ATPCO pre-submission validator checks syntactic correctness but not cross-category semantic conflicts (e.g. Cat 5 advance purchase window exceeding Cat 6 min stay) — these pass technical validation but create unsellable fares in GDS ⚠
3.1 Submit fare transactions via ATPCO Fares Portal Same-day filing completion for urgent competitive matches ≥95% ATPCO urgent filing (T+0) incurs a per-record surcharge — frequent reactive strategies against low-cost competitors inflate pricing operations costs, with no SLA guarantee on urgent processing times
3.2 Process ATPCO validation error report Blocking error resolution turnaround ≤1 business hour; warning resolution ≤4 hrs ATPCO error codes are non-self-explanatory (e.g. ERR 1078) — teams maintain internal error codebooks not derived from ATPCO official documentation, creating tribal knowledge dependency and onboarding risk ⚠
3.3 Confirm effective date sequencing and filing receipt Effective date accuracy 100% — zero same-day date conflicts or timezone mismatches Time-zone ambiguity in ATPCO effective dates (UTC vs local first/last point of departure) causes fares to go live or expire at unintended local times, particularly affecting international and trans-Pacific filings
4.1 Verify fare distribution across GDS channels GDS distribution lag ≤2 hrs post-ATPCO effective time; 100% coverage across all three major GDS channels Travelport GDS historically lags Sabre and Amadeus by 30–90 min on ATPCO fare updates — agencies on Travelport may sell at outdated fares during the distribution gap, triggering Agency Debit Memo (ADM) disputes
4.2 Perform live GDS pricing audit Pricing accuracy ≥99.8% across audit sample of ≥20 itineraries per market GDS combinability logic (Cat 10) can produce unintended through-fares when new local fares combine with existing through-fares — audit must include half-round-trip combinations, not only O&D pairs, or combinability errors reach ticketing queues ⚠
4.3 Activate NDC and direct channel fare distribution NDC channel parity with GDS fares ≤15 min lag; NDC fare availability ≥98% of filed itineraries NDC fare structures (IATA NDC 17.2+) do not natively support all ATPCO rule categories — ancillary-bundled and seat-inclusive fares require custom NDC offer mapping, adding 1–2 hrs to activation and creating parity gaps vs GDS
5.1 Monitor competitive matching and over-filing activity Competitor over-filing detection within 4 hrs; monitoring coverage ≥95% of revenue-significant markets ATPCO Insights competitive feed does not cover NDC-only fares filed by competitors — LCC fare strategies distributed solely via NDC are invisible in ATPCO monitoring, creating a growing competitive intelligence gap
5.2 Execute periodic ATPCO tariff rule audit Stale rule ratio (>180 days since last review) ≤5% of active tariff records Accumulated legacy Cat 25 rules from prior sale campaigns create tariff bloat — stale rules still price in GDS if not explicitly expired, leading to unintended low-fare availability on high-demand travel dates and uncaptured revenue
5.3 File fare retraction or amendment as required Retraction-to-GDS-depublication lag ≤2 hrs via urgent ATPCO channel; zero open retraction requests >4 hrs ATPCO retraction does not immediately purge cached fare data in all GDS ticketing queues — tickets issued in the depublication window may be priced at retracted fares, requiring ADM recovery process and eroding agency trust ⚠
