StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Define LFG eligibility scope & fare rules Policy configuration cycle ≤5 business days; zero ambiguous eligibility clauses flagged by Legal ATPCO fare rule structures make same-fare-basis comparisons across carriers difficult; mismatched fare conditions (e.g. refundability, baggage) trigger spurious customer claims that are hard to reject
1.2 Configure claim window & compensation tiers Configuration accuracy ≥99% — zero erroneous payouts in first 30 days post-launch Altéa PSS compensation rule tables require manual maintenance; there is no native LFG workflow module, so each rule change must be scripted separately for web, call-centre, and API channels
1.3 Validate policy against DOT tariff requirements Compliance review completion ≤3 business days; zero DOT tariff violations at launch DOT price advertising rules (14 CFR Part 399) require precise disclosure of LFG terms in all channels; a mismatch between the ATPCO-filed rule and the website copy has triggered enforcement action at peer carriers ⚠
2.1 Schedule automated competitor fare data feeds Market coverage ≥95% of LFG-eligible routes; data freshness ≤4 hours Screen-scraping competitor OTA prices violates most carrier terms of service and is legally risky; ATPCO-published fares lag real-time OTA dynamic pricing by several hours, creating a window where a competitor's low fare expires before the airline can verify a claim
2.2 Capture immutable reference fare at time of booking Fare snapshot latency ≤2 s per booking; capture rate ≥99.9% of LFG-eligible transactions Dynamic pricing means the fare quoted to a customer can change within minutes of booking; the snapshot must be cryptographically signed or write-once to prevent retroactive manipulation by either party
2.3 Classify competitor fare for LFG comparability Correct comparability classification ≥97%; false-positive ineligibility rate ≤2% Bundled fares (seat + bag + flexibility) vs. unbundled competitor fares create apples-to-oranges comparisons that PROS cannot resolve automatically; a manual adjudication queue exists but has no SLA, causing consumer complaints ⚠
3.1 Receive LFG claim via digital or voice channel Claim intake acknowledgement ≤4 business hours; self-service digital claim completion rate ≥55% High volume of ineligible claims (expired competitor fares, wrong route or date) creates manual review burden; Salesforce has no automated fraud-scoring integration, so every claim enters the same queue regardless of obvious ineligibility signals
3.2 Verify booking eligibility against LFG policy rules Eligibility verification turnaround ≤2 business hours; accuracy ≥98% PNR modification history in Altéa is often incomplete for bookings made through OTA intermediaries; the original-fare field is overwritten by post-booking changes, making verification ambiguous and requiring manual GDS history extraction ⚠
3.3 Authenticate competitor fare evidence via GDS/ATPCO Evidence authentication turnaround ≤1 business hour; false-positive acceptance rate ≤3% Screenshots can be manipulated or show fares that were never genuinely bookable; the airline must independently verify via Sabre or ATPCO that the fare was published and seat was available at the claimed price at the time the customer booked ⚠
4.1 Calculate fare differential & compensation amount Calculation accuracy ≥99.9%; average compensation computation time ≤30 min Currency conversion for international routes and fare conditions (change fees, carrier-imposed surcharges, taxes) complicate net-fare differential calculations; PROS does not natively handle ATPCO tax stripping, requiring manual adjustments
4.2 Escalate large-differential claims for manager approval Escalated claim approval turnaround ≤4 business hours; escalation rate documented monthly Salesforce has no native pricing-authority approval workflow; escalations route via email, causing delays above threshold and creating an audit gap where manager decisions are not formally captured in the CRM record ⚠
4.3 Issue compensation decision notification to customer Notification delivery ≤15 min of decision; post-claim CSAT ≥4.2/5.0 Multi-channel customer communication (email, app push, SMS) requires manual coordination across Salesforce and a separate ESP; there is no unified outbound notification service, so message duplication and inconsistent status language are recurring issues
5.1 Process refund or travel credit to customer account Refund processing ≤7 business days (per DOT requirement); travel credit issuance ≤24 hours; zero incorrect postings SAP refund processing requires the Altéa PNR to be in a settled state; open itineraries with future segments block automated refund initiation, requiring manual intervention by Revenue Accounting and causing SLA breaches
5.2 Award frequent flyer miles compensation if applicable Miles credit turnaround ≤24 hours; posting accuracy ≥99.99% Miles compensation valuation vs. cash refund creates accounting complexity — Loyalty posts miles at face value but Finance recognises liability at estimated redemption cost; batch reconciliation between the two systems introduces a T+1 lag that delays audit sign-off
5.3 Update PNR & CRM record with claim disposition Record update completeness ≥99.5%; full audit trail retained for 24-month regulatory period Altéa OSI remarks are free-text fields; there is no structured LFG claim field in the PNR, so data extraction for analytics requires brittle regex parsing that breaks when agents use non-standard remark formats
6.1 Aggregate LFG claim volume & payout metrics Dashboard refresh ≤T+1 business day; cross-system data completeness ≥98% Claims data sits in Salesforce, financial settlements in SAP, and booking records in Altéa — no native cross-system ETL pipeline exists; manual exports introduce reconciliation errors and a recurring 3–5% data gap between Finance and Operations views
6.2 Benchmark program cost against revenue retention LFG program ROI ≥2.5× (revenue retained per $1 paid out); cost-per-claim ≤$45 Counterfactual analysis (would the customer have cancelled without the guarantee?) cannot be measured without A/B test infrastructure; ROI is estimated using proxy metrics rather than observed behaviour, limiting confidence in market-level policy decisions ⚠
6.3 Recommend & implement LFG policy adjustments Policy revision cycle ≤30 days; zero lapse in published guarantee terms during transition; ATPCO filing lead time ≥7 days before effective date ATPCO fare rule amendments require a minimum 7-day advance notice period for GDS distribution; when a competitor launches an aggressive promotion, the airline cannot rapidly narrow LFG eligibility to limit liability — it is locked in for at least a week
