Slide 3 of 3 — KPIs & Risk Register
StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Ingest Competitor Fare Data Feeds Feed freshness ≤4 hours from ATPCO filing event OAG data reflects filed fares, not real-time availability — lowest bookable fare may differ; requires GDS cross-check
1.2 Pull GDS Availability & Lowest Bookable Fare GDS poll coverage ≥95% of monitored O&Ds per cycle GDS screen-scraping agreements restrict query frequency; high-volume polling risks Sabre rate-limit suspension
1.3 Validate and Cleanse Incoming Fare Data Data quality score ≥98% (nulls + duplicates < 2% of records) Multi-currency fare records require real-time FX normalisation; stale FX rates distort price-index calculations
1.4 Gate: Assess Data Quality Threshold Re-ingestion rate <5% of daily batches Repeated feed failures during ATPCO filing windows (Mon/Fri peaks) delay competitive response by 24-48 hours ⚠
2.1 Map Competitor Fare Families to Own Fare Basis Crosswalk coverage ≥90% of active competitor fare codes Unbundled competitor fares (ancillary-stripped) inflate apparent price gap; true total-price comparison requires ancillary fee modelling
2.2 Calculate Competitive Price Index by O&D Price index within ±5% of strategic target band for ≥80% of monitored O&Ds PROS RM price-index module requires manual refresh when own fares are updated mid-cycle; automated trigger not yet configured
2.3 Identify O&Ds with Material Competitive Fare Gap Shortlist generated within 2 hours of data ingestion cycle completion PROS RM gap detection relies on static threshold rules; dynamic thresholds by market maturity and load factor are not natively supported ⚠
3.1 Segment Demand Elasticity by O&D and Cabin Elasticity model R² ≥0.80 for top-50 revenue O&Ds SkyCAST elasticity models are calibrated on historical data; post-pandemic travel pattern shifts reduce model accuracy on recently-opened routes
3.2 Model Revenue Impact of Fare Repositioning Model run time ≤30 minutes per O&D scenario batch NRM scenario modelling does not automatically account for competitor retaliation; analyst must manually layer in retaliation assumptions
3.3 Gate: Revenue Uplift Exceeds Approval Threshold Repricing candidates with confirmed positive revenue case ≥85% of submissions Threshold calibration is manual and reviewed only quarterly; rapidly-changing competitive environments render thresholds stale within weeks ⚠
3.4 Build Fare Repositioning Recommendation Package Recommendation package completed within 1 business day of gate approval Manual deck preparation in Tableau/PowerPoint is time-intensive; no templated recommendation workflow exists, leading to inconsistent submission quality
4.1 Route Recommendation to Pricing Committee Committee review turnaround ≤2 business days from submission Pricing committee quorum requirements delay time-sensitive competitive responses; competitors can re-price in hours while approval takes days
4.2 Gate: Pricing Committee Approval Decision First-pass approval rate ≥70% of submitted recommendations Committee rejections due to incomplete competitor context require analyst rework, consuming 1-2 days and potentially missing the competitive window ⚠
4.3 File Approved Fare Changes via ATPCO Filing accuracy rate ≥99.5%; zero Cat-15/Cat-25 rule errors ATPCO filing cycles (Mon and Thu effective dates) constrain competitive response agility; urgent off-cycle filings require ATPCO expedite fee
4.4 Load and Validate Fares in Altéa PSS Fare loading SLA ≤2 hours post-ATPCO effective date; booking-flow validation pass rate 100% Altéa fare loader occasionally misinterprets complex Cat-25 combinability rules, producing phantom availability or incorrect pricing at booking — requires manual QA
5.1 Monitor Post-Change Booked-Fare Mix Monitoring dashboard refreshed every 4 hours; booked yield vs. target tracked from day 1 post-filing NRM post-change monitoring requires manual dashboard setup per O&D; no automated alert when booked yield deviates >5% from forecast
5.2 Gate: Evaluate Competitive Position Restoration Target: competitive price index within ±5% of strategic band within 14 days of fare change effective date Competitor retaliation (matching or undercutting) triggers a second repricing cycle; rapid tit-for-tat fare wars erode yield for all carriers with no passenger benefit ⚠
5.3 Publish Intelligence Report and Close Cycle Cycle report published within 5 business days of position-restored confirmation Institutional knowledge from closed cycles is rarely captured systematically; repeated competitive patterns go unrecognised across analyst handovers
