StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Receive & Log Charter Request Request-to-quote turnaround ≤4 hours of receipt No standardised intake form; requests arrive via email, phone, and broker portals with inconsistent data quality, causing re-work before feasibility can begin
1.2 Validate Route & Airport Feasibility Feasibility assessment completed ≤2 hours of request receipt Airport infrastructure constraints (runway PCN, curfews, Category III ILS availability) not always current in SkySYM, requiring manual NOTAM and AIP cross-check ⚠
1.3 Assess Fleet Availability for Charter Date Charter fleet utilisation rate ≥85% on confirmed charter days Charter demand routinely conflicts with peak scheduled operations; no dedicated charter fleet buffer exists, forcing displacement of scheduled rotations ⚠
2.1 Verify Air Service Agreements & Permit Scope Permit scope identified ≤1 business day of feasibility sign-off ASA gaps for new international charter destinations; some governments (Middle East, West Africa) require 72–120 hours advance notice, invalidating short-lead requests
2.2 Apply for Overflight & Landing Permits Permit approval rate ≥95% within 48 hours of application Permit rejections or multi-day delays from foreign CAAs can invalidate charter date commitments already communicated to clients; no automated status polling in SITA system ⚠
2.3 Coordinate Airport Slot & Ground Handler Slot secured ≥5 days before departure; ground handler confirmed ≥72 hours before STD Ad-hoc charter requests often arrive within 48 hours of departure, leaving no slot buffer at Level 3 coordinated airports; late slots attract handling surcharges of 30–50% above standard rates ⚠
3.1 Assign Aircraft Tail & Confirm MX Release Aircraft assigned ≥72 hours before departure; MX release confirmed ≥24 hours before STD Last-minute MEL findings discovered during pre-departure inspection force tail swaps after crew pairing, catering, and ground handler briefings are complete, causing cascading delays ⚠
3.2 Build Crew Pairing Within FAR Part 117 Limits Crew assignment completed ≥48 hours before departure; 100% FAR Part 117 duty compliance Charter routes often exceed standard trip lengths or require international type ratings (ETOPS or specific country approvals); limited qualified crew pool for remote or new destinations ⚠
3.3 Confirm Special Handling & Catering Requirements Special service confirmation delivered ≤24 hours after crew assignment VIP and government charter clients frequently request bespoke catering and in-cabin configurations incompatible with standard galley setup; last-minute changes after catering uplift incur waste costs
4.1 Calculate All-In Charter Price & Margin Gross margin on charter ≥15% above direct operating cost; quote issued ≤2 hours of feasibility confirmation Fuel price volatility between quote acceptance and flight date creates margin erosion; no automatic fuel surcharge adjustment mechanism is integrated into the charter pricing model
4.2 Issue Charter Contract & Collect Deposit Contract executed ≤24 hours of quote acceptance; deposit received ≥5 days before departure Payment delays from charter brokers are common; deposit terms not enforced consistently; SAP AR integration gaps mean charter revenue recognition is sometimes deferred to the following month-end ⚠
4.3 Load Charter Flight into PSS & Block Inventory Flight created in PSS ≤4 hours of contract execution; zero open inventory seats on charter segment Charter flights must be created as special service segments in Altéa; misclassification as scheduled flights causes revenue leakage and double-counting in RM yield reporting
5.1 File ICAO Flight Plan & Check NOTAMs Flight plan filed ≥3 hours before STD; NOTAM briefing completed ≥90 min before STD Last-minute NOTAM closures (runway, restricted airspace, volcanic ash advisories) require re-routing; no automated re-route trigger exists for charter flights outside the scheduled network ⚠
5.2 Conduct Pre-Departure Crew & Cabin Briefing Briefing completed ≥90 min before STD; 100% crew attendance confirmed VIP or government charter clients sometimes request last-minute route or timing changes post-briefing, requiring flight plan amendment and potential slot forfeiture at the destination
5.3 Execute Charter Flight & Capture Actuals Block time variance ≤10 min vs. planned; fuel burn within ±3% of flight plan estimate ATC delays on ad-hoc routes not automatically captured in Altéa; actual fuel and time data require manual post-flight reconciliation, creating a 24–48 hour reporting lag
6.1 Reconcile Charter Revenue & Costs in SAP Revenue recognition completed ≤5 business days of flight; invoice matching rate ≥90% automated Charter cost allocation to cost centres is manual; catering and handling invoices frequently arrive 30+ days post-flight, requiring accruals that distort period-end reporting
6.2 Update Charter Pricing Model & Lessons Learned Pricing model refresh cycle: quarterly; margin forecast accuracy improvement ≥5% per cycle Lessons-learned data are not systematically fed back into the charter pricing model; repeat cost overruns on the same international routes indicate a structural feedback loop gap
