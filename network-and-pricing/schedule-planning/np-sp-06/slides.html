StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Capture schedule change request in SkyWORKS Request logged within 2 hours of trigger receipt Change requests arrive via email, Slack, and verbal channels with no single intake system — risk of duplicate or conflicting change records in SkyWORKS
1.2 Classify change type, scope, and urgency Classification completed within 4 hours of request capture Seasonal vs. irregular-operations changes require different downstream workflows; mis-classification delays IATA slot filing deadlines (SCR/ACR windows)
1.3 Confirm route licence and fleet authority Feasibility gate cleared within 1 business day; 100% of changes with regulatory exposure escalated before modelling International route authority changes require DOT/foreign CAA coordination with 30–90 day lead times — late identification blocks schedule publication ⚠
2.1 Simulate O&D demand for proposed schedule Forecast MAPE ≤8% on 90-day horizon; load factor projection ≥83% for change to proceed SkyCAST demand models are calibrated on historical actuals — new or thin markets with <12 months of data produce unreliable forecasts, requiring manual analyst override
2.2 Model network-wide ripple effects in SkySYM Zero MCT violations in output schedule; connecting flow spill ≤2% of total affected O&D SkySYM scenario runs can take 45–90 minutes for full network simulation — time pressure during irregular operations compresses analysis window and increases error risk
2.3 Run fleet assignment optimisation to resolve conflicts Fleet conflict resolution rate ≥95% within 24 hours; gauge utilisation variance ≤5% vs. baseline plan Fleet-constrained periods (peak summer, MAX grounding scenarios) leave SkyMAX with limited substitution options — manual intervention required and risks cascading reassignments
2.4 Gate: validate change against network thresholds Gate review completed within 1 business day; ≥90% of changes pass gate on first submission Gate criteria (load factor, contribution margin, MCT compliance) are not formally codified — approval outcome varies by reviewer, creating schedule quality inconsistency ⚠
3.1 Check slot availability and filing deadlines 100% of slot change requests filed before IATA deadline; slot return compliance rate ≥98% Level 3 coordinated airports (e.g., LHR, NRT) require slot exchanges negotiated bilaterally — lack of alliance membership limits swap leverage and can block desired time windows
3.2 Validate gate and terminal capacity at affected airports Gate conflict resolution within 48 hours of identification; turnaround time ≥25 min protected for all affected rotations Airport AMS data feeds are not always synchronised with SkyWORKS — gate conflicts discovered late in the process require emergency reassignments that increase ground delay risk
3.3 Gate: confirm slot and infrastructure availability Slot and gate confirmation within 5 business days of change initiation; escalation resolved within 10 business days Slot negotiation at congested airports (ORD, LAX, JFK) can extend beyond planning horizons — forces provisional schedule publication with risk of subsequent amendment ⚠
4.1 Model revenue and displacement impact in Amadeus NRM Net revenue impact calculated within 2 business days; minimum threshold: net contribution change ≥ -$25K per changed rotation Amadeus NRM O&D optimisation recalibrates across all connected itineraries — a single frequency change can trigger widespread inventory reoptimisation that temporarily degrades load factors on unrelated flights
4.2 Assess fare structure and pricing impact via ATPCO 100% of fares requiring re-filing identified before SSIM submission; fare amendment cycle ≤48 hours ATPCO fare rules contain explicit schedule-dependency conditions (permitted flights, connection times) — schedule changes that breach these rules automatically invalidate published fares and require emergency re-filing
4.3 Gate: commercial approval of net revenue impact Commercial gate decision within 2 business days; ≤15% of changes recycled at this gate Commercial gate approval is sequential with no parallel workstream — schedule change velocity is capped at VP availability, creating bottlenecks during peak planning periods (S/W season transitions) ⚠
5.1 Update master schedule and generate SSIM file SSIM generated within 4 hours of commercial approval; zero data integrity errors in SSIM validation report SkyWORKS SSIM export requires manual mapping of airline-internal equipment codes to IATA aircraft type designators — mapping errors cause GDS display discrepancies and passenger booking failures
5.2 Submit SSIM to GDS channels and NDC API Gateway GDS schedule propagation within 24 hours of SSIM submission; NDC channel update within 4 hours; zero unacknowledged submissions Each GDS has a different SSIM ingestion pipeline and error-reporting format — failures in one channel are not visible in another, requiring manual cross-channel reconciliation to confirm schedule accuracy
5.3 Gate: validate schedule accuracy across all channels Schedule accuracy ≥99.5% across all channels within 48 hours of submission; zero flight-level discrepancies in departure time or equipment type OAG Schedule Analyser is updated on a 48-hour lag — real-time discrepancy detection requires direct GDS API queries that are not automated in the current toolchain ⚠
6.1 Trigger involuntary change notifications via Altéa PSS 100% of affected passengers notified within 24 hours of schedule change publication; DOT significant change threshold compliance rate 100% Altéa PSS applies DOT notification rules based on schedule delta thresholds — but interline and codeshare bookings held in partner PSS systems are not automatically notified, creating DOT compliance risk
6.2 Process re-accommodation requests and fee waivers Re-accommodation rate ≥92% on same-day alternatives; average re-booking handle time ≤8 minutes; refund processing within DOT 7-day credit card window High-volume schedule changes (e.g., full season overhaul) generate re-accommodation queues that exceed contact centre capacity — automated re-accommodation via Altéa rebooking engine has a 78% acceptance rate, leaving 22% requiring agent intervention
6.3 Monitor post-implementation KPIs and close change record Load factor on changed flights ≥83% within 30 days; OTP ≥78% D0; re-accommodation completion rate ≥98% within 72 hours of notification Post-implementation tracking is done manually in Tableau by pulling Altéa booking exports — there is no automated feedback loop from actuals into SkyCAST demand models, so forecast accuracy improvements from change outcomes are not systematically captured
