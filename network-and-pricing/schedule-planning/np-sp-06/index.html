Airline-Specific Risks & Pain Points

Change requests arrive via email, Slack, and verbal channels with no single intake system — risk of duplicate or conflicting change records in SkyWORKS
Seasonal vs. irregular-operations changes require different downstream workflows; mis-classification delays IATA slot filing deadlines (SCR/ACR windows)
International route authority changes require DOT/foreign CAA coordination with 30–90 day lead times — late identification blocks schedule publication
SkyCAST demand models are calibrated on historical actuals — new or thin markets with <12 months of data produce unreliable forecasts, requiring manual analyst override
SkySYM scenario runs can take 45–90 minutes for full network simulation — time pressure during irregular operations compresses analysis window and increases error risk
Fleet-constrained periods (peak summer, MAX grounding scenarios) leave SkyMAX with limited substitution options — manual intervention required and risks cascading reassignments
