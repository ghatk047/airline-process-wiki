StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Extract sales transactions from PSS and GDS feeds Data extraction completeness ≥99.5% of daily transactions Altéa feed latency can delay T+1 reporting; GDS interline sales require separate reconciliation from Sabre and Travelport settlement files
1.2 Ingest GDS booking and yield data via settlement files GDS data ingestion within 4 hours of BSP file receipt Sabre PRISM and Travelport use different file formats requiring dual ETL pipelines; mismatched PNR cross-references cause ~2% of records to fail automatic join
1.3 Validate data quality and apply cleansing rules Data quality score ≥98% (nulls, duplicates, invalid fare codes <2%) ATPCO fare basis code changes not always reflected in Altéa within same reporting cycle, causing mis-classification of fare families ⚠
1.4 Load cleansed data to analytics data warehouse Load completion within 2 hours of data quality sign-off; zero load failures Redshift cluster contention during concurrent ETL and BI query windows causes load job timeouts; workload management (WLM) tuning needed per reporting cycle
2.1 Configure KPI targets and alert thresholds in BI platform 100% of approved KPI targets loaded before reporting cycle open Mid-year target revisions require manual threshold updates across multiple Tableau workbooks; no automated sync from SAP S/4HANA budget module
2.2 Build channel and revenue performance dashboards Dashboard refresh latency ≤15 minutes from data load completion NDC API Gateway booking data lacks full itinerary detail compared to GDS PNRs, creating reporting asymmetry between channels that distorts yield comparisons
2.3 Validate dashboard figures against source system totals Dashboard-to-SAP revenue variance ≤0.5% before stakeholder distribution BSP settlement timing differences mean Altéa ticketing revenue and SAP AR recognised revenue diverge by up to 3 days, complicating same-day reconciliation ⚠
3.1 Analyse GDS channel yield and cost-per-booking GDS net yield ≥$0.08/RPM after distribution cost deduction GDS incentive overrides negotiated per agency are not always reflected in PRISM reporting, causing under-reporting of true distribution cost by up to 12%
3.2 Assess NDC and direct channel conversion rates NDC channel conversion rate ≥2.8%; direct web conversion ≥4.5% NDC API Gateway does not expose abandoned search data uniformly across aggregators, making full-funnel attribution incomplete for ~30% of NDC traffic
3.3 Review corporate and TMC account performance vs contract ≥90% of corporate accounts at or above 80% of contracted volume target PRISM reporting lag of 48–72 hours means corporate account shortfalls are identified too late in the quarter for proactive remediation conversations with TMCs ⚠
4.1 Analyse fare class mix and RASK by route and market Average RASK ≥$0.145; premium fare class share ≥22% of total revenue NRM O&D attribution for multi-segment itineraries uses pro-rate allocation that diverges from finance segment-level revenue, creating dual versions of RASK truth
4.2 Monitor load factor and yield trends vs prior year Load factor ≥83%; yield variance to prior year within ±5% Seasonal schedule changes (IATA SSIM S/W schedule swaps) cause route-level YoY comparisons to be distorted when new routes replace discontinued ones mid-period
4.3 Identify routes and segments below revenue performance target Zero routes with RASK <80% of target unaddressed beyond two consecutive reporting cycles Thin O&D markets with <10 daily passengers produce statistically unreliable RASK readings; automated flags generate false positives requiring manual analyst review ⚠
5.1 Produce budget variance and forecast deviation analysis Variance commentary complete for all lines deviating >±3% from budget SAP CO rolling forecast is updated monthly while sales actuals close weekly, creating a structural lag that makes mid-month variance analysis unreliable for operational decisions
5.2 Compile executive management reporting pack Management pack distributed by business day 5 of the following month Manual consolidation from multiple source reports (Tableau, SAP, PRISM) into a single Power BI pack introduces transcription risk and is a single-point-of-failure if the analyst is absent
5.3 Assess exceptions and determine escalation requirement 100% of Red-rated KPIs reviewed within 24 hours of report distribution No automated linkage between Tableau RAG alerts and calendar invites or escalation workflows; exception follow-through depends on manual distribution list hygiene ⚠
5.4 Distribute reports and archive reporting cycle artefacts 100% of reports archived within 1 business day; distribution list accuracy ≥99% No single authoritative distribution list; stale email groups cause reports to reach departed employees or miss new regional sales directors after org changes
