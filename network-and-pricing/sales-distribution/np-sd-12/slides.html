1.1 Execute metasearch partner agreement & API onboarding Partner onboarding lead time ≤30 days from contract signature Each metasearch partner requires bespoke API configurations; NDC schema versioning mismatches between partners cause silent feed failures with no error surfaced to airline
1.2 Configure fare & schedule feed for metasearch ingestion Feed configuration accuracy ≥99% (zero critical field mappings missing) ATPCO filing propagation latency (up to 24h) means published fares lag dynamic pricing changes in Amadeus NRM, creating an arbitrage window exploited by OTAs
1.3 Validate feed quality & schema compliance Zero critical schema errors before go-live; feed round-trip response ≤2s (p95) NDC 21.3 vs 17.2 schema incompatibility across metasearch partners; several comparison sites (Momondo, Kayak) accept only legacy EDIFACT/GDS feeds, forcing dual-channel maintenance ⚠
2.1 Publish fare cache to metasearch price index Fare cache refresh latency ≤15 minutes; stale fare rate <2% Fare cache staleness causes price disparity between metasearch display price and booking engine checkout; leads to consumer complaints and potential EU261/ATPCO fare filing violations
2.2 Sync real-time seat availability via NDC/GDS Availability accuracy ≥98.5% vs Altéa live booking class status GDS RBD (Reservation Booking Designator) mapping inconsistencies between Altéa and Sabre GDS cause availability discrepancies; sold-out fare classes displayed as available on Skyscanner, generating 'sorry, fare unavailable' errors at checkout
2.3 Perform fare parity audit across distribution channels Fare parity compliance ≥95% across monitored routes; exception resolution ≤4 hours Dynamic pricing adjustments in Amadeus NRM create unintentional fare disparities across channels; corporate/negotiated fares inadvertently surfaced on public metasearch breach TMC contract confidentiality clauses ⚠
3.1 Define CPC bid strategy by route and market segment Target CPC ≤$0.45 domestic routes; ≤$1.20 international routes Metasearch auction dynamics require continuous bid optimisation; static bid tables lose price competitiveness within hours during peak booking windows (holiday periods, fare sales)
3.2 Monitor click-through rates and impression share CTR ≥1.8% on primary routes; impression share ≥40% for top 20 O&D markets Metasearch partner APIs throttle reporting data refresh to 6-hour intervals; bid adjustments based on stale data reduce campaign ROI during intraday demand spikes ⚠
3.3 Adjust bids using demand signals and competitor fares ROAS (return on ad spend) ≥4.5x on metasearch channel; bid update turnaround ≤2 hours PROS RM demand signals are not natively integrated with Google Flights bid management console; manual data transfer introduces 24-hour lag, blunting competitive responsiveness
4.1 Generate deep-link redirect URL to booking engine Deep-link generation latency ≤800ms (p95); link validity ≥99.9% Session parameter passing between metasearch redirect and booking engine breaks on Safari/iOS mobile browsers due to ITP cookie restrictions; causes booking abandonment rate >35% on mobile
4.2 Track metasearch click-to-confirmed-booking conversion Metasearch-to-booking conversion ≥2.5%; cart abandonment ≤65% Cross-domain tracking gaps between metasearch redirect and airline.com booking funnel cause 20–30% attribution loss; inflates apparent cost-per-booking and understates true metasearch channel value ⚠
5.1 Aggregate metasearch channel performance data Data pipeline freshness ≤4 hours; dashboard availability ≥99.5% Each metasearch partner (Google Flights, Kayak, Skyscanner, Momondo) exposes proprietary reporting APIs with incompatible schemas; ETL pipeline maintenance requires dedicated engineering resource
5.2 Calculate cost-per-booking and channel contribution margin Metasearch cost-per-booking ≤$8.50 domestic; ≤$18.00 international Attribution of ancillary revenue (checked bags, seat upgrades, travel insurance) to the originating metasearch channel is inconsistent across reporting systems, systematically understating true channel margin contribution ⚠
6.1 Reconcile metasearch partner click invoices vs tracked clicks Invoice match rate ≥97%; dispute raised within 10 business days of invoice receipt Discrepancies between partner-reported clicks and airline-side tracking arise from differing bot/invalid-traffic filtering rules; Google Flights click audit process takes 45–90 days, creating cash flow exposure on disputed amounts ⚠
6.2 Raise formal dispute with metasearch partner for excess clicks Dispute resolution ≤30 days; credit recovery rate ≥90% of disputed amount Absence of a neutral click audit standard across metasearch platforms means dispute outcomes depend on partner goodwill; airlines with low traffic volume have limited negotiating leverage
6.3 Process approved metasearch invoices for payment Payment processed within contract terms (Net 30/Net 45); zero late payment penalties Multi-currency CPC invoices (USD, EUR, GBP) require FX rate alignment between SAP FI and partner billing systems; rate discrepancies >0.5% trigger re-approval workflows, delaying payment and risking vendor relationship
