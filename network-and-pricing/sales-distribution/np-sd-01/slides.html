Slide 3 of 3 — KPIs & Risk Register
StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Negotiate and renew GDS participation agreements Blended GDS cost per segment ≤ $8.50; contract renewal completed ≥ 60 days before expiry GDS segment fees exceed direct booking cost by 3–5x; renegotiation leverage is weak without a credible NDC alternative to threaten channel shift ⚠
1.2 Configure airline inventory and booking classes in GDS GDS profile accuracy 100%; go-live within 5 business days of agreement signing RBD misalignment between RM inventory buckets and GDS booking class display causes sell-up failures and unintended availability leakage
1.3 Validate GDS neutral display compliance Neutral display compliance 100%; exceptions resolved within 48 hours GDS display algorithm rankings can deprioritise non-alliance carriers; limited contractual recourse under IATA display rules for low-frequency routes ⚠
2.1 File published fares to ATPCO for GDS distribution Fare filing lead time ≤ 24 hours from approval; filing accuracy rate ≥ 99.5%; fares live in GDS within 6 hours of ATPCO sync ATPCO distribution windows are time-boxed (4× daily sync cycles); last-minute competitive fare responses are delayed by up to 6 hours, costing revenue on price-sensitive O&Ds
2.2 Distribute ancillary content via GDS Rich Content & Branding Ancillary attachment rate via GDS ≥ 18%; branded content display coverage ≥ 95% of GDS-connected terminals supporting RCB Many TMCs still use legacy green-screen GDS terminals with no RCB rendering capability; ancillary revenue opportunity is entirely lost for these bookings without an NDC fallback
2.3 Validate fare parity across all GDS channels Fare parity compliance ≥ 99%; parity exceptions resolved within 2 hours of detection Discrepancies between GDS fare display and website pricing trigger OTA meta-search penalties and TMC complaints; manual reconciliation across three GDSs is resource-intensive ⚠
3.1 Onboard travel agency and assign GDS PCC Agency onboarding cycle time ≤ 3 business days; zero unauthorised PCC provisioning incidents per quarter IATA agency accreditation lapses (bankruptcy, fraud, deregistration) require rapid GDS access revocation; absence of real-time IATA status feed creates compliance exposure windows ⚠
3.2 Configure corporate account discount in Sabre PRISM Corporate account setup within 5 business days of contract execution; discount application accuracy 100% PRISM corporate deal tracking requires manual reconciliation with GDS booking data; unclaimed corporate discounts cause revenue leakage and contract compliance disputes with corporates
3.3 Set up automated booking data feed to TMC back-office Data feed latency ≤ 4 hours; booking record completeness ≥ 99.5%; zero PNR data truncation incidents TMC back-office platforms require GDS-specific data field mappings; mapping errors cause incomplete trip records, failed expense reporting, and TMC SLA breaches
4.1 Monitor GDS channel booking performance and cost GDS channel share of total bookings ≤ 40%; weekly channel cost variance report delivered every Monday GDS booking data arrives with 24–48 hour lag in standard feeds; real-time channel shift detection requires a separate streaming pipeline from Amadeus Altéa PSS not available in standard GDS reports
4.2 Audit and reconcile monthly GDS segment fee invoice Invoice reconciliation accuracy ≥ 99%; GDS billing disputes filed within 30 days of invoice receipt GDS fee invoices aggregate millions of micro-transactions across three channels; manual audit is error-prone and GDS contracts impose 30–60 day dispute windows, causing irrecoverable revenue leakage on late discoveries ⚠
5.1 Identify and prioritise agencies for NDC migration NDC-enabled agencies ≥ 30% of total GDS booking volume within 12 months Large TMCs resist NDC migration due to back-office integration costs; differential incentive structures (EDIFACT vs. NDC surcharges) create agency channel conflict and risk booking diversion to competing carriers ⚠
5.2 Onboard certified agency to NDC API Gateway NDC onboarding cycle time ≤ 10 business days; NDC API error rate < 1% at go-live NDC API versioning fragmentation (IATA 17.2 vs. 21.3) creates compatibility issues; agency aggregators (Verteil, Travelfusion) introduce response latency and data field truncation that breaks ancillary flows ⚠
5.3 Monitor NDC adoption rate and distribution cost savings NDC booking share ≥ 25% of total distribution within 18 months; NDC cost per booking ≤ $2.50 vs. ≥ $8.50 for GDS NDC content gaps for group bookings, interline itineraries, and ticket reissuance force fallback to GDS for complex itineraries; true channel cost comparison is distorted by these forced GDS segments
