StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Define direct channel KPI targets and benchmarks Direct channel share of total bookings ≥ 65%; cost per direct booking ≤ $3.50 vs. GDS ≥ $8.50 No industry-standard benchmark for direct channel share forces reliance on internal year-on-year trend; low-cost carrier targets (70%+) may not be achievable without eliminating GDS completely
1.2 Select distribution schema — NDC 21.3 vs. EDIFACT NDC-capable agency coverage ≥ 40% of managed travel volume within 12 months TMC and OTA NDC readiness varies widely; dual-schema support (NDC + EDIFACT) doubles integration maintenance burden and can create fare display inconsistencies ⚠
1.3 Configure NDC API Gateway offer and order schemas API response time ≤ 800ms at p95; schema validation pass rate 100% on IATA conformance test suite NDC API latency degrades when Amadeus Altéa PSS availability calls chain through multiple O&D legs; caching strategy must balance freshness vs. response speed
1.4 Integrate Amadeus Altéa PSS real-time availability feed Availability feed uptime ≥ 99.95%; stale availability incidents < 2 per month Altéa PSS availability polling under peak traffic (holiday season surges) can cause throttling; CDN-layer availability caching introduces risk of selling closed inventory
2.1 Load fare products and ancillary catalog to web engine Fare loading accuracy 100%; catalog sync from ATPCO to web engine ≤ 4 hours after fare filing ATPCO fare filing cycle (Cat 25 / branded fare rules) requires manual QA against web display; discrepancies between filed rules and displayed conditions create regulatory and consumer protection risk
2.2 Apply dynamic pricing rules for direct channel uplift Direct channel average fare premium over GDS ≥ 0% (parity floor); promotional uplift in direct conversions ≥ 8% during campaign windows Fare parity clauses in GDS contracts prohibit offering lower fares on direct channel without also lowering GDS fares; undermines direct channel cost advantage and reduces RM flexibility
2.3 Validate fare display and regulatory compliance Zero DOT full-fare advertising violations; fare display QA pass rate 100% pre-launch DOT Part 399 all-in pricing rules require taxes and fees displayed at first point of fare quotation; dynamic fee structures (carrier-imposed surcharges) change frequently and must be synced in real time ⚠
3.1 Monitor booking funnel conversion metrics in real time Overall booking conversion rate ≥ 3.5% (search-to-book); mobile app conversion ≥ 4.2%; funnel abandonment at payment ≤ 18% Session stitching across device and login state is unreliable without a persistent customer ID; anonymous user journeys cannot be attributed to downstream revenue, skewing funnel analysis ⚠
3.2 Run A/B tests on booking flow UX variants A/B test cycle time ≤ 3 weeks; conversion lift from winning variants ≥ 0.5% per test cycle Low-frequency route markets have insufficient traffic to reach A/B test statistical significance within acceptable test windows, forcing pooled analysis that dilutes route-specific behavioural insights ⚠
3.3 Optimise ancillary upsell prompts at checkout Ancillary attach rate ≥ 45% of direct bookings; average ancillary revenue per direct booking ≥ $28 Overly aggressive ancillary prompts increase checkout friction and raise abandonment; tension between ancillary revenue maximisation and conversion rate requires continual tuning of prompt timing and frequency
4.1 Submit payment to CyberSource gateway and tokenise card Payment authorisation success rate ≥ 97%; 3DS2 frictionless flow rate ≥ 85%; gateway downtime ≤ 0.05% monthly 3-D Secure challenge flows increase checkout abandonment by 12–18%; SCA (Strong Customer Authentication) mandates in EU/UK apply when serving international routes and require issuer cooperation beyond airline control ⚠
4.2 Screen transaction for fraud risk with Accertify Fraud chargeback rate ≤ 0.05% of transaction value; false positive decline rate ≤ 0.8%; manual review queue cleared within 2 hours Flight booking fraud patterns (mule accounts, card-not-present abuse on international routes) evolve faster than rule-update cycles; model retraining lag creates a window of elevated fraud exposure during new attack patterns ⚠
4.3 Confirm booking and issue PNR in Amadeus Altéa PSS PNR creation success rate ≥ 99.9%; confirmation email delivery within 60 seconds of booking; seat inventory decremented correctly 100% Race conditions between simultaneous bookings on the last seat in an RBD bucket can cause duplicate sells before Altéa inventory lock propagates; requires seat re-offer logic and customer compensation workflow
5.1 Accrue Rapid Rewards miles for direct booking Miles posting accuracy 100%; accrual posted within 72 hours of booking; direct channel bonus miles correctly applied ≥ 99.5% of eligible transactions Miles accrual rules for ancillary spend (seat upgrades, bags) are inconsistently applied when ancillaries are added post-booking via app; loyalty platform integration requires separate API call that can fail silently
5.2 Generate direct channel performance dashboard Dashboard refresh latency ≤ 4 hours; direct channel share reported to VP-level weekly; data completeness ≥ 99% Channel attribution is contested between direct and indirect when customers research on GDS/OTA then book direct; last-click attribution overstates direct channel contribution and misguides media spend decisions
5.3 Review direct channel share vs. target and escalate gaps Direct channel share ≥ 65%; month-over-month share growth ≥ 0.5pp when below target; corrective action plan issued within 5 business days of confirmed underperformance Structural direct channel share ceiling exists for airlines with large managed travel / TMC-dependent corporate customer base; share targets set for leisure mix may not apply to corporate-heavy routes without a distinct corporate portal strategy ⚠
