StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Receive and log charter inquiry in CRM Inquiry-to-quote response time ≤4 hours for Group >50 pax Unstructured enquiry intake (email/phone) causes data loss and delays; no dedicated charter request portal integrated with Salesforce
1.2 Validate client credit standing and compliance Credit check completed within 2 hours of inquiry receipt Manual credit verification via third-party tools not embedded in Salesforce; repeat credit checks for known clients due to lack of CRM integration ⚠
1.3 Check aircraft and crew availability in schedule Availability check completed within 1 business hour; fleet utilisation conflict rate <5% Charter requests compete with scheduled service rotations; SkyWORKS schedule locks can prevent real-time availability reads during peak planning cycles ⚠
2.1 Calculate block-hour cost and fuel uplift estimate Cost model accuracy vs actuals ≤5% variance on completed charters Amadeus NRM is O&D-optimised for scheduled service; charter block-hour cost models are maintained in offline Excel workbooks outside the RM system, creating version control risk
2.2 Assess displaced scheduled revenue opportunity cost Charter net margin ≥15% above displaced scheduled service yield Displaced revenue calculation requires manual extraction of RM forecast data; no automated what-if tool to compare charter vs scheduled revenue in real time
2.3 Obtain Revenue Management approval for charter pricing RM approval turnaround ≤4 hours; pricing approval SLA breach rate <10% No formal workflow routing for charter pricing approvals in Amadeus NRM; approvals handled via email, creating audit trail gaps for regulatory and financial compliance ⚠
2.4 Build and transmit formal charter proposal to client Proposal issued within 6 hours of RM approval; proposal acceptance rate ≥40% Proposal templates are maintained outside Salesforce; manual formatting increases turnaround time and introduces inconsistency across sales reps
3.1 Negotiate charter terms and revise pricing if needed Negotiation rounds ≤3 before agreement or rejection; average deal close time ≤5 business days Negotiation iterations not tracked in CRM opportunity stages; pricing concessions granted below floor price without RM visibility due to lack of automated guardrails ⚠
3.2 Generate charter contract from approved terms Contract execution within 24 hours of verbal agreement; contract error rate <2% DocuSign not integrated with Salesforce CRM; contract data entry is manual, risking discrepancies between CRM opportunity record and signed contract terms ⚠
3.3 Collect deposit payment and record in receivables Deposit received within 5 business days of contract execution; deposit collection rate ≥95% No automated dunning workflow for charter deposit collection in SAP; manual follow-up required, and overdue deposits are not automatically flagged in Salesforce CRM ⚠
4.1 Apply for overflight and landing permits Permit applications filed ≥10 days before departure for international routes; permit clearance rate ≥98% Permit processing times vary by country (24 hours to 10+ days); no centralised permit tracking system — status monitored via email chains with handling agents, risking missed deadlines ⚠
4.2 File flight plan and request airport slots Slot confirmed ≥5 days before departure; slot change rate <10% post-confirmation Charter flights at slot-coordinated airports (IATA Level 3) compete with scheduled carriers; last-minute charter requests are often rejected, requiring costly alternative routing
4.3 Assign crew pairing and verify duty-time compliance Crew assigned ≥72 hours before departure; FAR Part 117 violation rate = 0% Charter dates often conflict with scheduled crew pairings; Jeppesen requires manual pairing creation for ad-hoc charters, and duty-time buffers may be exhausted if schedule disruptions precede the charter ⚠
4.4 Coordinate fuel uplift, catering, and ground handling Ground handling confirmed ≥48 hours before departure; catering order accuracy ≥98% Charter stations may be uncontracted airports; negotiating ad-hoc fuel and handling rates increases cost unpredictability and erodes the charter margin estimated in Phase 2
5.1 Execute charter flight and record departure/arrival actuals Charter on-time departure (D0) ≥85%; pax manifest discrepancy rate <0.5% Charter pax manifests are often finalised late by clients; last-minute pax additions or removals require real-time weight & balance recalculations, delaying departure
5.2 Capture actual costs and reconcile against estimate Cost reconciliation completed within 5 business days of flight; cost variance ≤8% of estimate Fuel and handling invoices from ad-hoc stations arrive weeks after the flight; SAP posting cutoffs create accrual uncertainty, requiring manual re-opening of periods for charter settlements ⚠
5.3 Issue final invoice and collect balance payment Final invoice issued within 7 business days of flight; payment collected within contract terms (net 14 days); DSO for charter ≤21 days Client disputes over fuel surcharges or extras delay final payment; no automated dispute escalation workflow in SAP, requiring manual intervention by Finance and Sales teams ⚠
6.1 Conduct post-charter debrief and update client record Debrief completed within 10 business days of flight; repeat charter conversion rate ≥30% Post-charter learnings (pricing accuracy, permit delays, ground handling issues) are not systematically fed back into pricing models or permit planning checklists, causing repeated errors
