StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Validate Interline Ticketing Agreement status 100% of active partners with valid ITA before ticket issuance enabled MITA agreements lapse without automated renewal alerts — expired ITAs cause ticket rejection at GDS, creating passenger disruption and manual escalation to partner airline's commercial team ⚠
1.2 Load partner carrier tariff and fare rules Tariff load completed within 24 hours of ATPCO effective date ATPCO batch updates publish at 00:01 EST — late-night automation failures leave stale fare rules in production until manual intervention, causing mispriced interline itineraries
1.3 Configure MITA SSR message formats in PSS SSR message delivery success rate ≥99.5% EDIFACT PADIS message version mismatches between carriers require manual mapping tables — each new partner requires 2–4 weeks of IT configuration, delaying commercial go-live
2.1 Register codeshare flight designator in Amadeus Altéa Codeshare schedule loaded ≥90 days before first departure date Operating carrier schedule changes propagate with latency — SkyWORKS SSIM refresh cycle is 24 hours, meaning same-day schedule swaps create ghost codeshare flights visible in GDS
2.2 Sync partner inventory blocks via EDIFACT SSR/ACK Inventory sync acknowledgement (ACK) received within 15 minutes of block request Dual inventory control (marketing vs operating carrier) creates overbooking risk — NRM cannot directly query operating carrier's live seat map, relying on asynchronous SSR messages with no guaranteed delivery SLA ⚠
2.3 File codeshare fares via ATPCO and validate combinability Zero misfiled combinability rules causing GDS pricing errors at launch ATPCO Category 10 combinability rules are complex — errors in end-on-end fare construction go undetected until passengers receive incorrect fares at booking, requiring mass repricing and IROPS-level agent intervention
3.1 Receive interline booking request from GDS or NDC GDS booking response time ≤3 seconds for interline itineraries NDC-sourced interline bookings lack standardised offer/order IDs compatible with legacy EDIFACT settlement — carriers without full NDC back-office integration cannot receive or settle NDC-issued interline tickets
3.2 Perform fare construction combinability check Fare construction error rate ≤0.5% of all interline transactions Half-round-trip (HRT) and end-on-end combination logic in Altéa pricing engine has known edge cases for 3+ carrier itineraries — manual audit required for itineraries crossing IATA traffic conference areas ⚠
3.3 Issue interline e-ticket via BSP or ARC Ticket issuance success rate ≥99.8%; time-to-issue ≤60 seconds BSP settlement reporting requires per-coupon carrier code validation — system-generated document check digit errors in Altéa have caused bulk ticket voids requiring 48-hour manual reissue queues
3.4 Transmit PNR and coupon data to operating carrier PNR transmission to operating carrier within 5 minutes of ticket issuance; delivery confirmation ≥99% IATCI (Interline Through Check-In) handoff failures occur when operating carrier's PSS is on a different version of the EDIFACT PADIS standard — passengers arrive at operating carrier's check-in counter with no booking record
4.1 Calculate revenue proration using IATA Proration rules Proration calculation completed within T+1 business day of travel date IATA Proration Rules Manual (PRM) updates twice yearly — revenue accounting system requires manual rule table updates, and delayed updates cause incorrect proration splits that take months to detect via interline billing reconciliation
4.2 Generate and submit interline billing memo via IATA CASS Billing memo submission before monthly IATA ICH cutoff (25th of month); dispute rate ≤2% ICH submission deadlines are fixed — revenue accounting system outages during the last 3 business days of the month result in missed cutoffs, deferring settlement by a full month and creating cash flow exposure
4.3 Reconcile and approve interline billing statements Reconciliation completed within 10 business days of billing statement receipt; dispute resolution ≤30 days Coupon usage data gaps (e.g., missing lift coupons from operating carrier) prevent automated matching — manual reconciliation for unmatched coupons exceeds 200 hours/month at mid-size carriers ⚠
4.4 Post approved proration revenue to general ledger GL posting completed within 2 business days of billing approval; zero unposted items at month-end close Interline revenue accounting system integration with SAP FI relies on nightly batch file transfer — intra-month adjustments require manual journal entries, increasing month-end close effort and audit trail risk
5.1 Detect schedule disruption affecting interline itinerary Disruption notification to marketing carrier within 30 minutes of operating carrier declaring IROPS Operating carrier IROPS notifications arrive via unstructured email or telephone — no automated EDIFACT OC (Other Communication) message standard exists, meaning marketing carrier agents learn of disruptions reactively when passengers call ⚠
5.2 Reroute passenger on alternate interline or codeshare flight Passenger reprotection offer presented within 60 minutes of IROPS declaration; acceptance rate ≥85% IATA Resolution 735d mandates specific rebooking rules for involuntary rerouting — agents are not always trained on which rules apply to each interline agreement, leading to incorrect fare differences being charged or waived
5.3 Process interline ticket reissue or refund Reissue or refund processing completed within 24 hours of passenger request; ADM dispute rate ≤1% BSP Agent Debit Memos (ADMs) for incorrectly waived change fees create disputes lasting 6–12 months — interline reissue waiver codes are not standardised across carriers, causing downstream billing errors that inflate interline dispute volumes
