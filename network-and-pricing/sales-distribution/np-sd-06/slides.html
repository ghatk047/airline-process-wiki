StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Receive group travel request via agency or portal Request acknowledgement within 4 business hours; ≥95% SLA adherence Group requests arriving via email bypass PSS, creating untracked shadow bookings outside Altéa Groups
1.2 Qualify group size and policy eligibility 100% of groups below policy threshold redirected within 1 business hour Inconsistent group-size thresholds across markets (10 pax domestic vs. 15 pax international) cause misrouting ⚠
1.3 Check flight seat availability for group block Seat availability response time <30 seconds; provisional hold valid for 72 hours Provisional holds consume open inventory without commitment, degrading yield; no automated hold expiry in Altéa Groups without manual follow-up
2.1 Calculate group fare and applicable discount tier Fare calculation accuracy 100%; group fare ≥85% of equivalent published Y/B/M fare ATPCO group fare rule updates (filed twice per IATA season) can invalidate in-progress quotes; agents must manually cross-check effective dates
2.2 Apply contracted agency or corporate override discount Contract compliance rate ≥98%; override correctly applied to all contracted accounts Sabre PRISM contract data can be out of sync with Altéa fare filing if corporate account team updates rates bilaterally outside PRISM
2.3 Assess yield impact against revenue plan Group segment revenue contribution ≥ displacement cost; load factor impact <3 pp on high-demand flights NRM displacement cost models use O&D demand curves that may undervalue high-yield late-booking individual traffic displaced by early group blocks ⚠
2.4 Negotiate revised terms if yield threshold not met Negotiation-to-acceptance rate ≥60% of referred cases; cycle time ≤2 business days No structured counter-offer workflow in Altéa Groups — negotiation conducted off-system via email, losing audit trail
2.5 Issue formal group quotation to agency Quote issued within 24 hours of pricing approval; quote acceptance rate ≥70% Quote documents generated outside Altéa as manual PDFs; version control errors lead to agency booking on superseded fare terms
3.1 Execute group contract and capture deposit obligation Contract creation within 4 hours of acceptance; 100% of deposit deadlines captured in PSS Altéa Groups does not send automated deposit due-date reminders to agencies; agents must maintain manual chase calendars
3.2 Confirm deposit receipt and activate group booking Deposit-to-confirmation cycle ≤1 business day; 100% deposit reconciliation with SAP AR BSP/ARC settlement remittance data arrives with 24–48 hour lag, delaying booking confirmation and creating inventory limbo ⚠
3.3 Release unconfirmed seat block if deposit overdue 100% of overdue blocks released within 2 hours of deadline; zero revenue leakage from zombie holds Manual release process dependent on agent availability; after-hours expirations accumulate until next business day, blocking revenue optimisation
4.1 Collect passenger name list from agency PNL submission by T−21 days; name completion rate ≥90% by T−14 days Agencies frequently submit names in non-standard formats requiring manual re-keying into Altéa; APIS-compliant data (passport, DOB) often missing at initial submission
4.2 Validate name count against contracted seat block Name-count discrepancy <5% of group size; chase cycle completed within 48 hours of deadline Groups with >10% shortfall after T−7 days create difficult trade-off between releasing revenue-generating seats and maintaining client relationship ⚠
4.3 Process name changes within contractual tolerance Name changes processed within 4 hours of request; change fee collection rate 100% Post-ticketing name changes require ticket reissue via ATPCO exchange rules; agents unfamiliar with involuntary vs. voluntary reissue procedures generate MCO errors ⚠
5.1 Trigger final ticketing and collect balance payment 100% ticket issuance by T−5 days; balance collection within 48 hours of ticketing Ticketing queue backlogs during peak season (summer, holidays) cause missed deadlines; auto-ticketing rules in Altéa are not universally configured for group bookings ⚠
5.2 Cancel non-ticketed seats and release to inventory Seat release within 2 hours of deadline; revenue recovery via reopen sale ≥80% of released seats Last-minute seat release on high-load flights can trigger yield recalculation in NRM, causing overbooking alerts that require manual adjudication
5.3 Reconcile group revenue against sales forecast Revenue variance within ±5% of group forecast; report delivered within 3 business days of departure NRM revenue accounting and Altéa ticketing revenue figures frequently diverge due to currency rounding and BSP reporting timing differences; manual reconciliation required
5.4 Update group sales KPI dashboard and close record Group conversion rate ≥65%; average group booking lead time ≥45 days; change-fee revenue tracked monthly Tableau dashboard relies on Altéa data extracts with T+1 latency; real-time group sales performance is not visible to management during the booking window
