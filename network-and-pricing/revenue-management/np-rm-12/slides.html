StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Load authorized capacity & fleet config Capacity load accuracy 100% before sales open Late fleet swaps from network planning trigger manual capacity corrections in AltéaRM; no automated re-sync with SkyMAX fleet assignment module
1.2 Initialize fare class booking buckets All booking classes live ≥14 days before departure; 0 missing bucket errors at booking open ATPCO fare class proliferation (20+ classes on competitive routes) increases bucket management complexity; AltéaRM UI does not support bulk bucket edits
1.3 Apply overbooking factors by flight segment Target involuntary denied boarding rate ≤0.05 per 10,000 passengers No-show model degrades on routes with recent schedule changes or post-event demand spikes; manual recalibration required in AltéaRM — no auto-retraining trigger ⚠
2.1 Monitor real-time booking pace vs forecast Pace variance alert triggered within 15 minutes of threshold breach AltéaRM polling interval from Altéa PSS is near-real-time but not event-driven; sudden group bookings or flash sales can spike pace index before alert fires
2.2 Evaluate booking pace deviation threshold System recommendation generated within 5 minutes of threshold breach; analyst review SLA ≤30 min Threshold calibration is static per route cluster — unusual demand patterns (e.g. redirected traffic, airline irregular operations) generate false positive alerts requiring analyst intervention ⚠
2.3 Flag flight for priority availability review ≥90% of flagged flights actioned within analyst SLA; <5% false-positive escalation rate High-frequency flagging on congested departure banks (e.g. Monday AM banks) overwhelms analyst queues; PROS RM AI prioritisation not yet integrated at this carrier
3.1 Generate availability adjustment recommendation Revenue uplift from accepted recommendations ≥1.5% per adjusted flight vs control group AltéaRM recommendations do not factor competitor real-time availability in Sabre/Travelport GDS; ATPCO fare data lags competitor filing by up to 2 hours
3.2 Validate recommendation against RM policy bounds Policy override rate ≤8% of system recommendations; override reason coded for feedback loop RM policy rules are maintained in AltéaRM configuration tables — change request lead time is 5 business days, creating misalignment during rapid competitive responses ⚠
3.3 Apply manual override with documented reason Manual override revenue outcome tracked: ≥60% of overrides must outperform system recommendation at T+7 days post-departure Override audit trail in AltéaRM is flight-level only — no automatic post-departure outcome attribution; manual tracking in spreadsheets introduces error
3.4 Broadcast availability updates to all channels Availability propagation to all GDS channels within 60 seconds of AltéaRM commit; NDC API latency ≤5 seconds Sabre and Travelport GDS availability caching can delay display of closed classes by up to 90 seconds — undercuts intended revenue protection window on high-demand flights
4.1 Receive and assess group booking request Group quote turnaround ≤4 hours for ≤50 pax; ≤24 hours for >50 pax Group bookings bypass normal availability controls in Altéa PSS — large groups (>30 pax) can displace higher-yield individual bookings if allotment limits not enforced in real time
4.2 Validate group request against allotment policy Group allotment approval rate within policy: ≥95% handled without senior escalation; group revenue mix target ≤20% of total flight revenue AltéaRM group allotment module requires manual entry of allotment limits per flight — no automated inheritance from network-level group policy, causing inconsistency across the system ⚠
4.3 Confirm group block and adjust net inventory Group block confirmation rate ≥85% within quoted hold period; no-show rate on confirmed groups ≤12% Group blocks held without deposit reduce effective inventory for individual revenue-maximising bookings — Altéa PSS release rules must be manually configured per group record
5.1 Conduct T-72h pre-departure inventory review Final load factor target ≥83%; revenue per available seat kilometre (RASK) vs budget reviewed at T-72h T-72h review is manual and sequential per flight — on banks of 80+ departures, analyst capacity limits force triage; lower-revenue routes reviewed last or skipped entirely
5.2 Evaluate unsold inventory against threshold Unsold seat recovery via last-minute availability opening: target ≥65% of exposed seats sold at ≥breakeven yield Static threshold rules for opening lower classes do not account for connecting passenger itinerary value — opening cheap last-minute classes may dilute O&D revenue on competitive routes ⚠
5.3 Open lower fare classes and clear waitlist Waitlist clearance rate ≥90% within 2 hours of class opening; confirmed waitlist pax CSAT ≥4.2/5 Waitlist auto-confirmation in Altéa PSS sends immediate ticketing deadline — passengers who do not respond within 2 hours forfeit the seat, requiring manual re-processing by reservations agents
5.4 Freeze availability at check-in cutoff Check-in cutoff applied accurately on 100% of departures; overbooking resolution (VDB/IDB) initiated within 30 minutes of cutoff if overbooked Irregular operations (IROPS) delay departures after availability freeze — stranded passengers rebooked on alternate flights consume inventory already frozen for those flights, requiring emergency availability unlocks in Altéa PSS
