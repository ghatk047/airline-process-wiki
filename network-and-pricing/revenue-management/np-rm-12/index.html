Airline-Specific Risks & Pain Points

Late fleet swaps from network planning trigger manual capacity corrections in AltéaRM; no automated re-sync with SkyMAX fleet assignment module
ATPCO fare class proliferation (20+ classes on competitive routes) increases bucket management complexity; AltéaRM UI does not support bulk bucket edits
No-show model degrades on routes with recent schedule changes or post-event demand spikes; manual recalibration required in AltéaRM — no auto-retraining trigger
AltéaRM polling interval from Altéa PSS is near-real-time but not event-driven; sudden group bookings or flash sales can spike pace index before alert fires
Threshold calibration is static per route cluster — unusual demand patterns (e.g. redirected traffic, airline irregular operations) generate false positive alerts requiring analyst intervention
High-frequency flagging on congested departure banks (e.g. Monday AM banks) overwhelms analyst queues; PROS RM AI prioritisation not yet integrated at this carrier
