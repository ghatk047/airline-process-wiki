StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Extract RM forecast accuracy metrics Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) ≤15% across all O&D markets Amadeus NRM MAPE reporting requires custom extract scripts; no native scheduled accuracy dashboard, increasing analyst effort and risk of metric inconsistency
1.2 Identify underperforming O&D markets ≥90% of O&D markets within MAPE threshold within 30 days of calibration cycle Cross-market comparison requires manual joins between Amadeus NRM outputs and AWS Redshift actuals; data latency of up to 24 hrs can mask intra-day booking pattern shifts
1.3 Assess booking curve deviations from expected pickup Booking curve R² ≥0.92 across flagged markets; decision to proceed with recalibration if ≥3 markets fail threshold Structural demand shifts post-COVID (early leisure surge, late corporate recovery) have permanently altered booking curves; NRM baseline curves calibrated pre-2023 may no longer reflect current market behaviour ⚠
2.1 Extract historical booking and ticketing data Dataset completeness ≥98% of scheduled departures in scope; extract runtime <2 hours AWS Redshift query performance degrades on unpartitioned historical tables; full 24-month O&D extracts for 100+ markets can exceed 4-hour SLA without query optimisation
2.2 Validate input data quality and completeness Data quality score ≥95% (null rate <2%, duplicate bookings <0.5%, fare outliers <1%) Amadeus Altéa PSS ticket voids and exchanges frequently generate duplicate or partial booking records that inflate cancellation rate inputs to the NRM demand model, distorting recalibration outputs ⚠
2.3 Load competitive fare and capacity benchmarks Competitive data coverage ≥85% of target O&D markets; data freshness within 48 hours of filing date ATPCO fare data covers only filed public fares; negotiated corporate and NDC-only fares are not visible, creating a systematic blind spot in price elasticity model inputs for corporate-heavy routes
3.1 Recalibrate demand forecast model coefficients Post-recalibration MAPE improvement ≥10% vs. baseline on hold-out validation set Amadeus NRM parameter update interface requires batch file upload in proprietary format; no API-driven calibration workflow, increasing manual error risk and extending cycle time to 3–5 business days
3.2 Update price elasticity and WTP model curves Bid price accuracy: simulated revenue within ±2% of optimal on hold-out markets; elasticity curve R² ≥0.88 WTP segmentation in Amadeus NRM is limited to cabin-level; lack of sub-segment granularity (e.g. loyalty tier, corporate account) reduces model precision for high-value O&D pairs with mixed traffic composition
3.3 Tune overbooking safety factors by route type Denied boarding rate ≤0.05 per 1,000 passengers; seat spoilage rate ≤2% of available seats Denied boarding compensation exposure under EU261/2004 and DOT rules creates asymmetric financial risk; NRM overbooking model does not natively incorporate regulatory compensation cost as an optimisation constraint ⚠
4.1 Execute shadow-mode simulation on hold-out period Shadow simulation runtime ≤8 hours; simulated revenue variance vs. actuals ≤2% Amadeus NRM shadow mode does not replicate real-time competitor price reactions; static competitor assumptions in simulation overstate revenue uplift potential by an estimated 0.5–1.0% on competitive routes
4.2 Evaluate shadow revenue vs. actual revenue Simulated revenue uplift ≥1.0% vs. actuals across all market clusters; no individual cluster showing revenue degradation >0.5% Revenue attribution between calibration changes and external demand factors (events, competitor capacity changes) is not automated; manual attribution analysis adds 1–2 days to the validation cycle
4.3 Run live A/B test on selected pilot markets A/B revenue uplift ≥1.5% in pilot markets over 4-week test window at 95% statistical confidence Selecting statistically valid control markets is difficult on thin-route network where few true analogues exist; contamination risk between test and control markets sharing connections distorts A/B results ⚠
5.1 Stage calibrated parameters in UAT environment UAT regression test pass rate 100% on critical path; deployment staging completed within 1 business day Amadeus NRM UAT environment is shared with pricing and ancillary revenue teams; uncoordinated deployments from parallel projects create parameter conflicts that require vendor support engagement to resolve
5.2 Present calibration results for RM leadership sign-off Sign-off decision turnaround ≤2 business days; zero production deployments without documented approval Calibration approval process is undocumented in most carriers; absence of a formal change control board for RM model changes means deployments can proceed without revenue risk assessment, creating audit exposure ⚠
5.3 Deploy calibrated parameters to production NRM Production deployment completed within 4-hour maintenance window; zero unplanned system downtime during deployment Amadeus NRM production parameter updates require a maintenance window coordinated with Amadeus support; last-minute scheduling conflicts with Altéa PSS maintenance windows have caused deployment delays of up to 72 hours
5.4 Monitor live KPIs post-deployment RASK improvement ≥1.0% vs. pre-calibration baseline within 30 days; no market showing load factor decline >2 percentage points Post-deployment monitoring relies on manual Tableau dashboard review; absence of automated anomaly detection means revenue degradation from a miscalibrated market cluster may go undetected for 3–5 days, compounding financial impact
