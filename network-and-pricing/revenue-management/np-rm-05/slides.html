Slide 3 of 3 — KPIs & Risk Register
StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Receive Group Booking Request Request acknowledgement within 4 business hours; ≥95% SLA compliance Requests arriving via multiple channels (email, GDS PNR, SWABIZ) require manual consolidation into Altéa — no single intake API across all channels
1.2 Validate Group Size Eligibility 100% of sub-threshold requests correctly redirected to retail within 1 business hour Groups splitting into sub-10 clusters to access retail fares while exploiting group seat release policies — requires cross-PNR duplicate-name detection ⚠
1.3 Check Blackout & Peak Period Restrictions Blackout date check completed in <10 minutes; alternate date acceptance rate ≥40% Peak calendars maintained manually in NRM and not automatically synced with marketing/events calendar — agents may quote blocked dates before checking ⚠
1.4 Confirm O&D, Routing & Itinerary Itinerary confirmed and filed in PSS within 2 hours of initial request Multi-segment itineraries require manual linking of PNRs across codeshare or interline partners — slow process for international group bookings
2.1 Query Real-Time O&D Inventory Availability Inventory query response in <30 seconds; data freshness lag ≤5 minutes NRM availability data can lag real-time PSS inventory by up to 5 minutes during high-volume periods — group blocks booked on stale data risk overbooking
2.2 Run Displacement Cost Analysis Displacement model accuracy ≥88% vs. actual realised revenue; analysis completed in <15 minutes Displacement models rely on NRM booking curves that may not fully account for last-minute premium fare uplift potential — groups in high-demand markets routinely under-priced
2.3 Assess Net Revenue Impact of Group Block Group acceptance rate ≥65% of qualified requests; average group yield ≥85% of displacement cost Sales teams operate under volume targets that incentivise accepting revenue-negative groups — tension between group sales quota and RM yield discipline ⚠
3.1 Calculate Block Fare Using Group Pricing Model Group fare ≥95% of minimum acceptable yield threshold; pricing turnaround ≤30 minutes Group pricing model does not natively integrate with ATPCO filed fares — group quotes are manual overrides that must be cross-checked against published fare rules to avoid BSP/ARC audit exceptions
3.2 Validate Fare Against Discount Authority Matrix 100% of quotes validated against authority matrix before issue; zero out-of-authority quotes issued Discount authority matrices are updated quarterly in spreadsheets and manually imported into NRM — stale thresholds lead to incorrect authority-level routing ⚠
3.3 Generate Formal Group Quote Document Quote issued within 24 hours of request receipt; quote acceptance rate ≥55% Quote templates are maintained outside PSS in Word/PDF — manual population risks errors in fare amounts, deadlines, and contract terms; no audit trail within the booking system
4.1 Conduct Sales Negotiation with Group Organiser Negotiation cycle ≤3 rounds to final agreement; average concession depth ≤5% below initial quote Sales managers lack real-time competitive fare visibility during negotiation — competitors' group rates not available in PRISM, forcing reliance on OAG or manual fare searches ⚠
4.2 Obtain Final RM Approval for Confirmed Terms Approval response within 4 business hours; ≤10% of groups requiring director-level escalation Approval workflow is email-based with no system-enforced audit trail — SOX compliance reviews frequently flag missing RM sign-offs on large group bookings ⚠
4.3 Confirm Group Booking & Collect Deposit Deposit collection rate ≥98% of confirmed bookings; booking confirmation issued within 2 hours of receipt of signed contract Deposit processing via bank transfer requires manual reconciliation against PNR in SAP S/4HANA Finance — 2-3 day lag between payment receipt and booking confirmation causes organiser escalations
5.1 Create Seat Block in PSS Inventory Seat block created within 1 hour of booking confirmation; zero inventory discrepancy vs. PNR Hard blocks in Altéa reduce sellable capacity visible to GDS channels immediately — premature blocking of large groups in advance of name submission increases unsold seat risk
5.2 Monitor Name Release & Ticketing Deadlines ≥90% of names submitted by first release deadline; ticketing deadline breach rate <5% PSS deadline alerts are not integrated with CRM or email workflows — coordinators manually track deadlines in spreadsheets, leading to missed release dates and last-minute seat block management failures ⚠
5.3 Release Unsold Seats to Open Inventory Unsold seats released ≥72 hours before departure; recaptured revenue from released seats ≥60% of displacement cost on released units Late seat release (within 72 hours of departure) significantly reduces recapture probability — NRM booking curves flatten below T-3 days, leaving released group seats unsold and generating direct revenue loss ⚠
6.1 Process Final Passenger Manifest & Settlement Final settlement completed within 5 business days post-travel; payment reconciliation accuracy ≥99.5% Name changes and no-shows on group bookings trigger penalty fee calculations that must be manually computed and re-invoiced — no automated penalty engine in Altéa for group contracts
6.2 Calculate Revenue Variance vs. Forecast Forecast vs. actual variance ≤8%; group contribution to total flight revenue ≥12% on applicable routes Revenue data from SAP FI and NRM actuals must be joined manually in Redshift — no automated group P&L dashboard; variance analysis delayed by 2-3 days post-travel
6.3 Update RM Demand Forecasting Model with Actuals Model update completed within 10 business days post-travel; forecast accuracy improvement of ≥0.5% per cycle NRM group booking actuals are not automatically tagged separately from retail bookings — group demand is blended into overall O&D forecasts, distorting unconstrained demand estimates and causing systematic over-forecasting on high-group-volume routes
