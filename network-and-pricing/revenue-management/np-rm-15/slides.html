StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Ingest LCC published fares from ATPCO feed Fare update latency ≤4 hours from ATPCO filing; coverage ≥80% of LCC-filed fares LCC carriers increasingly use direct-channel-only fares not filed via ATPCO; ACI feed misses 20–35% of actual LCC selling prices on contested routes
1.2 Scrape LCC direct-channel fares via fare shopping Shopping coverage ≥95% of LCC-overlap routes completed within 24 hours; intraday refresh on routes with >50 weekly LCC departures LCC carriers revise fares multiple times per day; bot-detection mechanisms on Spirit and Frontier websites block automated scraping, requiring manual supplementation that introduces 12–24h lag
1.3 Flag routes exceeding LCC penetration threshold 100% of routes with LCC market share >25% flagged and tier-assigned within 48 hours of data refresh MIDT-sourced market share data lags 60–90 days; real-time share is modelled, not measured, creating tier misclassification risk on recently-entered LCC markets ⚠
2.1 Calculate fully-loaded fare differential vs LCC Fully-loaded fare gap calculated within 2 hours of trigger event; model accuracy ±5% vs actual LCC checkout price LCC ancillary fee schedules are not available via any standardised data feed and change without notice; manual fee updates lag by up to one week, overstating or understating the real passenger cost differential
2.2 Assess LCC seat capacity deployment on overlap routes Capacity share delta vs prior 4-week period reported weekly; >10% capacity increase by any LCC triggers immediate alert LCC carriers file schedule placeholders with inflated frequencies to hold airport slot positions; published seats overstate committed capacity by up to 15%, inflating perceived competitive threat and driving unnecessary yield dilution responses
2.3 Classify route competitive response mode 100% of High-tier LCC routes assigned response mode within 72 hours; response mode reviewed every 21 days Differentiation strategy (premium service, schedule convenience) is difficult to sustain on short-haul routes under 500 miles where LCC price advantage exceeds perceived product differential — risks revenue dilution without stimulation benefit materialising ⚠
3.1 Define route fare floor against unit cost recovery Fare floor covers variable cost per seat at target load factor ≥100% on 100% of routes; floor breach rate <2% of all filed competitive fares Short-haul CASM on thin regional routes can exceed the LCC's total all-in fare — matching requires contribution-negative seat sales; VP Revenue sign-off required for any floor breach, creating decision bottleneck during fast-moving LCC fare wars ⚠
3.2 Construct ATPCO fare basis codes and rule conditions Fare rule filing completeness ≥98% (zero missing ATPCO categories); internal peer review completed within 4 hours of draft creation ATPCO Category 15 (Sales Restrictions) mis-filings create GDS display errors that misdirect competitive fares to wrong markets or channels; full audit trail mandatory on all competitive-response fares to support regulatory review
3.3 Configure NRM inventory availability and bid-price thresholds Inventory control update applied within 2 hours of fare approval; AU accuracy ±2 seats vs modelled optimum at D-90 NRM bid-price recalibration for competitive fares risks cannibalising higher-yield bookings if LCC-stimulated demand does not materialise as forecast; demand signal validation required before activation on routes with >60% business-travel mix
3.4 Apply channel-specific fare availability configuration NDC-exclusive fares available within 4 hours of ATPCO filing; GDS display parity ≥95% across Sabre, Travelport, and Amadeus within 8 hours NDC fare surfacing in Sabre and Travelport requires bilateral API certification agreements not yet in place with all TMC partners; competitive fares may be invisible to managed-travel corporate buyers, ceding LCC-contested bookings to direct channels only ⚠
4.1 File competitive fare in ATPCO and validate GDS mirror Fare live in all three GDS within 8 hours of ATPCO filing; zero Category 15 validation errors; filing confirmed in ATPCO audit log ATPCO batch processing runs only three times daily (0200, 1000, 1800 ET); up to 8 hours may elapse before GDS visibility is restored, during which the LCC has already captured price-sensitive bookings that cannot be recovered
4.2 Activate competitive fare in Amadeus Altéa PSS PSS activation within 1 hour of ATPCO confirmation; zero inventory sync discrepancies between Altéa and NRM authorization levels PSS-NRM inventory sync misalignment during high-traffic booking periods can result in seats sold below NRM bid price; monitored via NRM exception queue with 30-minute reconciliation cycle during peak booking windows
4.3 Issue pricing action bulletin to sales and accounts Bulletin issued within 2 hours of fare activation; 100% of key account managers and top-50 agency contacts notified; read-receipt tracked in Salesforce Corporate account managers frequently learn of competitive price changes from TMC partners before receiving internal notification, creating a trust deficit and triggering premature contract renegotiation requests that consume revenue management bandwidth
5.1 Monitor booking velocity on LCC-contested routes Booking pace ≥95% of target curve by D-14; seat load factor ≥83% at D-7 on High-tier LCC routes LCC carriers routinely run flash sales 7–10 days prior to departure that distort historical pace curves; NRM demand models trained on non-LCC-disrupted data underestimate last-minute demand spikes and trigger premature availability closures
5.2 Compare RASM and PRASM against LCC benchmark RASM on contested routes within 5% of network average; PRASM improvement >2% versus pre-strategy baseline after 21-day observation window Attribution of RASM movement to pricing strategy versus macro demand shifts requires multivariate regression not available natively in NRM or SkyCAST; AWS Redshift custom model has a 3–5 day build latency that delays actionable insight ⚠
5.3 Trigger strategy review or escalate to pricing committee Strategy review cycle completed within 21 days of activation; escalated routes resolved by pricing committee within 5 business days; route RASM restored to within 5% of network average within 45 days Monthly pricing committee cadence is too slow to counter LCC tactical fare moves that shift daily; airlines with weekly governance cycles capture 3–5% more revenue on contested routes by responding before LCC-stimulated demand is fully absorbed ⚠
