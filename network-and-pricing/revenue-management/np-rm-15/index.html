Airline-Specific Risks & Pain Points

LCC carriers increasingly use direct-channel-only fares not filed via ATPCO; ACI feed misses 20–35% of actual LCC selling prices on contested routes
LCC carriers revise fares multiple times per day; bot-detection mechanisms on Spirit and Frontier websites block automated scraping, requiring manual supplementation that introduces 12–24h lag
MIDT-sourced market share data lags 60–90 days; real-time share is modelled, not measured, creating tier misclassification risk on recently-entered LCC markets
LCC ancillary fee schedules are not available via any standardised data feed and change without notice; manual fee updates lag by up to one week, overstating or understating the real passenger cost differential
LCC carriers file schedule placeholders with inflated frequencies to hold airport slot positions; published seats overstate committed capacity by up to 15%, inflating perceived competitive threat and driving unnecessary yield dilution responses
Differentiation strategy (premium service, schedule convenience) is difficult to sustain on short-haul routes under 500 miles where LCC price advantage exceeds perceived product differential — risks revenue dilution without stimulation benefit materialising
