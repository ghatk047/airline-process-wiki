StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Scan bookings approaching ticketing time limit TTL breach rate ≤0.5% of daily ticketed bookings Altéa queue refresh latency can delay TTL alerts by 15–30 min, causing bookings to lapse before analysts act
1.2 Flag PNRs with expired ticketing time limits 100% of expired TTL PNRs queued within 5 min of breach Agents in GDS (Sabre/Travelport) may rebook on new PNR to circumvent TTL, requiring cross-GDS deduplication ⚠
1.3 Validate fare basis code against booked RBD Fare-RBD mismatch detection rate ≥99% within 24 hrs of ticketing ATPCO Cat 25 private fare rules are not always loaded in GDS displays, causing agents to ticket on incorrect public fare basis ⚠
1.4 Verify fare construction rules and routing compliance Routing violation cases referred to recovery ≤0.3% of international tickets Complex Round-the-World or multi-carrier journeys with published interline fares lack automated combinability validation in NRM
2.1 Investigate and classify integrity exception Mean time to classify exception ≤30 min High exception queue volume during schedule irregularity events (IRROP) overwhelms 2-analyst teams, risking revenue leakage
2.2 Decide to extend TTL or cancel non-compliant booking TTL extension approval rate ≤15% of breached PNRs (excess indicates agent abuse) No automated scoring model to rank extension worthiness — decisions rely on analyst judgement, creating inconsistency across markets ⚠
2.3 Cancel non-ticketed PNRs and release inventory Inventory released within 15 min of cancellation decision; recapture rate ≥60% on high-demand flights Seat release timing relative to NRM availability update cycle can delay recapture on flights within 72 hrs of departure
3.1 Process ticket void within 24-hour BSP window Same-day void processing rate ≥98%; zero late voids submitted outside BSP cut-off IATA BSP cut-off times vary by country (midnight local vs. UTC), causing agents to miss the void window on international sales ⚠
3.2 Assess refund eligibility against fare rules Fare rule compliance on refund decisions ≥99.5% ATPCO Cat 31 ticket changes rules for bundled ancillaries (seats, bags) are not consistently interpreted by Altéa auto-refund module ⚠
3.3 Process involuntary refund for schedule change or IRROP Involuntary refund processing ≤72 hrs of passenger request; DOT 24-hr refund rule compliance = 100% DOT 24-hour refund rule (14 CFR 259.5) requires next-business-day processing; Altéa batch refund jobs occasionally miss the SLA during high-IRROP periods
3.4 Reissue ticket for voluntary fare exchange Exchange error rate ≤0.8% of reissued tickets; penalty collection accuracy 100% Complex multi-carrier reissues on interline itineraries require manual fare recalculation — Altéa auto-reissue module not triggered for codeshare segments
4.1 Review incoming Agency Debit Memos from GDS ADM review completion within 15 days of issuance (IATA resolution deadline); ADM recovery rate ≥85% IATA ADM resolution deadlines (15 days) are tight; Sabre and Amadeus GDS present ADM data in different formats, requiring manual normalisation
4.2 Dispute or accept ADM within IATA resolution window Dispute win rate ≥70% of contested ADMs; zero ADMs missed past IATA 15-day window Airlines cannot dispute ADMs if original error was due to their own fare filing omission in ATPCO — requires internal root-cause log to differentiate ⚠
4.3 Escalate fraudulent booking patterns for recovery Fraud pattern detected within 48 hrs of first occurrence; zero undetected churned-inventory incidents per quarter NRM does not natively flag cross-GDS duplicate booking patterns — requires manual cross-referencing of Sabre, Amadeus, and Travelport PNR exports ⚠
5.1 Reconcile BSP/ARC sales data against PSS ticket log Reconciliation completion by T+3 business days; unmatched items ≤0.1% of gross ticket revenue BSP billing files use IATA Standard Numeric Code (SNC) which must be mapped to SAP cost centre hierarchy — mapping table breaks when new routes are added without finance notification ⚠
5.2 Resolve settlement discrepancies and post corrections Discrepancy resolution rate ≥98% within 10 business days of identification Cross-currency settlement discrepancies on BSP billings (e.g., EUR vs. USD) create FX rounding differences that accumulate to material amounts over high-volume periods
6.1 Generate revenue integrity KPI dashboard Dashboard published by Monday 09:00 each week; data freshness ≤24 hrs Data pipeline from Altéa PSS to Tableau uses nightly batch export — intraday exceptions are invisible until next morning, delaying management response
6.2 Audit agency ticketing compliance and escalate violations Agencies with error rate >2% flagged for remediation within 30 days; systemic non-compliance resolved within 90 days PRISM data covers ARC (US/Canada) only — non-ARC international agency compliance monitoring requires separate BSP market-by-market data pull with no consolidated view ⚠
