StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Extract O&D Revenue & Booking Data Data extract completeness ≥99%; query SLA <4 hrs MIDT data covers only ~65% of bookings; direct/NDC channel bookings require separate aggregation from Altéa DCS, causing analyst reconciliation overhead
1.2 Pull Route Operating Cost Data Cost data refresh lag <24 hrs; CASM variance vs budget <2% Fleet commonality (737 family) limits aircraft-type cost granularity; fuel hedging gains/losses allocated at entity level not route level, distorting route CASM
1.3 Retrieve Competitive Fare Intelligence Competitor fare coverage ≥90% of routes under review LCC and ULCC competitors file fares with minimal lead time; ATPCO refresh cycle (2x weekly) creates stale data windows that allow mispriced response strategies
1.4 Validate Data Completeness & Reconcile Reconciliation exceptions <1% of route-months; sign-off completed within 1 business day Cross-system reconciliation between Altéa settlement data and SAP GL actuals regularly surfaces timing differences of 3–5 days for interline and codeshare revenue ⚠
2.1 Analyse Load Factor & Yield by Route System-wide load factor target ≥83%; yield degradation alert threshold >5% YoY Altéa NRM optimises at O&D level; thin point-to-point routes with no connecting traffic receive less sophisticated demand modelling, leading to premature sell-down
2.2 Model O&D Traffic Spill & Recapture Spill model accuracy ≥88% vs actual post-departure; recapture rate assumption validated quarterly Pure point-to-point network means spilled passengers rarely recaptured on connecting itineraries; recapture assumptions borrowed from hub-and-spoke models overstate true recapture by 10–15%
2.3 Assess Route Against Break-Even Load Factor Break-even LF calculation completed for 100% of routes under review Break-even LF sensitive to fuel price assumptions; a ±10% fuel swing shifts break-even by 3–4 pp, requiring frequent scenario refreshes that strain analyst bandwidth ⚠
3.1 Attribute Direct Operating Costs to Route Direct cost attribution covers ≥95% of route operating cost; CASM per ASM variance <1.5¢ vs prior period Maintenance event timing distorts monthly route CASM; an A-check occurring mid-month inflates CASM for that route by 8–12%, masking underlying profitability
3.2 Allocate Overhead & Indirect Costs to Route Overhead allocation methodology reviewed annually; allocation variance <3% across fleet No universally agreed overhead allocation driver; switching from ASM-based to departure-based allocation changes route rankings by up to 15 positions, creating internal disagreement on route verdicts
3.3 Compare CASM Against Fleet-Type Benchmark Routes with CASM >110% of fleet benchmark escalated for review 737-700 has ~18% higher CASM per ASM vs MAX 8; routes locked to -700 gauge due to gate constraints or runway length restrictions cannot benefit from gauge upgrades ⚠
4.1 Build Route Contribution Margin P&L Model P&L model completed for 100% of routes under review; contribution margin >0 for ≥85% of network routes Ancillary revenue (EarlyBird, upgraded boarding, baggage) not disaggregated at route level in Altéa; lump-sum allocation from corporate finance understates ancillary contribution on leisure-heavy routes by up to 20%
4.2 Run Frequency & Gauge Sensitivity Scenarios At least 3 scenarios modelled per underperforming route; scenario run time <2 hrs per route Fleet commonality reduces gauge flexibility to 737-700 vs 737-800/MAX 8; slot-constrained airports (DCA, LGA) limit frequency increase scenarios regardless of P&L outcome
4.3 Assess Network Contribution & Connectivity Value Network contribution adjustment applied to ≥100% of reviewed routes; connectivity premium >5% of direct P&L flagged for executive review Point-to-point model lacks hub feed economics; connectivity value difficult to quantify without connecting passenger data — WN does not sell connecting itineraries through GDS, limiting third-party validation ⚠
5.1 Prepare Route Profitability Scorecard Scorecard delivered within 5 business days of data extract; dashboard refresh SLA <1 hr Tableau dashboards sourced from AWS Redshift data lake; ETL pipeline latency of 6–8 hrs means executives sometimes review overnight decisions against stale figures
5.2 Review Findings with Commercial Leadership Leadership review completed within 10 business days of analysis; ≥90% of recommendations resolved without escalation to CEO Revenue Management and Network Planning use different optimisation horizons (RM: 90-day, NP: 12-18 month); misalignment on route verdicts for routes in near-term yield recovery frequently causes review rework ⚠
5.3 Document Decision & Update Route Status Route status update completed within 2 business days of approval; 100% of decisions documented with rationale SkyWORKS decision log is free-text; no structured field for profitability verdict — audit trail relies on manual documentation discipline, creating compliance risk during DOT reviews
