Route Profitability Analysis

L4 Step Detail

StepL4 Step NameRoleSystem InputOutputKPI Pain Point / RiskDecisionException
1.1 Extract O&D Revenue & Booking Data Network Analyst Amadeus SkyCAST Seasonal booking history, MIDT data feeds, BSP/ARC settlement files O&D revenue matrix by route, cabin class, and booking window Data extract completeness ≥99%; query SLA <4 hrs MIDT data covers only ~65% of bookings; direct/NDC channel bookings require separate aggregation from Altéa DCS, causing analyst reconciliation overhead
1.2 Pull Route Operating Cost Data Finance Business Partner SAP S/4HANA Finance (FI/CO) General ledger actuals, cost centre allocations, fuel price feeds Direct operating cost per departure by route and aircraft type Cost data refresh lag <24 hrs; CASM variance vs budget <2% Fleet commonality (737 family) limits aircraft-type cost granularity; fuel hedging gains/losses allocated at entity level not route level, distorting route CASM
1.3 Retrieve Competitive Fare Intelligence Network Analyst OAG Schedule Analyser Competitor SSIM files, OAG traffic data, ATPCO fare tariff feeds Competitive fare matrix and capacity share by route Competitor fare coverage ≥90% of routes under review LCC and ULCC competitors file fares with minimal lead time; ATPCO refresh cycle (2x weekly) creates stale data windows that allow mispriced response strategies
1.4 Validate Data Completeness & Reconcile Senior Network Planner AWS Redshift O&D revenue matrix, cost extract, competitive data, Altéa PNR data Reconciled route data package; data quality scorecard Reconciliation exceptions <1% of route-months; sign-off completed within 1 business day Cross-system reconciliation between Altéa settlement data and SAP GL actuals regularly surfaces timing differences of 3–5 days for interline and codeshare revenue ✓ ✓
2.1 Analyse Load Factor & Yield by Route Revenue Management Analyst Amadeus Revenue Management (NRM / AltéaRM) Reconciled route data package, cabin inventory records Load factor and yield report by route, DOW, and season System-wide load factor target ≥83%; yield degradation alert threshold >5% YoY Altéa NRM optimises at O&D level; thin point-to-point routes with no connecting traffic receive less sophisticated demand modelling, leading to premature sell-down
2.2 Model O&D Traffic Spill & Recapture Network Analyst Amadeus SkyCAST Load factor report, O&D demand signals, competitor capacity data Spill/recapture estimates; demand elasticity coefficients by route Spill model accuracy ≥88% vs actual post-departure; recapture rate assumption validated quarterly Pure point-to-point network means spilled passengers rarely recaptured on connecting itineraries; recapture assumptions borrowed from hub-and-spoke models overstate true recapture by 10–15%
2.3 Assess Route Against Break-Even Load Factor Senior Network Planner Amadeus SkyCAST Yield data, direct operating costs per ASM, average fare Break-even load factor by route; routes flagged for detailed cost review Break-even LF calculation completed for 100% of routes under review Break-even LF sensitive to fuel price assumptions; a ±10% fuel swing shifts break-even by 3–4 pp, requiring frequent scenario refreshes that strain analyst bandwidth ✓
3.1 Attribute Direct Operating Costs to Route Finance Business Partner SAP S/4HANA Finance (FI/CO) Cost centre actuals, block hours by route, fuel consumption by tail Direct CASM (crew, fuel, maintenance, airport fees) per route Direct cost attribution covers ≥95% of route operating cost; CASM per ASM variance <1.5¢ vs prior period Maintenance event timing distorts monthly route CASM; an A-check occurring mid-month inflates CASM for that route by 8–12%, masking underlying profitability ✓
3.2 Allocate Overhead & Indirect Costs to Route Finance Business Partner SAP S/4HANA Finance (FI/CO) Corporate overhead pools (IT, HR, HQ), fleet lease costs, depreciation schedules Fully-loaded CASM per route including overhead allocation Overhead allocation methodology reviewed annually; allocation variance <3% across fleet No universally agreed overhead allocation driver; switching from ASM-based to departure-based allocation changes route rankings by up to 15 positions, creating internal disagreement on route verdicts
3.3 Compare CASM Against Fleet-Type Benchmark Senior Network Planner Amadeus SkySYM Fully-loaded CASM, benchmark CASM by 737 variant (700/800/MAX 8) CASM variance report; routes flagged for gauge optimisation review Routes with CASM >110% of fleet benchmark escalated for review 737-700 has ~18% higher CASM per ASM vs MAX 8; routes locked to -700 gauge due to gate constraints or runway length restrictions cannot benefit from gauge upgrades ✓
4.1 Build Route Contribution Margin P&L Model Senior Network Planner Amadeus SkySYM O&D revenue matrix, direct CASM, overhead allocation, ancillary revenue estimates Route P&L model: RASM, CASM, contribution margin, EBIT per route P&L model completed for 100% of routes under review; contribution margin >0 for ≥85% of network routes Ancillary revenue (EarlyBird, upgraded boarding, baggage) not disaggregated at route level in Altéa; lump-sum allocation from corporate finance understates ancillary contribution on leisure-heavy routes by up to 20%
4.2 Run Frequency & Gauge Sensitivity Scenarios Network Planning Manager Amadeus SkySYM Route P&L model, fleet assignment options, schedule constraints from SkyWORKS Scenario comparison matrix: +1 freq, -1 freq, gauge-up, gauge-down, suspend At least 3 scenarios modelled per underperforming route; scenario run time <2 hrs per route Fleet commonality reduces gauge flexibility to 737-700 vs 737-800/MAX 8; slot-constrained airports (DCA, LGA) limit frequency increase scenarios regardless of P&L outcome
4.3 Assess Network Contribution & Connectivity Value VP Network Planning Amadeus SkySYM Scenario matrix, network connectivity model, Rapid Rewards contribution data Revised profitability ranking incorporating network externality value Network contribution adjustment applied to ≥100% of reviewed routes; connectivity premium >5% of direct P&L flagged for executive review Point-to-point model lacks hub feed economics; connectivity value difficult to quantify without connecting passenger data — WN does not sell connecting itineraries through GDS, limiting third-party validation ✓
5.1 Prepare Route Profitability Scorecard Network Planning Manager Tableau Route P&L models, scenario outputs, competitive benchmarks Executive route profitability dashboard: RAG status, trend, strategic recommendation Scorecard delivered within 5 business days of data extract; dashboard refresh SLA <1 hr Tableau dashboards sourced from AWS Redshift data lake; ETL pipeline latency of 6–8 hrs means executives sometimes review overnight decisions against stale figures
5.2 Review Findings with Commercial Leadership VP Network Planning Tableau Route profitability scorecard, RM yield forecast, Sales channel input Reviewed recommendation with leadership commentary; routes approved/deferred/escalated Leadership review completed within 10 business days of analysis; ≥90% of recommendations resolved without escalation to CEO Revenue Management and Network Planning use different optimisation horizons (RM: 90-day, NP: 12-18 month); misalignment on route verdicts for routes in near-term yield recovery frequently causes review rework ✓ ✓
5.3 Document Decision & Update Route Status Network Analyst Amadeus SkyWORKS Approved recommendation, leadership sign-off record Route status updated in SkyWORKS (Grow/Maintain/Reduce/Suspend); decision audit trail Route status update completed within 2 business days of approval; 100% of decisions documented with rationale SkyWORKS decision log is free-text; no structured field for profitability verdict — audit trail relies on manual documentation discipline, creating compliance risk during DOT reviews
