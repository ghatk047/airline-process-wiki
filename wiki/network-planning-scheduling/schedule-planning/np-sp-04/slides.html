StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Analyse seasonal schedule demand vs slot inventory Slot gap identification rate ≥95% before coordinator submission deadline SkyCAST demand forecasts do not auto-reconcile with live coordinator slot libraries — manual cross-check required, introducing errors in high-frequency bank builds
1.2 Classify constrained airports by IATA Level 2 / Level 3 100% of Level 3 airports correctly classified before coordinator submission window opens IATA airport level classification updates lag real-world congestion changes by up to one season; misclassification causes incorrect submission routing and missed deadlines ⚠
1.3 Generate prioritised slot request register Slot requests submitted ≥8 weeks before IATA coordination deadline Late submission forfeits priority queue position at 47% of Level 3 coordinators; SkyWORKS has no built-in deadline tracking for each coordinator's unique submission calendar
2.1 Extract historical slot series from IATA CRS portal 100% of historical series extracted before coordinator portal submission window closes IATA CRS portal availability falls below 99.5% during peak submission windows; fallback is manual email-based SSIM exchange with no automated validation
2.2 Validate 80/20 utilisation rule compliance per series Grandfather right retention rate ≥98% of eligible series per IATA WSG Operational disruptions (weather, ATC) erode utilisation ratios; airlines must file force majeure exemptions within 5 days of each triggering event — SkyWORKS does not auto-flag breaches in real time ⚠
2.3 File force majeure exemptions for at-risk series Exemption filed within 5 calendar days of triggering disruption event Missed exemption window results in permanent series loss with no appeals mechanism at most Level 3 airports; coordinator acceptance rates vary by region (EU coordinators accept ~82%, US FAA exempt)
3.1 Generate SSIM-formatted SCR slot request messages SSIM file validation error rate < 1% on first submission SkyWORKS SSIM export requires manual field mapping for airports not in the standard IATA airport list; common source of format errors that delay acceptance by coordinator systems
3.2 Submit slot requests to each airport coordinator 100% of requests submitted before each coordinator's unique deadline Airport coordinators (ACNUSA, NATS, DFS, FAA, etc.) each operate different portal UIs with no single API standard; multi-airport submissions require manual portal navigation per coordinator
3.3 Evaluate initial slot allocation vs requested schedule Allocation match rate ≥85% of requested slots in initial SCT response Coordinators allocate on grandfather-then-new-entrant priority; late filers receive only residual slots, which are frequently at off-peak times incompatible with planned bank structures ⚠
4.1 Prepare negotiation brief for IATA slot conference Brief finalised ≥2 weeks before IATA slot conference opening date SkySYM scenario export does not auto-generate slot swap proposals; analyst must manually map network simulation outputs to coordinator slot pool data, taking 3–5 days per conference cycle
4.2 Negotiate slot swaps and alternatives at conference ≥70% of critical slot gaps (top 10 revenue routes) resolved at conference Bilateral swap negotiations are blocked when the counterparty airline holds conflicting grandfather claims on the same series; coordinator cannot force swaps without both parties' consent under IATA WSG §9 ⚠
4.3 File appeal for unresolved critical slot gaps Appeal response received within 5 business days of filing Appeal panels have no binding authority at most coordinators; outcomes are advisory only, leaving airlines with no recourse if coordinator declines to intervene in grandfather disputes
5.1 Confirm final slot allocation and accept coordinator offer 100% of confirmed slots acknowledged within 24h of coordinator receipt Coordinator systems deliver SCT messages in overnight batch files; missed or delayed messages require manual reconciliation with coordinator helpdesk, often taking 2–3 business days to resolve
5.2 Integrate confirmed slots into SkyWORKS schedule build Schedule-to-slot reconciliation accuracy ≥99.5% before SSIM publication Slot times and SkyWORKS schedule times drift due to timezone and DST mapping errors during SSIM import; DST boundary airports (e.g. LORD, EIDW) are a recurring source of off-by-one-hour faults ⚠
5.3 Publish season SSIM file to GDS channels and PSS SSIM distributed to all GDS ≤72h after final coordinator confirmation GDS distribution from SkyWORKS is fire-and-forget with no real-time ACK; distribution failures are silent and only detected when downstream booking discrepancies surface 24–48h later
6.1 Monitor weekly slot utilisation against 80% threshold Slot series utilisation ≥80% per IATA WSG standard throughout season Operational cancellations and schedule swaps erode utilisation without triggering automated alerts in SkyWORKS; compliance analysts must run manual SQL queries against ops data to compute rolling utilisation ratios ⚠
6.2 Return or swap underperforming slot series Slot returns submitted ≥48h before departure per IATA WSG in-season return deadline Late returns are forfeited to the slot pool with no recovery; repeated late-return flags accumulate as coordinator penalty records that reduce priority scoring in subsequent season bids
6.3 Execute ad-hoc slot swaps for operational recovery Ad-hoc swap processed within 2h of disruption notification during coordinator office hours Coordinator swap availability is limited to office hours (typically 07:00–19:00 local); out-of-hours disruptions at Level 3 airports have no swap mechanism, forcing cancellation or slot violation
