Airline-Specific Risks & Pain Points

ACARS frequency gaps over oceanic or remote continental segments require fallback to HF voice, increasing dispatcher workload by 40%
SELCAL code conflicts across shared fleet pools cause missed ATC calls; manual cross-check process has no automated deconfliction in legacy ACARS ground system
Gate-to-aircraft ACARS connectivity failures affect ~3% of departures; ground crew must physically confirm avionics status with no automated alert to dispatch
Voice backup coordination relies on manual frequency lookups; no integrated fallback workflow in dispatch system creates inconsistent crew briefing quality
Automatic OOOI triggering depends on door-closure sensor accuracy; carrier-specific wiring faults on ageing narrowbodies cause delayed or missed OUT messages, distorting OTP metrics
Manual crew entry of FOB in FliteDeck creates transcription errors; no automated FMS-to-ACARS fuel bridge in older EFB configurations
