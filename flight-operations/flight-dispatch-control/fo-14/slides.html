1.1 Load ACARS datalink frequencies for planned route Datalink configuration complete ≥15 min before departure ACARS frequency gaps over oceanic or remote continental segments require fallback to HF voice, increasing dispatcher workload by 40%
1.2 Assign and verify SELCAL code for aircraft SELCAL assignment accuracy 100%; no duplicate codes per sector SELCAL code conflicts across shared fleet pools cause missed ATC calls; manual cross-check process has no automated deconfliction in legacy ACARS ground system
1.3 Test ACARS uplink connectivity with Operations Control Uplink test round-trip <90 seconds; link quality ≥95% message delivery Gate-to-aircraft ACARS connectivity failures affect ~3% of departures; ground crew must physically confirm avionics status with no automated alert to dispatch ⚠
1.4 Activate voice backup if ACARS uplink fails Voice backup activation within 5 min of confirmed datalink failure Voice backup coordination relies on manual frequency lookups; no integrated fallback workflow in dispatch system creates inconsistent crew briefing quality
2.1 Transmit OUT and OFF OOOI messages via ACARS OOOI transmission latency <30 seconds of triggering event; 99.5% capture rate Automatic OOOI triggering depends on door-closure sensor accuracy; carrier-specific wiring faults on ageing narrowbodies cause delayed or missed OUT messages, distorting OTP metrics
2.2 Send initial position report and fuel state to dispatch Initial report transmitted within 10 min of wheels-off Manual crew entry of FOB in FliteDeck creates transcription errors; no automated FMS-to-ACARS fuel bridge in older EFB configurations
2.3 Evaluate departure delay or route amendment requirement Route amendment decision issued ≥5 min before off-block when ATC delay >15 min ATC ground delay programmes (GDPs) issued with <10 min notice require simultaneous reroute and crew notification; NAVBLUE slot management tool lacks real-time GDP ingestion from FAA TFMS ⚠
2.4 Uplink revised route clearance or holding instructions Crew acknowledgement received within 3 min of uplink; amendment compliance 100% CPDLC message formatting errors between airline system and FAA FANS-1/A avionics create rejected uplinks requiring voice re-confirmation, adding 4–8 min average delay
3.1 Monitor automated ACARS position reports en-route Position report receipt interval ≤30 min; missed report alert within 5 min of overdue ACARS position reporting gaps over North Atlantic and Pacific oceanic tracks require manual ADS-C polling; latency up to 15 min leaves dispatch with stale situational awareness
3.2 Process and acknowledge dispatch uplink messages Uplink acknowledgement within 5 min for operational messages; 100% receipt confirmation High-volume uplink queues during irregular operations (IRROPs) overload crew attention; no message priority ranking in FliteDeck causes safety-critical messages to queue behind routine updates
3.3 Execute HF radio handoff for oceanic or remote operations HF contact established within 10 min of oceanic FIR entry; first-call success rate ≥90% HF propagation variability in high-latitude routes (NAT track system) causes failed initial contacts; airlines without SATCOM-equipped fleets have no reliable backup, risking SLOP deviation reporting failures
3.4 Assess weather deviation or emergency communication need Dispatcher response to crew deviation request <2 min; emergency acknowledgement <1 min Weather deviation requests during oceanic segments require simultaneous ATC and OCC coordination over SATCOM and ACARS; latency in SATCOM voice degrades timely clearance issuance ⚠
3.5 Coordinate weather deviation or emergency with ATC and OCC Deviation clearance loop closed (crew request → ATC clearance → crew confirmation) <15 min Multi-party coordination chain (crew → dispatch → ATC → dispatch → crew) has no single-system view; handoffs via separate ACARS and voice systems create documentation gaps for post-event safety review
4.1 Receive and acknowledge D-ATIS update via datalink D-ATIS received ≥30 min before destination ETA; ATIS currency check at top of descent Airports without D-ATIS capability require VHF voice ATIS, adding crew workload; ATIS letter change between receipt and landing causes approach briefing invalidation with no automated crew alert
4.2 Transmit final ETA and fuel state to dispatch and OCC ETA accuracy ±3 min versus actual on-blocks; fuel state report within 1 min of TOD FMS ETA drift in RVSM/RNP environments caused by ATC tactical vectoring is not automatically pushed to ACARS; dispatcher uses stale ETA for gate planning, causing downstream turnaround delays
4.3 Confirm destination availability with dispatch Go/no-go decision for destination communicated to crew ≥20 min before ETA NAVBLUE NOTAM integration does not ingest real-time runway contamination SNOWTAM data; dispatchers must manually cross-check ATIS and ATC PIREPs, creating decision latency in winter operations ⚠
4.4 Coordinate diversion to alternate airport if required Alternate airport decision and crew uplink <5 min of diversion trigger; fuel sufficient for alternate + 30 min holding confirmed Alternate airport ATIS and gate availability data not integrated in NAVBLUE; dispatcher must manually contact destination OCC and alternate airport ops, adding 8–12 min to diversion coordination loop
5.1 Download and archive ACARS message logs post-flight 100% of ACARS logs archived within 2 hours of on-blocks; retention period ≥24 months per ICAO Annex 6 SITA ACARS ground system purges raw message buffers after 72 hours; airlines without automated archive pipelines lose communications audit trails required for accident investigation and FAA inspection
5.2 Review post-flight communications for anomalies or gaps Post-flight communications review completed within 24 hours of arrival; anomaly detection rate ≥95% vs manual audit No automated anomaly detection across ACARS and voice logs; analysts manually cross-reference two separate systems, limiting review throughput to ~30 flights/analyst/day ⚠
5.3 File ATC communications deviation or irregularity report Deviation report filed within 48 hours of event per FAR Part 121.711; 100% compliance with ASAP/ASRS voluntary reporting where applicable Dispatcher irregularity reporting workflow is disconnected from ACARS archive system; manual re-entry of message evidence into SMS creates transcription errors and delays in safety trend analysis
