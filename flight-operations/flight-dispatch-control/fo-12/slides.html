StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Crew sign-in and duty period validation Sign-in completion ≥30 min prior to departure; FTL compliance 100% FAR Part 117 / EASA FTL limits require real-time cumulative duty tracking across time zones — late roster updates in Jeppesen Crew Management can create compliance gaps at sign-in
1.2 Validate fatigue risk and FTL compliance FTL violation rate <0.1% of monthly departures Fatigue Risk Management System (FRMS) data is not always integrated with real-time crew tracking; manual overrides by Crew Scheduling introduce compliance risk during IROPS ⚠
1.3 Retrieve and open crew briefing package Briefing package retrieval within 2 min of sign-in; package currency ≤60 min old at review EFB synchronisation delays on congested airport Wi-Fi (especially at hub gates) can result in crews accessing stale briefing data — no automatic staleness alert in FliteDeck
2.1 Review METAR, TAF, and SIGMET reports Weather briefing completed ≥20 min before STD; 100% SIGMET acknowledgement logged Rapidly evolving convective SIGMETs issued by NOAA Aviation Weather Center can be uploaded to GoDirect with 10–15 min latency — crew may depart with outdated convective picture
2.2 Assess destination and alternate minima Correct alternate selected per ICAO Annex 2 / FAR 121.619; fuel-to-alternate margin ≥45 min Low-visibility operations (Cat II/III) require special OpSpecs approval in FAA WebOPSS; not all airports in the network have current CAT II/III plates loaded in FliteDeck for all aircraft types
2.3 Confirm weather acceptable for planned departure Weather-related departure decision communicated to OCC ≥15 min before STD Dispatcher and Captain may reach independent weather conclusions using different data sources (GoDirect vs airline wx feed) with no reconciliation workflow — leads to coordination delays at the gate ⚠
3.1 Review computerised flight plan and fuel load Flight plan review complete ≥15 min before STD; fuel uplift variance ≤2% vs planned NAVBLUE flight plan generation relies on current NOTAM and airspace restriction feeds; late TFR activations (military, VIP) can invalidate the planned route after the plan has been generated but before crew signs off
3.2 Review dispatch release for regulatory compliance Dispatch release co-sign rate without amendment ≥92%; amendment resolution time ≤10 min Dispatch releases generated in NAVBLUE are not dynamically linked to MEL deferral records in Boeing Toolbox — Captain must manually cross-check deferred items against the release, increasing error risk under time pressure ⚠
3.3 Co-sign dispatch release with Dispatcher Dispatch release signed ≥10 min before STD; 100% digital co-signature audit trail FAR 121.663 requires both Captain and Dispatcher to hold the release jointly — paper-based release at remote stations without NAVBLUE access creates audit trail gaps and scan-to-file delays
4.1 Review NOTAM package for departure and en-route NOTAM review complete prior to departure; safety-critical NOTAMs (runway closures, ILS unserviceable) flagged 100% NOTAM volume has increased >300% since 2010 (FAA data); FliteDeck's automated filtering still surfaces 80–120 NOTAMs per flight, creating information overload and risk that critical items are missed
4.2 Review MEL and CDL deferred item list MEL review completion rate 100% prior to boarding; deferral-related delays <0.5% of departures MEL records in Boeing Toolbox are updated by Maintenance Control but real-time sync to crew EFB (FliteDeck) is not guaranteed — crews may board an aircraft unaware of a late deferral logged after their briefing ⚠
4.3 Confirm airworthiness release from Maintenance Maintenance release issued ≥45 min before STD; 100% release traceability in AMOS Maintenance release signature workflows in AMOS require LMB engineer to physically sign the tech log — at outstations without AMOS connectivity, paper tech logs create a digitisation lag and potential duplicate entry errors
5.1 Conduct pre-flight exterior walk-around Walk-around completed ≥30 min before STD; defect-to-report time ≤5 min FliteDeck EFB walk-around checklists are not connected to AMOS defect recording — items found during walk-around must be re-entered manually in the aircraft tech log, creating duplication and potential omission
5.2 Confirm final weight & balance load sheet Load sheet issued ≤15 min before STD; CG deviation from plan <0.5% MAC Last-minute passenger no-shows or late cargo add-ons require load sheet recalculation in Altéa — under the 10-min gate departure pressure window, rushed W&B recalculations increase the risk of CG errors
5.3 Obtain ATC clearance and departure slot Clearance received ≥5 min before pushback; ATFM slot adherence ≥94% ATFM slot allocations from EUROCONTROL NMOC (or FAA EDCT in North America) are issued dynamically and can shift within the last 10 min — ACARS delivery to cockpit adds latency vs. VHF voice clearance, risking slot miss ⚠
