1.1 Ingest METAR/TAF feeds from NWS/NOAA Data refresh latency ≤5 min for all airports in route network NWS ASOS outages at smaller spoke airports create data gaps; GoDirect fallback to model-derived obs introduces ±3°C temperature error affecting performance calculations
1.2 Ingest SIGMET, AIRMET & convective outlook 100% of active domestic SIGMETs captured within 10 min of NWS issuance International SIGMET ingestion via SADIS requires separate subscription; coverage gaps over oceanic tracks (OTS) require manual cross-check against NAT SIGMET bulletins
1.3 Validate data currency and completeness ≥98% of route-relevant stations reporting within 30-min window at pre-departure check No automated alerting when upstream NWS feed drops; dispatcher must manually notice missing station data during busy multi-departure windows ⚠
2.1 Analyse route-specific weather hazards Hazard assessment completed ≥45 min before scheduled departure (D-45) Rapidly developing convection (pop-up cells) outpaces 1-hourly radar update cycle; AviationSentry nowcast accuracy degrades beyond +3 hr for mesoscale convective systems
2.2 Assess departure, destination & alternate wx Alternate airport identification completed for 100% of IFR releases within D-60 TAF validity windows (±1 hr) create ambiguity at scheduled arrival time; low-frequency alternates may lack TAF service, requiring meteorological judgement unsupported by system data
2.3 Classify weather threat level against ops specs Zero flights released into Red-classified weather corridor without Duty Manager override sign-off OpSpec thresholds for convective avoidance (20 nm lateral separation from CBs) not programmatically enforced in AviationSentry; relies on dispatcher visual judgement of radar returns ⚠
3.1 Cross-reference weather threats with flight plans 100% of active flight plans checked against current SIGMET polygons pre-departure NAVBLUE polygon overlay requires manual SIGMET boundary entry when NWS feed XML schema changes; integration SLA between NWS and NAVBLUE API is 15 min, creating a window of stale data
3.2 Calculate fuel burn impact of weather deviations Fuel recalculation turnaround ≤8 min per rerouted flight to preserve D-45 release window NAVBLUE performance model uses scheduled aircraft type; last-minute tail swaps (B737-700 for -800) require re-entry of performance data manually — no automated tail-type pull from Altéa OCC
3.3 Verify alternate airport weather acceptability Alternate confirmation completed for 100% of IFR dispatch releases before crew briefing Peak-demand periods force simultaneous alternate re-evaluation for multiple flights; limited dispatcher bandwidth increases risk of using stale TAF data beyond 6-hour validity window ⚠
4.1 Issue or withhold dispatch weather release ≤2% of departures held >30 min beyond SOBT for weather-related dispatch rework Dispatcher joint release authority requires co-sign from PIC; when crew is in-bound the electronic co-sign workflow in NAVBLUE times out after 15 min, forcing a manual paper release process
4.2 Brief flight crew on significant weather via EFB Crew acknowledgment of weather briefing received for 100% of Amber/Red-classified departures EFB push notification delivery confirmation is not real-time; dispatcher cannot distinguish between un-delivered and unread briefings, risking undocumented crew awareness gaps before FAR 121.601 compliance check
4.3 Confirm crew acceptance of dispatch release Crew acceptance ACARS message received ≥20 min before SOBT for 95% of departures ACARS acceptance workflow is non-standardised across gate types; at congested gates crews sometimes provide verbal acceptance to ground ops without ACARS confirmation, leaving no auditable record for FAR 121.663 ⚠
5.1 Monitor en-route convective activity in real time Dispatcher workstation refresh ≤5 min; proximity alert generated within 2 min of threshold breach Single dispatcher managing 15–20 simultaneous en-route flights cannot sustain continuous radar monitoring; AviationSentry automated proximity alerting requires manual polygon configuration per flight and resets on sector boundary crossings
5.2 Evaluate PIREP and crew weather deviation reports PIREP relay to NWS/ATC within 15 min of crew report for 90% of severe turbulence encounters PIREP data entry format inconsistency between crews (free-text vs. structured ACARS form) reduces automated parsing accuracy in AviationSentry; significant events may be missed in multi-flight monitoring queue
5.3 Assess need for divert, reroute or amended release Amended release issued within 10 min of divert-trigger threshold being met; zero diversions without prior dispatcher-crew coordination Amended release workflow in NAVBLUE requires re-opening a filed plan and triggers full recalculation; under time pressure dispatchers sometimes relay route changes verbally without formal amended release, creating FAR 121.663 compliance exposure ⚠
