StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Trigger wireless QAR data offload at gate Offload completion within 10 min of gate arrival; flight coverage rate ≥99% WiFi connectivity gaps at remote apron stands force manual USB retrieval, adding 4–6 hr to processing queue and delaying same-day exceedance review
1.2 Validate data frame completeness and CRC integrity Data completeness ≥98% of flight duration; frame error rate <0.1% Pre-2010 QAR units use proprietary frame formats requiring manual decoding — adds up to 2 hr per flight to the processing queue and delays exceedance detection ⚠
1.3 Ingest supplemental ACARS performance snapshots ACARS data merge success rate ≥95%; gap-fill latency <30 min ACARS snapshot frequency (every 10–30 min) is too coarse to reconstruct fine-grained exceedance events; oceanic airspace has no ACARS coverage, leaving gap-fill as an estimate only
2.1 Execute automated exceedance detection run Detection run completion <30 min per flight; false positive rate <5% Threshold libraries require manual calibration per aircraft type variant (e.g., 737-800 vs MAX 8 have different structural limits); miscalibration generates false positives that erode crew confidence in the FOQA programme
2.2 Classify exceedances by severity Cat A/B/C Classification accuracy ≥97%; all Cat A events reviewed within 24 hr of detection IOSA audit and airline AQP programme use different category boundary definitions — maintaining dual threshold tables increases configuration error risk and complicates audit trail documentation ⚠
2.3 Escalate Cat A exceedance to Flight Safety Cat A notification delivered to Flight Safety Manager <2 hr of detection Integration between Safety Line FOQA and Intelex SMS is typically manual (CSV export/import), introducing notification latency that risks breaching 24-hour internal reporting SLAs and downstream regulatory filing windows
3.1 Extract engine performance parameters post-flight Parameter extraction completeness ≥99.5%; processing latency <15 min post-landing GoDirect data feed latency for long-haul international operations can exceed 1 hr post-landing due to satellite link congestion, delaying trend detection for time-sensitive engine health events
3.2 Compare engine trends against MRO baseline EGT margin loss ≤1°C per flight cycle; alerts triggered at ±3σ deviation from fleet mean AMOS engine baselines require manual update after each shop visit — stale baselines mask post-maintenance performance degradation and delay early warning detection of incipient failures ⚠
3.3 Raise maintenance alert for engine trend deviation Maintenance alert actioned within 4 hr of detection; on-wing unplanned engine removal rate <0.05 per 1,000 flight hours Tight aircraft utilisation (≥13 hr/day on narrowbody fleets) means borescope inspections are routinely deferred due to limited ground time, allowing early-stage engine degradation to progress to AOG events
4.1 Cross-reference event with ATC and meteorological records Contextualisation report completed within 48 hr of event detection ATC radar data access requires a formal request to the relevant ANSP in most regions, with turnaround of 3–10 business days — delays investigation timelines and risks crew debriefing weeks after the event
4.2 Conduct de-identified ASAP crew debrief ASAP debrief completed within 72 hr of event detection; crew voluntary disclosure rate ≥80% Crew participation in ASAP is undermined in organisations with punitive safety cultures; legal protections for voluntary disclosures vary by jurisdiction, limiting debrief effectiveness for international crew members ⚠
4.3 Route finding to training or close investigation Corrective action closure within 30 days; recurrence rate for same event type <5% within 12 months No automated integration between Intelex SMS and airline LMS platforms — manual training referral process results in tracking gaps, unverifiable completion, and repeat exceedances from the same crew pairing
5.1 Publish monthly FOQA performance dashboard Dashboard published by 5th calendar day of each month; ≥12 FOQA KPIs tracked and trended Manual data export pipeline from Safety Line FOQA to Tableau breaks with each platform version upgrade — dashboard reconciliation consumes 3–4 hr/month of analyst time on non-value-adding data plumbing
5.2 Evaluate event against mandatory SDR reporting threshold Mandatory reporting decisions documented within 48 hr of investigation close FAR 121.703 SDR thresholds are ambiguous for software-defined exceedances such as FMS autoflight anomalies — each case requires legal review, delaying time-sensitive filings and creating regulatory exposure ⚠
5.3 Submit SDR or ASAP report to FAA SDR submitted within 96 hr of discovery per FAR 121.703; ASAP report submitted within 24 hr of event FAA DrAFT portal experiences 2–3 unplanned outages per year — no automated failover submission path means airlines risk missing mandatory filing deadlines and face potential enforcement action
5.4 Archive FDR and QAR data per FAR 91.609 retention policy 100% of FDR/QAR files archived within 24 hr of processing completion; retention period ≥25 hr (FDR per FAR 91.609), ≥2 yr (QAR programme data) AWS S3 lifecycle policy misconfiguration has caused premature deletion of QAR data at multiple carriers — audit trail gaps expose the airline to FAA enforcement action and inability to reconstruct accident sequences
