Airline-Specific Risks & Pain Points

GoDirect ATIS latency can lag live tower broadcasts by 3–5 min during high-volume periods, risking crew departure on stale altimeter settings
NAVBLUE route optimisation does not automatically incorporate FAA Coded Departure Routes (CDRs) during Ground Delay Programs, requiring manual dispatcher override
TFMS EDCT updates can arrive with <15 min notice, making ground crew coordination nearly impossible during high-frequency departure banks at focus cities
FAA does not guarantee slot substitution approval; carriers without dedicated ATC coordination desk (like low-cost carriers) suffer higher rejection rates
Voice clearance delivery congestion at Class B airports (ORD, ATL, LAX) causes average 8–12 min clearance delays during push banks; PDC via ACARS not universally available
ATC short-notice route amendments (e.g. miles-in-trail restrictions) require rapid NAVBLUE recalculation that may invalidate pre-filed fuel load, triggering re-release
