Airline-Specific Risks & Pain Points

Incomplete or incorrectly completed shipper declarations are the leading cause of DG non-compliance; IATA DGR Sec 8.1.6 mandates specific formats but non-industry shippers routinely submit non-conforming documents requiring full re-inspection
IATA DGR is revised annually; local DGR lookup databases at handling agents may lag the official publication by 4–8 weeks, causing classification mismatches particularly for newly added UN numbers in Class 9
Lithium battery shipments (Class 9, UN3480/3481) account for >40% of DG non-compliance findings; IATA DGR Sec 3.9 requires state-of-charge ≤30% which is physically unverifiable without specialised equipment, creating acceptance risk
Individual airlines maintain forbidden DG lists that exceed IATA minimums; policy updates are not always propagated to the CargoWise rules engine in real time, creating a window where inadvertent acceptance of operator-prohibited DG is possible
Mixed fleet operations mean PAX aircraft quantity limits are significantly more restrictive than CAO limits for the same DG class; last-minute aircraft swaps change applicable limits and require full re-validation — Altéa DCS does not automatically trigger re-check on tail number change
IATA DGR Addendum for State Variations is updated bi-annually but some states (notably China CAAC and several Middle East CAAs) impose interim restrictions not captured in the published addendum, requiring manual dispatcher lookup against state CAA circulars
