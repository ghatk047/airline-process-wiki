StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Import approved pairings into rostering module 100% of approved pairings imported with zero data loss; import completed ≤2 hours after CM-01 sign-off Pairing handoff format mismatches between Jeppesen pairing and rostering modules cause silent truncation of augmented-crew flags — manual audit required for long-haul pairings
1.2 Configure bid period parameters and CBA rules Bid package published ≥21 days before period start per CBA; 0 rule-configuration errors at bid launch CBA rule amendments require manual reconfiguration of Jeppesen constraint tables — no automated ingestion from contract management system; misconfigurations discovered only when crew protests are filed
1.3 Validate crew qualification and fleet eligibility 0 unqualified crew included in bid package; eligibility validation completed ≥25 days before period start Qualification expiry records in Boeing Toolbox / CAMP sync to Jeppesen with a 24–48-hour lag — risk of including crew whose type-rating or medical lapses within the bid window ⚠
2.1 Publish bid package to crew self-service portal Bid package live ≥21 days before bid close; 0 incorrect or duplicate pairings in published package Late schedule changes after bid publication require reissuing the full package, compressing crew bid time below the CBA-mandated minimum — triggers grievance filings and OCC planning disruption
2.2 Capture ranked bid preferences from crew members Bid submission rate ≥95% by deadline; average ranking depth ≥15 preferences per crew member Mobile app version fragmentation causes intermittent bid submission failures for crew on older devices — preference loss occurs without error confirmation, leading to unintended default-assignment triggers
2.3 Validate bid completeness and close bidding window 100% bid validation completed within 2 hours of bid close; non-bidder list issued to planning team within 1 hour Non-bidders assigned by reserve default rules increase reserve utilisation pressure during high-absence periods (holidays, training blocks), reducing buffer for day-of-operations coverage ⚠
3.1 Run automated seniority-based bid award algorithm Automated award covers ≥85% of pairings without manual intervention; optimisation run completes <3 hours Seniority-driven award algorithm creates systematic clustering — junior crew receive chronically undesirable pairings, driving grievance filings and attrition at base cities with high seniority concentration ⚠
3.2 Apply FRMS fatigue constraints to draft roster 0 crew assigned duties with predicted fatigue index >70 (Jeppesen FRMS 0–100 scale); FRMS validation completed <2 hours FRMS scoring relies on self-reported rest periods logged in CrewPortal — inaccurate entries produce false-compliant rosters that trigger FRMS alerts only on the day of operation when it is too late to rebalance ⚠
3.3 Check FAR Part 117 duty and rest compliance 0 FAR Part 117 violations in roster at publication; compliance check completed <4 hours Augmented-crew provisions under FAR Part 117 §117.21 are not fully parameterised in Jeppesen — ultra-long-range pairings require manual override and legal review, adding 24–48 hours to the rostering cycle ⚠
3.4 Resolve uncovered pairings via reserve assignment 0 uncovered pairings at draft finalisation; reserve utilisation ≤30% of available reserve pool Reserve pool insufficiency during peak holiday bid periods forces voluntary overtime — CBA premium-pay triggers create budget variance of up to 15% above planned crew cost in Q4 ⚠
4.1 Review and adjudicate crew bid award protests 100% of bid protests resolved within 5 business days per CBA; protest rate <2% of total crew bids Seniority disputes requiring CBA interpretation escalate to labor relations, extending resolution timelines and delaying roster finalisation — unresolved protests at publication date force provisional assignments ⚠
4.2 Incorporate pre-approved trip trades and swaps Trip trade approval cycle ≤48 hours; 0 qualification violations resulting from approved trades Trip trades approved outside Jeppesen via email bypass the system's built-in qualification check — latent safety risk discovered only at crew sign-in when crew tracking flags the mismatch
5.1 Obtain final compliance sign-off and lock roster Compliance sign-off completed ≥5 days before period start; 0 open violations at sign-off Compliance audit trail stored in a separate AWS S3/Redshift data lake has no direct linkage to the Jeppesen roster record — reconstructing evidence for FAA audit queries requires manual correlation across two systems ⚠
5.2 Publish final roster and notify crew via portal 100% of crew notified within 1 hour of publication; crew acknowledgment rate ≥90% within 48 hours of release Push-notification delivery rates drop to ~70% for crew on international layovers with low cellular connectivity — crew risk missing roster start dates, forcing manual telephone outreach by crew scheduling
5.3 Distribute final roster data feed to OCC Roster data available in OCC within 30 minutes of publication; 0 data-sync errors in first 24 hours Roster-to-OCC data feed uses legacy SFTP batch transfer rather than a real-time API — the resulting 30-minute lag means OCC operates on stale crew assignments during the handoff window, creating conflict risk for back-to-back duty pairings
