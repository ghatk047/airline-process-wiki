1.1 Ingest airman certificate data from FAA IACRA Data sync completion rate ≥99.5%; sync latency <4 hours FAA IACRA API throttling during peak batch windows causes stale cert data; no real-time push — polling-only model creates up to 24-hour lag for urgent status changes ⚠
1.2 Risk-rank crew by certification expiry proximity 100% of crew with expiry <30 days flagged Red; zero missed flags Jeppesen CM risk-ranking logic does not account for Special Issuance (SI) medical conditions with shorter validity windows, causing under-flagging of affected crew
1.3 Issue automated crew renewal notification Notification delivery rate ≥99%; crew acknowledgement within 5 business days Crew members on extended international layovers or LOA may miss email alerts; no SMS or push notification fallback in current Jeppesen CM configuration
2.1 Submit MedXpress application for AME examination MedXpress submission completed ≥21 days before certificate expiry MedXpress portal outages during FAA system maintenance windows block submission; crew booking AME appointments within <14 days of expiry creates duty-coverage gaps
2.2 Conduct Class 1 AME physical examination First-attempt Class 1 pass rate ≥92%; examination cycle time <2 hours Age-40 rule requires captains over 40 to hold Class 1 medical renewed every 6 months (vs. 12 months under 40), doubling administrative burden for senior captain cohort; resource-intensive for AME network
2.3 Validate medical certificate against FAR Part 67 Validation processing time <24 hours post-examination; Special Issuance cases escalated within 4 hours FAR Part 67 Special Issuance (SI) for conditions such as hypertension or AASM-treated OSA requires FAA Regional Flight Surgeon review averaging 30–90 days, creating extended grounding risk for borderline cases ⚠
2.4 Upload validated medical certificate to crew record Record update latency <2 hours post-certificate issue; zero scheduling errors attributable to stale medical data Manual PDF upload to Jeppesen CM creates transcription error risk; no automated IACRA-to-Jeppesen integration for medical records means dual-entry is required
3.1 Query aircraft type rating currency in Jeppesen CM Query response time <30 seconds; currency data accuracy ≥99.9% vs. FAA IACRA record B737 MAX 8 return-to-service post-2020 grounding introduced new FCTM differences training requirement tracked outside standard type rating schema in Jeppesen CM, causing manual gap-check overhead
3.2 Verify FAR 61.57 recency requirements for flight ops 100% of crew checked for recency currency before schedule publication; zero recency violations detected post-assignment FAR 61.57(d) instrument currency requires 6 approaches in 6 months; crew returning from extended medical leave frequently have lapsed IFR currency not flagged until check-in, requiring last-minute schedule swaps ⚠
3.3 Schedule simulator proficiency check for lapsed recency Simulator slot secured within 10 business days of recency lapse identified; no-show rate <3% Simulator capacity at airline training centres is shared with initial qualification training; short-notice recency check bookings are frequently bumped, extending grounding duration and creating crew shortage risk on high-frequency routes
4.1 Cross-reference certificates with FAA IACRA Airmen Inquiry 100% of active crew cross-referenced monthly; discrepancy detection rate >95% vs. manual audit IACRA Airmen Inquiry public API does not expose enforcement action flags or certificate surrender status in real time; airlines must rely on PRIA (Pilot Records Improvement Act) database for hiring but have no automated ongoing pull for current employees
4.2 Verify Special Issuance conditions and SODA exemptions 100% of crew with active SI conditions reviewed quarterly; zero SI deadline breaches Special Issuance conditions (e.g., post-cardiac event, CPAP therapy compliance) carry individual FAA-imposed timelines and monitoring requirements; tracking these in a generic crew management system without structured SI workflow fields leads to missed review deadlines ⚠
4.3 Update crew qualification matrix with verified certifications Qualification matrix updated within 4 hours of verification completion; scheduling system sync lag <1 hour Qualification matrix updates in Jeppesen CM do not automatically propagate to codeshare partner crew systems, requiring manual data exchange for any interline or wet-lease arrangements
5.1 Flag non-compliant crew and initiate grounding hold Grounding hold applied within 2 hours of compliance failure detection; zero non-compliant crew departing on scheduled flights Grounding holds in Jeppesen CM do not automatically trigger crew pay adjustment workflow in SAP SuccessFactors; manual payroll correction required for grounded crew on paid leave status, creating HR disputes
5.2 Remove crew from active schedule and source replacement Replacement crew sourced within 3 hours of grounding notification; schedule disruption rate <1% attributable to certification grounding Reserve crew pools sized for weather/irregular operations events — simultaneous grounding of multiple crew for certification non-compliance (e.g., post-audit sweep) can exhaust reserve capacity on thin-coverage focus city stations
5.3 Track remediation progress and certification renewal Average grounding duration <14 days for medical renewal cases; <7 days for recency cases Jeppesen CM has no structured remediation workflow module; compliance analysts track grounding cases in spreadsheets outside the system, creating audit trail gaps and no automated escalation if remediation stalls beyond SLA ⚠
5.4 Restore crew to active status and release scheduling block Reinstatement processing time <2 hours after compliance confirmed; zero scheduling errors on reinstatement day Reinstatement requires manual dual-approval (Crew Compliance Manager + Chief Pilot) with no digital workflow; paper sign-off process creates delays when approvers are on-duty in different time zones
6.1 Generate certification compliance dashboard in Tableau Dashboard refresh latency <4 hours; fleet-wide certification compliance rate KPI target ≥99.0% Tableau dashboard relies on manual Jeppesen CM data exports rather than live API feed; stale data during audits or regulatory inspections undermines credibility with FAA Principal Operations Inspector (POI) ⚠
6.2 Export FAA regulatory audit package and file deviations Audit package prepared within 24 hours of FAA request; zero open compliance findings older than 30 days Aviation Safety Action Program (ASAP) event reporting for certification deviations is voluntary and narrative-based; without structured data capture in Jeppesen CM, root cause trending across deviation events requires labour-intensive manual analysis
