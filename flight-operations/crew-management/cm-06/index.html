Airline-Specific Risks & Pain Points

FAA IACRA API throttling during peak batch windows causes stale cert data; no real-time push — polling-only model creates up to 24-hour lag for urgent status changes
Jeppesen CM risk-ranking logic does not account for Special Issuance (SI) medical conditions with shorter validity windows, causing under-flagging of affected crew
Crew members on extended international layovers or LOA may miss email alerts; no SMS or push notification fallback in current Jeppesen CM configuration
MedXpress portal outages during FAA system maintenance windows block submission; crew booking AME appointments within <14 days of expiry creates duty-coverage gaps
Age-40 rule requires captains over 40 to hold Class 1 medical renewed every 6 months (vs. 12 months under 40), doubling administrative burden for senior captain cohort; resource-intensive for AME network
FAR Part 67 Special Issuance (SI) for conditions such as hypertension or AASM-treated OSA requires FAA Regional Flight Surgeon review averaging 30–90 days, creating extended grounding risk for borderline cases
