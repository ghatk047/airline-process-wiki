1.1 Ingest threat intelligence feeds Feed ingestion latency ≤15 min from publication; IOC coverage ≥95% of subscribed feeds Aviation ISAC feed cadence is irregular; manual triage required when SITA portal and Microsoft Sentinel connector versions diverge after platform updates
1.2 Monitor SIEM for security anomalies Mean time to detect (MTTD) ≤4 hrs for high-severity events; alert queue SLA acknowledgement ≤15 min Amadeus Altéa PSS syslog format requires custom parser; parser breaks on Altéa quarterly patch releases causing 2–6 hr blind-spot windows
1.3 Correlate IOCs and assess threat credibility False positive rate ≤12% on high-severity alerts; IOC correlation throughput ≥500 indicators/hr GDS connection endpoints (Sabre, Travelport, Amadeus GDS) generate high IOC overlap with legitimate partner traffic, inflating false-positive rates ⚠
2.1 Schedule and execute authenticated vulnerability scan Scan coverage ≥98% of in-scope assets per cycle; scan cycle ≤7 days for internet-facing assets Crew management AWS-hosted system requires coordinated scan windows with Crew Ops to avoid disrupting real-time crew tracking; missed windows extend exposure
2.2 Prioritise vulnerabilities by CVSS and asset criticality P1 (CVSS ≥9.0) remediation SLA ≤24 hrs; P2 (CVSS 7.0–8.9) ≤7 days; mean vulnerability age ≤30 days Jeppesen FliteDeck EFB tablets are classified airline OT; patches require Boeing approval and airline maintenance sign-off, extending P2 remediation to 45+ days ⚠
2.3 Execute patch deployment or emergency remediation Patch success rate ≥99% without service disruption; emergency patch deployment ≤4 hrs for P1 Amadeus Altéa PSS patching requires Amadeus vendor coordination and PSS freeze window; unilateral patching risks PSS instability affecting booking and check-in functions
3.1 Classify and triage incoming security alert Triage completion ≤15 min for P1/P2; analyst throughput ≥20 alerts/hr per Tier-1 analyst NDC API Gateway traffic is high-volume JSON; DDoS patterns targeting NDC endpoints are difficult to distinguish from legitimate agency booking bursts without airline-specific baselines
3.2 Validate alert as true or false positive True positive confirmation rate ≥88%; false positive closure with tuning update ≤2 hrs SITA ACARS messages traversing legacy ground-to-air links generate unusual encoding patterns that trigger EDR heuristics; suppression rules require SITA vendor input to avoid masking real threats ⚠
3.3 Assign incident owner and escalate to IR team P1 escalation to IR team ≤30 min from true positive confirmation; IR lead acknowledgement ≤15 min On-call rotation gaps during irregular flight operations (e.g., irregular operations recovery periods) delay P1 escalation when SOC team is simultaneously supporting NOC cyber requests
4.1 Activate incident response playbook Playbook activation ≤5 min of IR lead assignment; playbook coverage ≥95% of known threat categories Airline-specific playbooks for PSS compromise and GDS channel manipulation are not included in XSOAR out-of-box content library; custom playbooks require quarterly review to stay current with Amadeus Altéa version changes
4.2 Isolate affected endpoints and network segments Containment time ≤1 hr for P1; mean time to contain (MTTC) ≤2 hrs across all incidents AWS-hosted crew management system cannot be hard-isolated without triggering crew tracking outage; partial isolation (egress block only) leaves lateral movement risk
4.3 Assess OT or avionics system involvement OT scope assessment completion ≤30 min of containment; 100% of OT-involved incidents flagged for TSA notification within required window EFB (Jeppesen FliteDeck) tablets on aircraft are in a grey zone between IT and OT asset classification; incident scope determination requires coordination between IT Security, Flight Operations, and Boeing technical representatives ⚠
4.4 Notify TSA and CISA per cybersecurity directive SD-04C TSA notification compliance: 100% on-time within 24-hr regulatory window; zero overdue reports TSA SD-04C reporting thresholds are ambiguous for incidents affecting systems that touch both IT and airline-operated networks (e.g., airport check-in kiosks on shared infrastructure); legal review adds time
4.5 Eradicate threat and preserve digital evidence Evidence collection completeness ≥99% of affected hosts; eradication verified by CrowdStrike scan before re-entry to network Volatile memory forensics on Amadeus Altéa reservation servers requires PSS vendor coordination; uncoordinated memory acquisition risks PSS process interruption affecting live bookings
5.1 Restore systems from clean validated backup RTO ≤4 hrs for P1 critical systems (PSS, crew system); RPO ≤1 hr for tier-1 systems Amadeus Altéa PSS restoration must be sequenced with Amadeus Altéa operations team in Madrid; time zone dependency and vendor SLA of 4-hr response means RTO targets are at risk for overnight incidents ⚠
5.2 Conduct post-incident review and root cause analysis PIR completion ≤5 business days for P1; 100% of action items assigned with due date; action item closure rate ≥85% within 30 days Airline operational disruption pressure (irregular operations recovery) competes with PIR scheduling; PIR action items for systemic GDS security gaps are deprioritised against commercial recovery objectives
5.3 Update controls, playbooks, and threat detection rules Detection rule update deployment ≤48 hrs of PIR sign-off; XSOAR playbook coverage improvement measured per PIR cycle Sentinel custom rules for Amadeus Altéa and Sabre GDS log formats must be regression-tested against production log samples after every rule update; lack of a dedicated security staging environment increases change risk
