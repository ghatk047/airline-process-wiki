StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Ingest daily BSP/ARC ticket sales data feed File ingestion success rate ≥99.5%; processing time <2 hours after BSP file drop BSP file format differences across IATA billing regions (e.g. BSP-Americas vs BSP-Europe) cause parsing errors for non-standard fare constructions; legacy ASCII flat-file format has no structured error reporting
1.2 Match ticket coupons against PSS booking records Automated coupon match rate ≥97%; unmatched coupons backlog <3% of daily volume PNR purge cycle (typically 90 days post-departure) removes booking records before interline billing arrives, causing match failures for delayed interline invoices; no automated re-match queue ⚠
1.3 Apply ATPCO fare basis code validation Fare validation error rate <1% of total coupons processed; validation completed within 4 hours of ingestion ATPCO fare filing publication lag (up to 48 hours after filing) means recently-sold fares may not yet appear in validation tables, generating false-positive errors on new fare products launched during peak sale windows
1.4 Resolve coupon validation exceptions Exception resolution within 5 business days; exception backlog <500 coupons at any time Manual exception handling volume spikes after IROP events when re-issued tickets carry non-standard fare constructions and waiver codes not recognised by the revenue accounting system; resolution requires coordination with airport and contact centre teams ⚠
2.1 Calculate interline revenue proration Proration processing completed within T+3 calendar days; interline dispute rate <2% of prorated billings IATA Resolution 850e proration formula conflicts with bilaterally-negotiated prorate agreements for specific carrier pairs; manual overrides are required and not fully auditable within REVERA, creating reconciliation risk
2.2 Apply ASC 606 revenue recognition rules Deferred revenue posting accuracy ≥99.9% of ticket value; period-end deferred revenue balance reconciled within T+5 business days Ancillary revenue bundling (e.g. seat upgrade + checked bag + base fare sold as one fare family) requires performance obligation disaggregation under ASC 606 Topic 606; the revenue accounting subledger does not support multi-element arrangement splitting natively, requiring manual journal overrides ⚠
2.3 Process EMD ancillary and ancillary revenue EMD-to-revenue match rate ≥98%; ancillary revenue posted within T+2 days of service delivery date EMD-A (associated, tied to flight coupon) vs EMD-S (standalone, sold independently) misclassification causes ancillary revenue to be booked to incorrect income lines; the distinction is not enforced at point of sale by all booking channels
3.1 Reconcile BSP billing memo vs internal records Net billing variance <0.1% of total settlement amount; reconciliation completed within T+1 business day of BSP memo receipt Currency conversion timing differences between the BSP billing date and the internal booking date create systematic variances in markets with volatile FX rates (e.g. ARS, TRY, EGP); IATA BSP Link applies the exchange rate at billing date, not booking date ⚠
3.2 Raise Agency Debit Memos for billing discrepancies ADM filed within IATA 15-day filing deadline; ADM acceptance rate by agencies ≥85% within 30 days IATA BSP Link ADM filing requires exact citation of the IATA resolution code authorising the ADM; incorrect or missing resolution citations cause automatic rejection, restarting the 15-day clock and risking write-off if the deadline cannot be met on re-submission
3.3 Approve and submit BSP settlement payment Payment submitted on BSP settlement value date with zero late-payment penalties; settlement accuracy 100% of approved net amount Dual-approval workflow between Revenue Accounting and Treasury creates a bottleneck when approvers are unavailable on the BSP settlement date; SAP S/4HANA workflow does not support time-sensitive escalation routing to backup approvers ⚠
3.4 Confirm BSP settlement receipt and archive Settlement confirmation archived within 24 hours of IATA acknowledgement; audit trail completeness 100% IATA BSP Link does not provide real-time payment confirmation; reconciliation relies on bank MT940 SWIFT message feeds which may lag by 1 business day, leaving the settlement status in IATA BSP Link as 'pending' and triggering false escalation alerts
4.1 Post revenue journal entries to SAP General Ledger Automated posting rate ≥95% of journal lines; manual journals <5%; posting completed within T+2 business days of period end Revenue accounting system to SAP interface uses scheduled batch file transfer; if a batch fails mid-run, partial postings create out-of-balance GL entries requiring manual reversal journals and repost, delaying period close
4.2 Perform revenue GL account reconciliation All revenue GL accounts reconciled within T+5 business days of month end; unexplained open items <$50,000 aggregate High volume of open items from ongoing interline billing disputes strains the reconciliation process; open item ageing is not automatically surfaced in BlackLine, requiring manual export and pivot analysis, often pushing sign-off past the T+5 target ⚠
4.3 Generate revenue reports and close accounting period Revenue flash report delivered to CFO within T+3 business days of period end; period close completed by T+7 business days Parallel month-end close activities (cost accounting, payroll, procurement AP) contend for SAP period-close resources; lock conflicts on shared profit centre and cost centre objects delay period close when multiple finance teams post simultaneously
