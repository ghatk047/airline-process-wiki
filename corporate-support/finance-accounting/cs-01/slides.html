StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Initiate annual planning cycle in SAP S/4HANA Planning cycle launched ≥12 weeks before fiscal year-end Misalignment between network planning schedule (Amadeus SkyWORKS) and finance calendar delays capacity cost assumptions by 2–3 weeks
1.2 Extract prior-year actuals from SAP FI/CO Actuals extraction completed within 3 business days of year-end close Manual reclassification of intercompany recharges between airline OpCo and MRO subsidiary inflates cost centre variances by up to 8%
1.3 Pull macro & fuel price assumptions Fuel price assumption reviewed against IATA Jet Fuel Monitor within ±5% of market consensus Jet fuel price volatility (±40% year-on-year) makes fixed planning assumptions obsolete within Q1; no direct feed from Bloomberg into SAP BPC requiring manual re-entry
1.4 CFO review and approval of planning assumptions Assumptions approved and published within 5 business days of Treasury submission Late delivery of network capacity plan from Amadeus SkyMAX optimisation run blocks CFO sign-off; fleet assignment iterations can extend 2+ weeks past planning calendar ⚠
2.1 Distribute budget templates via SAP BPC Templates distributed within 2 business days of assumption approval SAP BPC template version conflicts when cost centre hierarchy changes mid-cycle (e.g., post-restructuring) cause data submission errors requiring IT intervention
2.2 Departments enter cost centre budgets in SAP BPC On-time submission rate ≥90% of cost centres by stated deadline Operational departments (Ground Ops, Crew Planning) lack financial modelling skills; submissions frequently contain formula errors, requiring FP&A rework averaging 3 days per cycle
2.3 Validate completeness of departmental submissions 100% submission completeness achieved before consolidation window opens No automated reminder workflow in SAP BPC; FP&A team manually tracks submissions via spreadsheet, introducing version control risk ⚠
2.4 Validate headcount budget vs SAP SuccessFactors plan Headcount variance between BPC submission and SuccessFactors plan ≤2% at department level SAP SuccessFactors and SAP BPC do not share a live integration; headcount data exported via flat-file weekly, causing stale data when departments submit after the extract cut-off
2.5 Validate capex requests against fleet and route plan Capex budget total reconciled to strategic fleet plan within ±2% variance Boeing 737 MAX delivery slippages (historically 6–18 months) invalidate fleet-linked capex budgets; no automated feed from Boeing delivery portal into SAP CO requiring manual re-phasing
3.1 Consolidate full P&L, cash flow, and balance sheet Consolidation completed within 3 business days of submission deadline Intercompany eliminations between airline, MRO entity, and loyalty programme require manual journal entries in SAP BPC; risk of double-counting revenue estimated at 1–2% of group revenue
3.2 Assess consolidated budget vs EBITDAR target EBITDAR margin target ≥15% on consolidated airline revenue Fuel and labour costs (>65% of OPEX) are largely non-discretionary; gap closure options are limited and CFO pressure to cut maintenance capex risks AOG rate increases in Y+1 ⚠
3.3 Issue cut targets and iterate with cost centres Resubmission cycle completed within 5 business days of cut target issuance Repeated iteration loops (average 2.3 rounds per cycle) extend the planning calendar into operational freeze windows, increasing risk of budget being published after the fiscal year has started
3.4 Reconcile fuel hedge positions with treasury model Hedge coverage ratio target ≥50% of planned fuel consumption for next 12 months Fuel hedging positions managed in Bloomberg are not natively visible in SAP BPC; hedge P&L impact must be manually bridged, introducing a reporting lag of up to 5 business days
4.1 Prepare executive budget pack for Board presentation Board pack delivered ≥5 business days before Finance Committee meeting Manual export of SAP BPC data into Tableau introduces version risk; FP&A teams maintain parallel Excel models that diverge from SAP BPC source of truth, undermining data integrity
4.2 Present budget to Board Finance Committee Board approval obtained at first presentation in ≥75% of annual cycles Board members may request scenario re-runs (e.g., different fuel assumptions) that SAP BPC cannot perform live in the board room; delays approval by 1–2 weeks ⚠
4.3 Lock approved budget baseline in SAP BPC and SAP CO Budget locked and released to SAP CO within 3 business days of Board approval SAP BPC version locking does not prevent ad-hoc manual adjustments via SAP CO cost centre planning; shadow budgets maintained by operations managers erode single-source-of-truth discipline
5.1 Generate monthly actuals vs budget variance report Management accounts published within 5 business days of month-end close Month-end close extended by late Accounts Payable accruals from MRO vendors (average 3–4 day delay post month-end); delays variance reporting and triggers reforecast discussions without complete data
5.2 Assess variance against materiality threshold Reforecast trigger decision made within 2 business days of management accounts publication Absence of a formal materiality policy means reforecast decisions are made ad-hoc; in fuel price spike scenarios (e.g., geopolitical events), Finance may be 6–8 weeks behind market reality ⚠
5.3 Execute rolling reforecast and update SAP BPC Rolling reforecast cycle completed within 10 business days of trigger; forecast accuracy ≤3% vs subsequent actuals Revenue reforecast requires Amadeus NRM booking-curve data that is not available in SAP BPC; FP&A manually requests extracts from Revenue Management team, adding 2–3 day dependency
