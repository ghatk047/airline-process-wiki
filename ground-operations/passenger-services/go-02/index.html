Airline-Specific Risks & Pain Points

Altéa allowance rules do not sync in real-time with codeshare partner PSS, forcing manual overrides and creating revenue leakage on interline itineraries
Scale calibration drift at high-volume counters causes inconsistent weight readings; CUTE has no automated recalibration alert, so discrepancies are caught only during periodic QA audits
Agents waiving fees at discretion without supervisory approval triggers revenue leakage estimated at $30–50 per waived bag; Altéa has no mandatory approval workflow for fee waivers at Level 1 agent access
BSM transmission lag >30 sec to downstream stations causes missed sortation windows on tight connections; IATA Resolution 753 mandates scan tracking from this point but most carriers lack RFID at check-in, relying on barcode-only
Legacy inline EDS at secondary airports cannot handle post-pandemic capacity surge; single-lane configurations create 20–30 min queue backlogs during peak departures, directly causing departure delays
CT systems generate 10–15% false-positive rates for dense consumer items (lithium batteries, laptops), driving manual inspection queues that delay affected bags by 8–15 min and disrupt sortation sequence
