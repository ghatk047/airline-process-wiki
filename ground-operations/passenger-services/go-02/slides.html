StepL4 Step Name KPI / TargetPain Point / RiskGate
1.1 Verify passenger allowance in PSS Allowance lookup time ≤10 sec; fee notification accuracy ≥99% Altéa allowance rules do not sync in real-time with codeshare partner PSS, forcing manual overrides and creating revenue leakage on interline itineraries
1.2 Weigh and measure baggage at counter Average bag acceptance time ≤90 sec per bag; scale accuracy ±0.1 kg Scale calibration drift at high-volume counters causes inconsistent weight readings; CUTE has no automated recalibration alert, so discrepancies are caught only during periodic QA audits ⚠
1.3 Collect excess baggage fees Excess fee collection rate ≥98% of chargeable bags Agents waiving fees at discretion without supervisory approval triggers revenue leakage estimated at $30–50 per waived bag; Altéa has no mandatory approval workflow for fee waivers at Level 1 agent access
1.4 Print bag tag and transmit BSM BSM transmission accuracy ≥99.5%; BSM delivery to BHS ≤5 sec BSM transmission lag >30 sec to downstream stations causes missed sortation windows on tight connections; IATA Resolution 753 mandates scan tracking from this point but most carriers lack RFID at check-in, relying on barcode-only
2.1 Induct bag into automated EDS screening Screening throughput ≥1,200 bags/hr per lane; induction reject rate ≤0.5% Legacy inline EDS at secondary airports cannot handle post-pandemic capacity surge; single-lane configurations create 20–30 min queue backlogs during peak departures, directly causing departure delays
2.2 Evaluate automated threat detection result Automated clear rate ≥85%; false-positive rate ≤12% CT systems generate 10–15% false-positive rates for dense consumer items (lithium batteries, laptops), driving manual inspection queues that delay affected bags by 8–15 min and disrupt sortation sequence ⚠
2.3 Conduct manual bag search and resolve status Manual resolution time ≤15 min per bag; offload-to-notification time ≤5 min No automated link between TSA PARIS resolution status and airline BHS sortation system; cleared bags require a manual phone call to ground ops to re-inject, introducing misrouting risk and operator dependency during irregular operations
3.1 Sort bag to destination make-up area via BHS Sortation accuracy ≥99.7%; late bags to make-up ≤1% of total BHS sorter reading errors on damaged, bent, or folded barcoded bag tags cause misroutes; RFID fallback absent at most secondary airports despite IATA Resolution 753 requirement for 4-scan tracking — misrouted bags have no automated recovery path ⚠
3.2 Build ULD or loose-load baggage cart ULD build completion ≥30 min before scheduled departure; weight estimate variance ≤2% Manual ULD build-up has no real-time weight tracking — ramp agents estimate bag weight by handling; cumulative error causes load rebalancing at aircraft hold, adding avg 4–8 min to departure preparation
3.3 Reconcile bag count against active PAX list Pre-load reconciliation accuracy ≥99.5%; discrepancy resolution time ≤10 min Disconnected systems between check-in (Altéa DCS) and ramp (BagManager) mean reconciliation relies on manual counts at make-up; count gaps widen at off-hub outstations with limited IT infrastructure and no dedicated ramp IT support ⚠
4.1 Transport ULDs and carts to aircraft Bags at aircraft ≥25 min before scheduled departure; tug utilisation ≥80% Late gate changes (≤15 min before departure) force ULD re-routing on congested apron without automated rerouting guidance; ramp agents coordinate via radio only, causing ULD queuing conflicts and delay attribution to ground ops
4.2 Load baggage into aircraft hold per W&B plan Hold loading complete ≥15 min before departure (D-15); W&B CG within ±1% of plan Real-time W&B updates require accurate bag weights from check-in; estimated weights (used when CUTE scales drift) increase fuel burn by 0.1–0.3% per flight and may require unplanned last-minute cargo offload to maintain CG envelope
4.3 Reconcile loaded bags and offload no-shows 100% reconciliation before door closure; unaccompanied bag offload ≤10 min from identification ICAO Annex 17 requires offloading bags of passengers who check in but do not board; manual hold search for no-show bags averages 8–12 min delay per occurrence and is non-negotiable — no technology currently automates the physical search ⚠
5.1 Offload baggage from aircraft on arrival First bag on belt ≤20 min from block-in (domestic); ≤25 min (international) Short-turn flights (≤45 min ground time) require simultaneous offload and inbound load operations; ramp agents coordinate only via radio with no automated sequencing tool, increasing error rate and first-bag time during high-rotation operations
5.2 Deliver bags to assigned baggage claim belt Last bag on belt ≤35 min from block-in; on-time belt delivery rate ≥92% Belt assignment conflicts during irregular operations (multiple delayed arrivals simultaneously) cause commingling of bags from different flights; commingling increases WorldTracer PIR filings by ~30% during IROPS windows and triggers EC 261/2004 and DOT compensation exposure ⚠
5.3 Identify and file PIR for mishandled bags PIR filing time ≤10 min from passenger report; mishandling rate ≤5 per 1,000 passengers (IATA industry target) Missing intermediate scans (induction, sortation, or loading) leave no audit trail for liability disputes under EC 261/2004 and DOT baggage rules; WorldTracer matching fails without a complete scan history, leaving agents unable to locate bag origin or last-seen point
5.4 Trace, recover and return mishandled baggage 80% of mishandled bags returned within 24 hrs; permanently lost bag rate ≤0.5 per 1,000 passengers WorldTracer matching depends on accurate IATA barcode scans; damaged tags with no RFID fallback leave bags stranded in 'no-read' queues at transfer hubs with no automated escalation workflow — cases age beyond 5-day threshold, triggering DOT delayed baggage compensation requirements ⚠
