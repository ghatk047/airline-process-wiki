1.1 Capture Property Irregularity Report at arrival hall PIR captured within 20 min of carousel clearance ≥98% High passenger volumes at hub airports cause PIR queue backlogs; agents default to paper forms that delay WorldTracer entry by 2–4 hrs
1.2 Verify PNR and bag tag details in Altéa PNR match rate ≥99.5%; missing bag tag resolved via passenger description within 15 min Passengers without digital boarding passes cannot self-serve via kiosk; paper bag tags occasionally unreadable after sortation damage
1.3 Search local carousel, offload area, and left baggage Local recovery rate ≥35% within 30 min of PIR opening BagManager scan coverage gaps at outstations mean bags offloaded to wrong belt go undetected until next carousel audit ⚠
1.4 Assess local scan log — bag present at station? Same-day resolution for locally present bags ≥95% BRS scan logs not always synchronised with WorldTracer in real time; 5–10 min latency causes duplicate PIRs ⚠
2.1 Create AHL/OHD/DPR tracing message in WorldTracer AHL file created within 2 hrs of PIR ≥99%; OHD response from partner stations ≤4 hrs Interline itineraries with non-Altéa carriers require manual telex; SITA messaging delays to some outstations exceed 30 min
2.2 Query BRS scan history for last known bag location Last-scan data available within 5 min for ≥95% of bags with electronic tags RFID read rates vary by airport — legacy barcode-only stations provide no scan after check-in, forcing inference from load messages
2.3 Evaluate WorldTracer match within 24-hour window First match rate within 24 hrs ≥72% (IATA Resolution 743 target); escalation triggered <28% WorldTracer automatic matching fails when bag description is vague or bag tag damaged; human review adds 2–6 hr lag at high-volume stations ⚠
3.1 Send FWD message to station holding located bag FWD message sent within 30 min of match confirmation ≥98% Ground handler contracts at some outstations do not include expedite forwarding; next available flight may be 24–48 hrs away
3.2 Confirm bag shipped and track via BagJourney Forward shipment confirmed (scan at origin) within 4 hrs of FWD message ≥90% Rush tags issued at outstations not always scanned into BagJourney, causing blind spots until arrival at destination ⚠
3.3 Receive forwarded bag at destination and verify condition Bag condition documented at receipt ≥99%; damaged bags flagged for claims within 1 hr Damaged-in-transit bags generate secondary claims that delay closure; handler liability ambiguity between origin and transit stations
4.1 Contact passenger to confirm delivery preference Passenger contacted within 1 hr of RFL status ≥95%; delivery preference captured ≥98% Passenger contact data in PNR frequently outdated for connecting itineraries; GDPR constraints limit use of third-party contact enrichment
4.2 Schedule courier delivery via third-party delivery API Courier dispatched within 2 hrs of passenger confirmation ≥92%; delivery within 24 hrs of dispatch ≥85% Courier API integrations vary by country — some stations revert to manual phone booking, introducing 3–5 hr delays and no tracking visibility ⚠
4.3 Confirm delivery and obtain proof of receipt POD captured and attached to WorldTracer within 24 hrs of delivery ≥99%; WorldTracer file closed within 48 hrs of delivery Paper POD at outstations not digitised, leaving WorldTracer files open and inflating delayed-bag statistics for IATA reporting
5.1 Assess interim expense claim under Montreal Convention Interim claim assessed within 48 hrs of PIR ≥90%; average claim processing time ≤5 business days SDR-to-local-currency conversion rate must be applied at date of loss, not payment date — manual FX lookup creates errors in SAP FI journal entries ⚠
5.2 Process approved compensation payment via SAP Finance Payment executed within 7 business days of approval ≥95%; zero unapproved payments exceeding liability cap Payments to international passengers require SWIFT transfers through SAP AP — SWIFT processing adds 3–5 business days in some corridors
6.1 Transfer unmatched bag to lost baggage storage at day 5 100% of unmatched bags transferred to STO status by day 5; storage catalogue photo quality ≥95% usable Storage space at busy hubs is finite — airlines pay third-party warehouse fees after 10 days; uncatalogued bags cannot be matched to late-reporting passengers
6.2 Conduct extended 21-day owner identification search Overall bag reunification rate within 21 days ≥97% (IATA Baggage Report benchmark); unclaimed rate <3% Passengers who do not file a PIR at the airport cannot initiate a WorldTracer search online without the original PIR reference — prevents late claims ⚠
6.3 Dispose of unclaimed bag per property regulation 100% of disposed bags documented with disposal method and date; zero regulatory non-compliance events Unclaimed property laws vary by country — US DOT, EU Regulation 889/2002, and individual state statutes create inconsistent disposal timelines and donation requirements
