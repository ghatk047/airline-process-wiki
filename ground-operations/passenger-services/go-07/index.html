Airline-Specific Risks & Pain Points

Late pax additions and standby activations in Altéa can shift total bag weights by 200–400 kg after preliminary load plan is locked, requiring full recalculation
Standard passenger weight assumptions (IATA Standard Weights 2023) may under-estimate actual payload on leisure routes with heavy baggage allowances, skewing preliminary CG
Lithium battery shipments (IATA DGR Section II PI 966/967/968) increasingly misclassified by shippers; SITA DGM lacks real-time integration with courier manifest data, requiring manual cross-check
Paper or tablet-based LIR distribution to ramp agents creates version-control risk; last-minute config changes (late cargo acceptance) may not reach all loading teams before loading begins
Interline transfer bags arriving via multiple carriers carry different barcode standards (BCBP vs legacy 10-digit); SITA BagManager reconciliation failures require manual intervention, risking late bags
Wide-body aircraft use mixed ULD types (LD3, LD7, bulk) — incorrect ULD type selection delays loading due to incompatible cargo door dimensions; SITA LoadManager ULD type validation is advisory only
