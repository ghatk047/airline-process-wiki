1.1 Retrieve Final Pax Count & Baggage Weights Data retrieval completed ≤5 min after check-in close Late pax additions and standby activations in Altéa can shift total bag weights by 200–400 kg after preliminary load plan is locked, requiring full recalculation
1.2 Calculate Preliminary Weight & Balance Preliminary W&B issued ≤10 min after check-in close Standard passenger weight assumptions (IATA Standard Weights 2023) may under-estimate actual payload on leisure routes with heavy baggage allowances, skewing preliminary CG
1.3 Validate Dangerous Goods Declarations DG validation completed ≤15 min before cargo loading commences; zero IATA DGR Part 7 segregation violations Lithium battery shipments (IATA DGR Section II PI 966/967/968) increasingly misclassified by shippers; SITA DGM lacks real-time integration with courier manifest data, requiring manual cross-check ⚠
1.4 Generate Loading Instruction Report (LIR) LIR issued to ramp supervisor ≤20 min before scheduled loading start Paper or tablet-based LIR distribution to ramp agents creates version-control risk; last-minute config changes (late cargo acceptance) may not reach all loading teams before loading begins
2.1 Sort & Route Checked Baggage to Make-Up Area Baggage sortation accuracy ≥99.5%; all bags at make-up area ≥30 min before STD Interline transfer bags arriving via multiple carriers carry different barcode standards (BCBP vs legacy 10-digit); SITA BagManager reconciliation failures require manual intervention, risking late bags
2.2 Build ULD / Container per Load Plan Spec ULD build completed per LIR sequence; zero wrong-position items at loading start Wide-body aircraft use mixed ULD types (LD3, LD7, bulk) — incorrect ULD type selection delays loading due to incompatible cargo door dimensions; SITA LoadManager ULD type validation is advisory only
2.3 Weigh Completed ULD / Container 100% of ULDs weighed before loading; weight variance vs estimate ≤±50 kg per ULD Ramp-scale calibration drift (EASA Part-145 requirement: calibration every 12 months) causes systematic weight bias accumulating across all holds; discrepancies only surfaced at final W&B stage ⚠
2.4 Confirm ULD Weight Within Structural Limit Zero structural limit exceedances reaching the aircraft; resolution time ≤8 min per overweight ULD Aircraft type changes (e.g. A320 substituted for A321) alter bay structural limits mid-process; SITA LoadManager aircraft config library updates lag fleet changes by 24–48 hours ⚠
3.1 Confirm Ground Equipment Positioning at Aircraft GSE at aircraft ≤5 min after aircraft-on-blocks; zero loading delays attributable to GSE unavailability GSE pooling across multiple handlers at shared terminals creates resource contention; SITA AMS GSE tracking relies on manual check-in, not real-time GPS — actual vs reported positions diverge during peak banks
3.2 Load Cargo Holds per LIR Bay Assignment Loading compliance with LIR ≥99%; fwd/aft hold loaded within 20 min of start for narrowbody Handheld SITA LoadManager scan confirmation step is frequently skipped during high-tempo turns; missing scan events break baggage reconciliation and create BSM (Baggage Source Message) failures reported to IATA BRS
3.3 Load Special & Priority Items per NOTOC 100% NOTOC items loaded in approved positions; zero segregation violations on departure Live animal loading under IATA LAR requires temperature-zone compliance — cargo hold temperature monitoring (ACARS datalink) is not available on all aircraft types, creating regulatory exposure on LAR shipments
3.4 Verify Loading Sequence Matches LIR Loading conformance check completed before any hold door closed; discrepancy resolution ≤10 min Forward CG bias risk: ramp teams load forward holds first for efficiency; deviation from LIR sequence without re-run of W&B can produce an out-of-envelope CG that is only caught at step 4.3 ⚠
4.1 Record Actual Loaded Weights per Hold All hold weights entered within 5 min of loading completion Bulk-loaded items (oversized bags, strollers, wheelchairs) weighed individually at check-in but grouped in cargo manifests without piece-level tracking — cumulative weight error can exceed 100 kg on narrow-body aircraft
4.2 Compute Final ZFW, TOW & CG Position Final W&B calculation completed ≤15 min before STD; TOW within ±0.5% of fuel plan Boeing OPT structural limits database must match the exact aircraft tail number's modification status (SB-tracked changes alter limits); mismatches between OPT aircraft config and actual tail configuration require manual override with engineering sign-off
4.3 Confirm CG Within Certified Envelope CG in-envelope on first calculation ≥95% of flights; rebalance time ≤15 min when required Point-of-no-return conflict: CG rebalance after door closure requires re-opening hold, adding 10–20 min to turnaround; pressure from OCC to depart on-time leads to borderline CG positions being accepted with minimal margin ⚠
4.4 Generate & Sign Final Loadsheet Final loadsheet generated ≥15 min before STD; e-loadsheet adoption rate target ≥80% Regulatory requirement for wet-ink captain signature on paper loadsheet persists at many airports due to incomplete e-loadsheet adoption under IATA Simplifying the Business (StB) programme; creates courier dependency and delays on remote stands
5.1 Transmit Final Loadsheet to Flight Deck via ACARS ACARS loadsheet delivery confirmed ≤12 min before STD; delivery failure rate <1% ACARS datalink congestion during peak departure banks (multiple aircraft departing simultaneously) causes transmission delays of 3–8 min; crews on ACARS-equipped EFBs (Jeppesen FliteDeck) may not receive message before paperwork cut-off
5.2 Obtain Crew Acknowledgment of Loadsheet Crew acknowledgment received ≥10 min before STD; query resolution ≤5 min Crew queries about non-standard weights (e.g. cargo-only belly loads, heavy charters) require Load Controller to re-explain figures verbally — no structured digital query-and-response workflow exists in SITA LoadManager to track queries and responses for audit trail ⚠
5.3 Issue Last-Minute Change (LMC) if Required LMC issued and acknowledged ≤7 min before pushback; LMC rate <5% of departures Security-driven offloads (unaccompanied baggage removal per IATA Resolution 753 BRS) can occur as late as T-5 min; cascading CG shift from forward-hold offload frequently triggers full W&B recalculation, compressing final clearance window to under 3 min
5.4 Close Cargo Doors & Issue Station Clearance Hold doors closed and ramp-clear signal issued ≥5 min before STD; on-time departure (D0) contribution ≥85% Concurrent ground activities (refuelling, catering, water service) on the same aircraft require physical clearance coordination not tracked in SITA AMS — ramp supervisors rely on verbal radio confirmation, creating safety blind spots during rushed turns
