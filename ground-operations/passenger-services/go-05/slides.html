1.1 Receive inbound flight movement data Stand assignment confirmed ≥30 min before ETA Late gate changes due to ATC slot revisions or prior turn overrun cause cascading resource conflicts — AMS does not auto-trigger GSE reallocation
1.2 Allocate ramp resources to inbound flight Resource plan published ≥20 min before on-block; GSE utilisation ≥75% GSE availability gaps during peak banks — GroundStar planning horizon is 4 hours; sub-contracted handling agents may not sync real-time equipment status
1.3 Confirm stand readiness and brief ramp team Stand cleared ≥10 min before on-block for 95% of turns Stand conflicts when previous turn is delayed — SITA AMS does not automatically notify ramp crew of stand swap; verbal communication gaps cause missed briefings ⚠
2.1 Marshal aircraft to allocated stand On-block within 2 min of scheduled on-block time for 80% of turns Tight apron geometry at congested airports leads to proximity incidents — SITA AMS does not provide real-time apron conflict alerting; relies on manual visual clearance
2.2 Position chocks, connect GPU and air bridge GPU connected within 3 min of on-block; APU off within 5 min of on-block APU-off compliance is monitored manually — airlines incur fuel burn penalty and noise fines if APU is left running; GroundStar task completion is self-reported, not sensor-verified
2.3 Conduct FOD check and open aircraft holds FOD check completed within 5 min of chocking; zero FOD incidents per 10,000 turns FOD checks are paper-based at many stations — GroundStar task completion does not integrate with airside safety management system, preventing automated audit trail ⚠
2.4 Offload passenger baggage and ULDs First bag on belt ≤20 min after on-block; last bag ≤35 min for narrow-body ULD mismatch between manifest and physical load causes hold-up — SITA LoadPlanner relies on accurate originating station scanning; missing scan events create discrepancy exceptions requiring manual reconciliation
2.5 Offload cargo and mail per cargo manifest Cargo offload complete within 30 min of on-block; 100% DG loads documented per IATA DGR Dangerous goods (DG) misidentification at originating station discovered at offload — IATA DGR requires segregation but LoadPlanner DG flagging depends on shipper declaration accuracy
3.1 Perform lavatory and potable water servicing Lavatory service complete within 15 min of task start; zero potable water contamination events Lavatory service vehicles (LSVs) are a single point of failure at smaller stations — delay cascades to all turns; IATA AHM 810 minimum water quality checks are manual and not digitally recorded in GroundStar
3.2 Calculate and uplift fuel per dispatch release Fueling complete ≥15 min before scheduled departure; fuel quantity variance ≤50 kg vs. dispatch order Fuel density variation by temperature affects volumetric uplift — Into-Plane systems calculate in volume (litres/US gal); conversion errors between fuel slip and ACARS weight report create W&B discrepancies if not reconciled ⚠
3.3 Complete aircraft interior cleaning Cleaning complete within allocated turn time window; cabin cleanliness score ≥4.2/5 in post-flight survey Cleaning time is the most commonly cut activity during short turns — GroundStar task sequencing does not enforce minimum cleaning duration, and supervisors may sign off early under departure pressure
4.1 Load catering and provisioning per galley plan Catering loading complete ≥20 min before STD; zero missing trolley events per 1,000 turns Catering uplift errors (wrong galley, wrong count) discovered after door closure — gate-return for catering is operationally disruptive; caterers use paper manifests not integrated with Inform GroundStar task completion ⚠
4.2 Verify catering counts against flight specification Catering verification complete within 5 min of loading; discrepancy rate <0.5% of turns Passenger load changes after catering order cutoff (typically H-4) create over/under-provisioning — Amadeus Altéa PSS does not push live pax count updates to catering management system automatically
5.1 Receive final pax load and generate load plan Load plan generated ≤10 min after check-in closure; load factor accuracy ±1% of actual Late passenger changes (gate adds, no-shows) after load plan generation require manual LIR revision — SITA LoadPlanner does not auto-recalculate when Altéa PSS pax count changes post-closure ⚠
5.2 Load passenger baggage per approved load plan Baggage loaded per plan with ≤2% position deviation; loading complete ≥15 min before STD Ramp agents substitute ULD positions without updating LoadPlanner — actual vs. planned hold weights diverge, invalidating the W&B calculation and requiring a last-minute loadsheet reissue
5.3 Load outbound cargo and mail per manifest Cargo loading complete ≥20 min before STD; 100% DG segregation compliance per IATA DGR Time-sensitive cargo (pharma, perishables) may miss cut-off due to late trucking delivery — SITA LoadPlanner does not integrate with off-airport cargo acceptance systems to provide early cut-off warning
6.1 Calculate final weight and balance Loadsheet issued ≥10 min before STD; CG within certified envelope 100% of departures CG envelope violations caused by last-minute pax or cargo substitutions — SITA LoadPlanner CG alert requires manual Load Controller acknowledgement; no hard stop in system prevents issuance of out-of-envelope loadsheet ⚠
6.2 Transmit loadsheet to flight crew via ACARS ACARS loadsheet transmission ≥8 min before STD; crew acknowledgement within 2 min ACARS transmission failures at remote stations require fax or manual delivery — regulatory requirement (FAR Part 121 / EASA Ops) mandates crew receipt before departure; delays loadsheet delivery and pushes off-block
6.3 Disconnect all GSE and confirm all-clear All GSE cleared ≥3 min before off-block; zero FOD events during pushback phase GPU disconnect sequencing must precede APU start — uncoordinated disconnect in noisy ramp environment leads to momentary power loss events; GroundStar all-clear task is self-reported with no sensor confirmation ⚠
6.4 Coordinate pushback and engine start with ATC Off-block within 1 min of target off-block time (TOBT) for 78% of departures TOBT accuracy degrades during irregular operations — A-CDM TOBT updates from SITA AMS rely on all ground service completions being accurately logged; manual GroundStar updates lag real-time status, degrading ATC slot compliance
