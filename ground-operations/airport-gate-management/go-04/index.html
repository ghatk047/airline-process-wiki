Airline-Specific Risks & Pain Points

Late schedule amendments from airline ops (within T-6h) invalidate pre-built plans; no automated re-trigger of optimisation on change
Wide-body/narrow-body gate incompatibility not always captured in AMS master data — results in invalid auto-assignments requiring manual correction
Ad-hoc aircraft swaps driven by AOG maintenance events are not always reflected in Amadeus Altéa PSS in time for gate validation cycle
Cleaning and catering subcontractor SLA gaps at high-frequency turn stations cause gate hold-backs; A-CDM milestones not enforced contractually at all airports
SITA FIDS update latency (up to 90s per change batch) causes passenger confusion during rapid same-day gate changes at high-traffic terminals
ACARS signal dropout over oceanic routes causes ETA gaps; fallback to secondary radar feed introduces 15-min lag and degrades conflict detection accuracy
